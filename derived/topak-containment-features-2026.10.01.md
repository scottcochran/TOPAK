I'll pull the records that fix the archetype's confinement terms before listing requirements, so each one traces to a clause.

The archetype fixes the target: rung 3 under ADR-0034, a Build profile with all seven clauses passing, an in-house Persona on two independent lineages, and the ADR-0035 criterion in force. "Inside the verified object" has a precise meaning under ADR-0045 §5: a component is inside when a property is stated over it and a mutation of it fails a check, and it is a trusted-base row when a property takes it as a premise. So the requirements split into three groups: what must be checked, what the mechanism must do, and what the mechanism must be handed.

## Where the boundary of the verified object falls

1. **Inside: the appraisal-at-grant path and everything the lease carries.** The launch request, nonce derivation, verdict handling, session binding, no-silent-downgrade at renewal, Contested revocation, profile promotion and withdrawal, and the `Contained` predicate. Each must have a stated property and a mutation that breaks it. ADR-0034 §7 already has eight mutations; the successor module keeps all of them.
2. **Outside, as trust-root rows, by construction.** The CPU vendor's root of trust and firmware, the independent verifier, the promoted image with its Wasm runtime and profile, accelerator attestation, and the tool host are rows T-0045-16, 17, 20, 21 and 25. No containment theorem may absorb one of them silently. Each row is cited by the theorem that rests on it, with its residual on the face.
3. **A successor to `Confinement.tla`.** It corrects finding 6 so that `Evidence` reads no launch measurement at rung 0, carries the rung-0 row, and holds `Safe` at every rung with `M_HOST` breaking rungs 1 and 2 and changing nothing at rung 3. The non-vacuity witness `NeverContainedEffect` must be violated at rung 3 and hold at rung 0.
4. **The refinement row T-0045-28.** Until a chain from the checked module to `topak-node` exists, the containment mechanism is inside the verified specification and not the verified code. ADR-0045 §5 names that a debt owned by ADR-0033, not a residual the archetype keeps. Stage one is the trace harness: recorded launch, appraise, grant and revoke traces matched against the successor module.
5. **The theorem-premise binding updated.** `ContainedMeansConfined`, `ContestedStopped` and `FreshAppraisal` get their rung-3 row premises filled, and the inventory for an archetype deployment is computed from the table rather than claimed.

## What the mechanism must do at grant, renewal and revocation

6. **Launch is an operator request inside the node.** ADR-0036 §6: the node starts the guest from a promoted profile, appraises, grants to itself and bridges. A script beside the rehearsal is rung 1's honest place and not the archetype's.
7. **The profile is a promoted Build artifact.** Its digest is the first element of the lease's confinement field. A digest nobody promoted is not a reference value, and the node refuses to launch one.
8. **The nonce comes from the log.** A hash of epoch, committed log index, principal and round. The guest generates a session key pair, and the evidence binds nonce and public key. No clock is involved, so ADR-0032's trusted-time requirement does not arise.
9. **One replica appraises, and the verdict is ordered.** The grant entry carries profile digest, rung, verdict, nonce, evidence digest and session public key. At apply, every replica checks the verifier's signature and that the nonce's epoch is still current. That is ADR-0033 §4's apply-time re-check with one more condition.
10. **Three verdicts with fixed responses.** Attested grants at the declared rung. Unattestable refuses and never grants lower. Contested refuses and orders a targeted revocation of the principal, not the profile and not the Persona, attributed under F2 with the evidence.
11. **No silent downgrade, at grant and at renewal.** Renewal keeps the rung, which `Lease_A1.tla`'s `M_LOWER` already checks and `check_renewal` already enforces. A principal whose evidence stops reaching the rung loses its lease, not its containment.
12. **The session is the attested guest's.** At rung 3 the session key replaces the reference's bearer token for principals. A lease is unusable from any other guest, including the same principal's next one.
13. **Re-attestation is event-triggered.** It happens at every renewal with a fresh nonce, measures the launch again and not the running guest, and an epoch fence invalidates every pending appraisal. No interval exists.
14. **PC/EC at the verifier.** An unreachable verifier stops grants. Live leases run to renewal. Refusal, expiry and revocation take the path that needs no quorum.
15. **The verifier key lives in the issued rules.** ADR-0025 §1 item (f) holds it; replacing a verifier is an issuance. The reference's rules schema still owes the field, and the node must read it.
16. **`Contained(p)` is a lookup, never a judgment.** Live lease at rung 3 on an Attested verdict, profile meeting P3 to P5, Persona in house on ADR-0017's axis, and the criterion in force as a check of the issued rules. The constant `is_contained()` becomes that check, and nothing semantic enters it.
17. **Reference-value withdrawal is decided.** When a vendor revokes a firmware version, the profile's minimum platform TCB version moves by promotion under P7, and the open question of whether live leases are fenced gets an answer, probably a targeted revocation by profile digest.
18. **Evidence is retained.** Evidence bytes belong in the archive, which does not exist. A Contested revocation attributed under F2 with no retained evidence is an attribution to nothing.

## What the guest, the profile and the premises must supply

19. **All seven clauses pass.** One vsock channel to the front door and no network device. A measured read-only root, the workspace, the pinned corpus root and nothing else. No shell, package manager or interpreter reachable from the principal, which is the P3-passing image nobody has built. Tools as Wasm components per call with a workspace capability only. Weights inside the guest and in the launch measurement. One principal per guest, one guest per round, destroyed at round end. Reference values carried in the profile.
20. **Rung-3 evidence of the stated shape.** A confidential-VM hardware report whose measurement covers firmware, kernel, initrd, root hash, profile digest and Persona digest, appraised by a verifier that is neither the host operator nor the mechanism.
21. **Accelerator attestation specified.** P5 names it and T-0045-21 is reserved. Without it P5 is half met, and ADR-0037 §3 R3's admitting use of conformance has no rung to be a precondition at.
22. **The Run profile written.** ADR-0042 §6 is Proposed: P4 cannot hold for a Run tool and channel fidelity replaces it, with the tool host enforcing the declared channel. Until then the archetype's confinement covers Build only and Run principals are `Mediated` at every rung.
23. **Every host power subtracts.** ADR-0034 §5's table must stay true of the design: refuse, kill, starve, withhold, launch a rogue image, attach a device, alter a running guest, each ends in a subtraction. Side channels and memory interposers stay a declared residual.
24. **The trusted base declared row by row.** The deployment declaration names T-0045-16 to 22 at their rung ranges. An undeclared row is excluded, and an excluded row makes the premise false.
25. **The Persona premises.** In-house pre-training on two lineages independent under E1 to E5, ADR-0035 ratified with its thresholds as floor values, and the independence table promoted. The lineage record is projected from the log, which needs ingress-bounded rounds, which only a rung-1-or-better guest reading over the channel provides. The guest and the criterion depend on each other, and the guest comes first.
26. **The Persona digest covers prompt, decoder and weights.** ADR-0016 §6 puts it in the launch measurement, so a swapped decoder is a Contested divergence rather than an undetected substitution.
27. **Observer placement holds.** Observer seats are separate guests on separate devices, each at the declared rung. The Reader runs inside the Thinker's guest and its outputs are facts; at rung 3 with inference attested, conformance may admit.
28. **The ratification run at rung 3.** One honest round with the lease reporting `contained = true`, each refusal seen to fire, including Contested on a rogue image ending in a revoked principal and an unreachable verifier ending in no grant, and a rung-0 principal beside it so that `Contained` is not everyone.

Of these, items 3, 11 and the lease field exist today. Items 6 to 9, 12, 15 and 16 are specified and not built. Items 17, 18, 21 and 22 are open obligations ADR-0034 carries by name. Items 19, 20 and 25 are what the corpus means when it says almost nobody can afford the archetype.
