### I2-1: "Overlay first, reported upstream" is not a route the accepted admission test or ADR-0058 offers, and every record's status rests on it
- Against: decisions/ADR-0059.md:133 and decisions/ADR-0060.md:105 (and decisions/ADR-0023.md:39, ADOPTERS_GUIDE_README.md:84, decisions/ADR-0058.md:101 for the other side)
- Basis: Proposed
- Claim: "ADR-0023 §4: a variation that holds a capability the archetype lacks is not a rung, and the archetype is incomplete. **This record is therefore drafted as an overlay's**"
- Why wrong: ADR-0023 §4, Accepted, reads in full (decisions/ADR-0023.md:39): "If it holds a capability `A` lacks, it is not a demotion: `A` is incomplete and must be extended before the variation is admitted. **Rejection under this test is a finding about the archetype, not about the proposal.**" The Adopter's Guide says the same (ADOPTERS_GUIDE_README.md:84-85): "If your deployment needs something the archetype lacks, the archetype is incomplete. Report it; do not declare it as a variation." ADR-0058 does not change this: its §5 is headed "A deployment may grade above the archetype, and the admission test is unchanged" (decisions/ADR-0058.md:101), and its §6 quotes the Guide only as far as "the archetype is incomplete. Report it." (decisions/ADR-0058.md:112-113), which drops "do not declare it as a variation". So accepted text rejects a variation that holds a capability the archetype lacks until the archetype is extended. The overlay records leave out "must be extended before the variation is admitted" and conclude the opposite order: hold the capability as a deployment's overlay, report it, and extend the archetype later or never. ADR-0060 §4 does the same (decisions/ADR-0060.md:105-107). Each record then hangs "**C3** does not apply while this is an overlay" (decisions/ADR-0059.md:15) and "**Carries a debt against:** nothing while this is an overlay." (decisions/ADR-0059.md:28) on that position. ADR-0029 §3 (decisions/ADR-0029.md:51-58) has no status class for a ratified record in `decisions/` that is not the archetype's, so what ratifying one of these would put in force is stated nowhere. None of the ten asks for the overlay graph or the graded inventory that ADR-0058 §4 makes the content of an overlay (decisions/ADR-0058.md:88-89).
- Fix belongs in: ADR-0059 to ADR-0068 (Deployment position, Status note, the "what is reported upstream" sections); ADR-0058 or an amendment to ADR-0023 §4 if an overlay is to be allowed to hold a capability the archetype lacks
- Severity: High
- Confidence: high
- Already on the corpus's own lists: no (ADR-0068 Findings 2 notices the ADR-0045 §4 half only; see I2-17)
- **Verifier:** confirmed with correction
- **Verifier's correction:** The defect stands; two changes of scope. ### I2-2: An intent's budget is bounded by the seating's figure and starts afresh with each intent, so the charter's `budget` does not bound what a plane "may ever issue"
- Against: decisions/ADR-0059.md:62 and :68 (and decisions/ADR-0060.md:73, decisions/ADR-0063.md:157, spec/Intent.tla:101 and :118 for the other side)
- Basis: Proposed
- Claim: "| `budget` | The most leases the plane and every plane below it may ever issue |" and "so the root's tally bounds what is allocated across the whole tree, and what is issued in any subtree with no captured plane"
- Why wrong: ADR-0060 makes an intent "a charter for one round" that names "an activation budget" (decisions/ADR-0060.md:64-66), says "**Every field of an intent is the promoted seating's or narrower** ⟨Q4⟩" (:73), and ends an intent "when its budget is spent" (:76). ADR-0059 debits a budget only "when it is seated" (decisions/ADR-0059.md:68), and a later intent is "A round inside a promoted seating", "signed by one operator" (decisions/ADR-0059.md:95). No record says a later intent's budget is debited from what is left of the seating's. ADR-0063 confirms the reading: "Kept, as the absolute bound. It does not slide, so a long mission needs either a large budget or a new intent for every stretch" (decisions/ADR-0063.md:157). The module does the same: `Bounded(o) == EnvelopeOK(o) /\ o.budget <= SeatingBudget` (spec/Intent.tla:101) and, on each new intent, `/\ spent' = 0` (spec/Intent.tla:118). So an honest autonomous plane under n successive intents issues up to n times the seating's budget, on one operator's signature each time. That also departs from ADR-0055's budget, "a bound `N` on the leases the workflow may ever issue" (decisions/ADR-0055.md:75), and ADR-0060 §6 lists ADR-0055 as owing only "where a mesh runs" (decisions/ADR-0060.md:155).
- Fix belongs in: ADR-0060 §2 and §6; ADR-0059 §1; ADR-0063 Options; a successor to spec/Intent.tla
- Severity: Medium-High
- Confidence: high
- Already on the corpus's own lists: no

### I2-3: ADR-0063's bound is stated over "effects" but covers only charged entries; the set itself classes sends, beacons, hold, terminal behaviour and a relayed release act as effects, and none is charged
- Against: decisions/ADR-0063.md:96 (and decisions/ADR-0063.md:89, decisions/ADR-0065.md:60 and :66, decisions/ADR-0062.md:128, decisions/ADR-0066.md:84 for the other side)

- Why wrong: ADR-0063 §3 charges two things only: "What is charged is an effect: a `local` entry and an `ordered` one, as ADR-0042 §2 counts effects for an Observer. (:66-67). ADR-0062 §5: "**The beacon is an effect** ⟨R1⟩ (ADR-0015, ADR-0065 §2). After a lease ends a vehicle holds or carries out its terminal behaviour, and may send a beacon within its count."
