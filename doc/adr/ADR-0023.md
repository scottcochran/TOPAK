# ADR-0023 — Degeneracy as the deployment-variation relation; archetype as its unique maximum

**Status:** Accepted (genesis) · 2026.09.23 · confirmation required (ADR-0029 C3) ⟨B3⟩
**Status note:** Confirmation required under C3: §5 reverses ADR-0008's Observer scoping and
ADR-0017's ordering-primacy clause in effect, and neither superseding record is written. Until
confirmed, anything published citing this record's guarantees says "author-ratified".
**Blocks:** the three demotion records pending from 2026.09.13.

---

## Context

The 09.12 open list carried "Deployment profiles. Archetype as design target, lower rungs as documented subtractions. **Axis question unresolved.**" In the 48 hours since, three demotion proposals arrived on three different parameters — Persona provenance (ADR-0017), event-log legibility, Observer scoping — each framed as a "rung," which asserts a single total order. They are not comparable to one another.

Two of the three reverse Accepted records. The archetype frame is retroactive and currently unbounded: nothing says how many ratified decisions it may overturn, or on what test.

The Observer proposal failed on its own terms. Shared Observers see cross-principal timelines that per-principal Observers cannot, so neither is above the other. A ladder cannot order them, and the error was only caught by inspection.

Subtraction language ("what did we give up") yields prose, not an inventory, and offers no test for whether a proposed variation is admissible as a demotion at all.

## Decision

1. **The variation relation is degeneracy.** `D` is a degenerate form of archetype `A` iff `D` is obtained from `A` by driving one or more named mechanism parameters to a limiting value. *Degenerate* is the formal register; *reduced* is the operational register; they denote the same relation.

2. **The effect of degeneracy is premise loss, not property weakening.** Each parameter limit is bound to the theorem premises it falsifies. A degenerate deployment's inventory is `A`'s inventory minus every theorem whose premises are no longer discharged. Theorems are present or absent; there is no partial credit and no "weaker version of the theorem."

3. **The archetype is the unique maximum of the parameter lattice** — a reference point, not a recommended configuration. It is not required to be shippable.

4. **Admission test.** A proposed variation is a rung only if it is reachable from `A` by parameter limit. If it holds a capability `A` lacks, it is not a demotion: `A` is incomplete and must be extended before the variation is admitted. **Rejection under this test is a finding about the archetype, not about the proposal.**

   **4a. Type changes are admission conditions too.** ⟨R4⟩ Reachability by parameter limit is necessary and not sufficient. A variation is admissible only if it also declares, for every participant whose **type** it changes, what that change does to the gate and to the trusted base. The motivating cases are in ADR-0025's annex: under D-0025-1 and D-0025-2 the anchor key-holders stop being at the gate and become principals, and the gate moves to the genesis-declared rotation or eviction rule. Such a variation can void **no theorem at all** while moving what the theorems are about, so clause 2's premise-loss test does not see it. The declared type change is part of the record under §6 and is checked at the same gate.

5. **Rungs are per-axis.** "Rung" is always qualified by its parameter — *a legibility demotion*, *a provenance demotion*. A deployment's position is a tuple. The unqualified phrase "rung demotion" is barred from the corpus.

6. **Every degeneracy carries a declaration record** with fixed schema: **kind** ⟨R4⟩, parameter, archetype value, deployed value, premises falsified, theorems voided, **type changes** ⟨R4⟩, mitigation (optional), composition note (mandatory above one parameter). Declaration is mandatory; mitigation is not.

   **6a. Kind.** ⟨R4⟩ Two values. A **mechanism degeneracy** changes a value inside the verified object — the rules floor (ADR-0026 R1, R2, R9) is the worked case — and reopens the proofs that quantify over it; it ships as a mechanism release. A **declaration degeneracy** changes something outside it, such as anchor custody instantiating `Authentic` (ADR-0025 §2), and reopens nothing. D-0024-n are the first kind; A-0025 and D-0025-n are the second. The field is what makes ADR-0024's "a promotion degeneracy is a mechanism release, and an anchor degeneracy is a declaration" checkable rather than asserted.

   **6b. Type changes.** ⟨R4⟩ The field §4a requires: for each participant whose type the variation changes, its archetype type, its deployed type, where the gate sits after the change, and what enters or leaves the trusted base. Empty is a claim, stated as one: *no participant changes type.*

7. **Composition is not assumed monotone, and is discharged here.** ⟨R3⟩ The inventory of a multiply-degenerate deployment is not automatically the intersection of the singly-degenerate inventories: two parameter limits may jointly falsify a premise that neither falsifies alone. Composition over the parameter lattice is owned by this record, not by ADR-0013's exception mechanism. ~~Discharging composition is assigned to ADR-0013's exception mechanism, generalized from the policy layer to the architecture layer.~~ ⟨R1 · R3 · see S3-4⟩

   **7a. The composition note names the delta.** A declaration above one parameter (§6) carries a composition note listing every theorem whose premises are **jointly** falsified but **individually** discharged — the deployment's inventory loss beyond the union of the singly-degenerate losses. An empty delta is a claim, stated as one: *no theorem is lost to composition beyond the union.*

   **7b. Admission requires the note.** A composite variation is admissible under §4 only if 7a's note exists and its delta is stated. A missing note is not an empty delta, and the declaration fails at the Build promotion gate on the same check that reads the §6 schema.

   **7c. Ownership may move again.** If a composition rule later carries proof content of its own — an invariant with a mutation that must fail, as ADR-0013 §4 now has — it splits into its own record under the decomposition test that produced ADR-0028. This clause keeps that split available; it does not pre-empt it.

## Options considered

- **Single total order (status quo).** Rejected: three incomparable parameters already exist, and the Observer case is a counterexample from inside the corpus.
- **Baseline-plus-hardening.** Rejected on the 09.12 ground — it makes the strong configuration optional and the weak one default, inverting what the pattern is for.
- **Enumerated named profiles** ("SCI profile," "HIPAA profile"). Rejected: enumeration does not compose and the set grows per customer. Degeneracy generates profiles from parameters instead.
- **Lattice with no maximum.** Rejected: without a maximum there is nothing to prove theorems about and "demotion" has no referent.

## Consequences

**Gained.** The 09.12 axis question closes — there is no single axis, and degeneracy supplies ordering per parameter without requiring one globally. The admission test is mechanical, would have caught this morning's Observer error before it reached a record, and bounds retroactivity by making each rejection name a specific archetype extension rather than reopening the corpus. The declaration schema is checkable at the Build promotion gate instead of being a prose obligation. This is the fourth appearance of the monotonicity principle — grants/denials, floor refinement, authority direction, now deployment variation.

**Paid.** Nobody ships the archetype; every real deployment is degenerate, so the TLA+ theorems are proved about an unshipped configuration. Article prose must say this outright, with ISO 26262's safety-element-out-of-context as the precedent. Tractability and shippability now point in opposite directions on the Observer axis: the symmetry-reduction defense holds at the archetype, which is the harder model-checking case and the only one with a full inventory. The archetype grows whenever a variation fails admission, so the corpus never closes — honest, but it removes "the archetype is frozen" as a publication convenience. And *degenerate* reads pejoratively outside the formal register; the register split in §1 mitigates, and the glossary must note the minor collision with TLA+'s own "degenerate behavior."

## Obligations not discharged here

- **The parameter set is not enumerated by this record.** Known so far: Persona provenance (0017), event-log legibility (unnumbered), Observer scoping (unnumbered), effect-stream role (0005), and arguably HITL/HOTL/HOOTL posture. Enumeration is a follow-on and should be a table, not a list.
- ~~**Composition is assigned to 0013, which still has no owner.** This record increases 0013's load without staffing it.~~ **Rewritten 2026.09.23** ⟨R3⟩: composition is owned here (§7). What remains undischarged is narrower and is stated as its own obligation — **the composition note has a required shape but no mechanism behind it.** §7a says what the note must contain and §7b makes its presence an admission condition, so the gate can check that a note exists and is non-empty in form. Nothing checks that the delta it states is *correct*: that is a claim by the declaring deployment, like every other premise-loss claim in §2, and it is only as good as the theorem-to-premise binding the archetype publishes.
- **No proof that each parameter's chain is finite with a bottom.** If a chain is unbounded below, "degenerate" stays well-defined but "declared sacrifice" stops being enumerable.
- **ADR-0008 and ADR-0017's ordering-primacy clause are reversed in effect by §5.** Superseding records are required; this record does not itself supersede them.
