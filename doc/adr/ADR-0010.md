# ADR-0010 — The verified object is the enforcement mechanism, its epoch discipline, and a minimal policy floor

**Status:** Accepted (genesis) · 2026.09.12
**Pending:** A1, A2 (trigger ADR-0026; see History)
**Supersedes:** The 09.11 proposed text, which framed the bundle as an unconstrained parameter.

---

**Context.** Moon & Varshney place boundary policy inside the verified artifact and inside the
trusted base. TOPAK externalizes policy into signed Rego bundles that change at runtime without
re-verification. Full inclusion collapses EL zone 1 — Rego's purity over a pinned bundle is what
lets a partitioned node keep enforcing without coordination — and makes every policy edit a
consensus-ordered deploy. An unconstrained parameter preserves the zone but leaks the grants/denials
asymmetry at the policy layer: a locally-pinned bundle can grant with no consensus anywhere in the
loop, and a stale bundle is unknown rather than bounded.

**Decision.** The verified object is the enforcement mechanism, its version pinning, its epoch
fencing, and a **minimal policy floor** ⟨A1 · pending⟩. Operational bundles remain versioned parameters, admissible
only if they refine the floor — they may subtract authority, never add it. Refinement
(`bundle ⊑ floor`) is decided at promotion time as a Build obligation. **One floor, shared by Build
and Run**, instantiated through the same `Contain` component per ADR-0002. ⟨A2 · pending⟩

**Consequences.**

- Containment properties are proven absolutely against the floor and cannot be weakened by any
  admissible parameter. Grants trace to the floor; the floor moves only under consensus-ordered
  change control. Consensus for grants, local for denials, lifted from decisions to policy.
- Stale bundles become monotone-safe — bounded below by the floor regardless of age. EL zone 1
  survives intact, sound by construction rather than by operational care, the same shape as the
  lease argument.
- Refinement checking lands in Build, where unavailability costs a stalled pipeline. ADR-0007's
  syntactic-lift discipline makes the check decidable over a finite alphabet.
- **The floor must stay minimal.** Its reviewability by a human at deploy cadence is the whole basis
  for trusting Build's HITL gate; pushing operational policy down into it destroys that and
  re-couples policy to release cadence.
- **Accepted trade, stated rather than discovered:** this concentrates policy correctness in Build,
  which TOPAK declares the hostile domain. A vacuous floor admits every bundle and discharges every
  containment property over nothing, invisibly. Rebuttal to M&V on their own ground: inclusion
  relocates the same risk to the same domain, since ~~§4.2's~~ §4.6 and Appendix B's ⟨E1⟩ failure was a Build failure.
- Build's regress terminates on a verified artifact rather than a declared axiom set — the floor is
  the one artifact verified rather than promoted. A Build-domain escape and a Run-domain escape
  become one proof obligation.

**Tangentials this generates**

1. **ADR-0012 amend.** The resident default-deny bundle is now the floor, not merely the cold-start
   bundle. Status promotion; decision text needs rewriting to say so, and to note it was ratified
   ahead of its parent on an independent availability argument.
2. **ADR-0007 extension.** Totality, non-vacuity and discrimination gates must run on the **floor
   itself**, not only on operational bundles. This is the direct countermeasure to the vacuous-floor
   failure and the only thing standing between you and a silent ~~§4.2~~ §4.6 / Appendix B ⟨E1⟩.
3. **ADR-0009 and ADR-0006 become floor exceptions.** Break-glass adds authority and erasure is
   privileged — neither can be an ordinary refining bundle. Both must be modeled exceptions inside
   the verified object with their quorum predicates proven. Write the exception mechanism once,
   covering both; sequence them together after this.
4. **New ADR — floor authorship and change control.** "Who may propose a floor change" is a
   different authorization question from "who may promote a bundle," and it's where the human key
   and the vacuity gates concentrate. Deserves its own record.
5. **Still open:** one global floor versus one per principal class. Global is provable and may be
   too restrictive to be useful; per-class multiplies refinement checks. Not blocking, but it shapes
   item 4.
