# ADR-0019 — Optional decode observability

**Status:** Accepted (genesis) · 2026.09.12
**Status note:** Narrow form. The same-stream fork is resolved by this ratification, see Ratification note.
**Pending:** E1 (see History)
**Depends on:** ADR-0016 (Persona), ADR-0004, ADR-0006, ADR-0008
**Relates to:** ADR-0016 §8 ⟨E1 · pending⟩ (boundary-only replay)

---

## Context

ADR-0016 §8 ⟨E1 · pending⟩ scopes replay to boundary events, conceding that root-cause analysis can establish
*what* and never *why*. Logging the Thinker's emitted logits is the obvious partial
compensation.

**The obvious reading of it is wrong, and must not go in the article.** Logits are distribution
mass over next tokens, not considered-and-rejected decisions. Unless action selection is
structured as a single constrained token choice, a rejected *action* was never in the
distribution. Treating logprobs as introspection is the same category error as reading
attention weights as explanation, and it is well-trodden ground for a critic.

What the signal actually supports is narrower, decidable, and more useful.

---

## Decision

**Personas may optionally declare `decode_observability: topk_logprobs`, rung-gated. Its
purpose is decoding attestation, not introspection.**

Two uses, both verification rather than suitability:

1. **Decoding conformance.** Sampled token plus distribution plus the Persona's declared
   parameters makes conformance a decidable predicate over a signed artifact — legitimately a
   precondition, not merely evidence. This is the only new *admitting* check in the recent set.
2. **Substitution detection.** Distributional fingerprint drift is the second half of the
   MANAGE 3.2 answer. Lease attestation (ADR-0016 §6) catches a *declared* model change; drift
   catches an *undeclared* one. Neither covers both.

### Constraints

- **Top-k logprobs, not full logits.** Full vocabulary per token is not shippable.
- **Not in round topics.** Logprobs are cognitive payload and ADR-0004 excludes them from the
  control plane. They are also plausibly reconstructable toward the prompt, so retaining them
  after shredding a payload may preserve a shadow of the thing erasure promised to destroy.
  **This makes storage location an ADR-0006 decision, not an implementation detail.**
- **Semantic reads are deny-only.** Conformance and distributional distance are decidable and
  may admit. Any *threshold* on drift, or any "entropy looks healthy" judgment, is ADR-0007
  semantic and may only deny or quarantine.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

- Closes the undeclared-substitution half of the original MANAGE 3.2 exposure.
- Gives ADR-0017's rung ordering a capability basis: providers that expose no logprobs
  structurally cannot supply this check.

### Negative

- **It breaks the same-stream property.** ADR-0016 §8 ⟨E1 · pending⟩ keeps `L` honest by ensuring replay
  shows no investigator anything the Observer lacked at detection time. Logging logprobs the
  Observer does not consume reintroduces exactly that asymmetry.

  **Resolved at ratification by option 2.** The asymmetry is accepted and bounded: the logprob
  stream is scoped to decoding conformance and substitution detection, and may not be cited in
  any claim about Observer detection latency `L` or in any argument that depends on the
  same-stream property.

  Option 1 — putting the Observer on the logprob stream — remains available as a future
  amendment, and the ordering is deliberate. Adding the Observer later widens what the stream
  may be cited for; citing it first and adding the Observer afterwards cannot be undone. The
  narrow form is the monotone choice, which is the same argument used everywhere else in this
  pattern.

- Volume. Top-k per token across a Thinker's output is a large stream for a signal used
  intermittently, which argues for sampling — and sampled conformance checking is a weaker
  claim that needs stating as such.

---

## Ratification note

Accepted in **narrow form**: the record is ratified, its scope is bounded by the resolution
above, and any widening is an amendment rather than an implementation choice. ADR-0016 §9
carries the matching constraint on the Persona field, so the bound holds from both directions.

---

## Open

- **Costing option 1** — what an Observer on the logprob stream does to ADR-0008's replica
  count. Not blocking; it is the amendment path, not a debt.
- **Sampling rate versus conformance strength.**
- **Does conformance failure deny the action or evict the principal?** A decoding-parameter
  violation is a Persona-integrity failure, not a policy violation, which suggests eviction —
  but eviction on a statistical signal is a strong response and interacts with ADR-0017's rung
  guarantees.
