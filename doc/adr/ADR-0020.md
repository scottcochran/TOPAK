# ADR-0020 — Replay scope is boundary events only

**Status:** Accepted (genesis) · 2026.09.12
**Status note:** Scoped, with revisit triggers.
**Pending:** E1 (see History)
**Split from:** ADR-0016 §8, before ratification
**Depends on:** ADR-0004 (control-plane facts separated from cognitive payloads), ADR-0003
(round topics), ADR-0006 (erasure)
**Note:** Numbering — decided before ADR-0017 through ADR-0019 and numbered after them. Gaps and
non-chronological numbering in an ADR log are self-documenting; silent renumbering is what
breaks cross-references.

---

## Context

Drafted inside ADR-0016 as §8 and split out on the decomposition test: replay scope can flip
with Persona untouched, and Persona can be restructured with replay scope standing. Two
decisions.

The split matters more than usual here because this record is the one explicitly written as
temporary. When its revisit triggers fire, the amendment should land on a record about replay —
not on a record titled *The model enters the system as a Persona*, with every cross-reference to
boundary-only replay pointing at a decision about model identity. That is ADR-0003's bundling
failure, diagnosed on 09.12 and avoided here by one session.

---

## Decision

**Replay reconstructs what crossed the membrane, not what happened inside it.**

The replayable record for a round is:

- actions attempted, **including those denied**
- effects emitted
- lease and epoch transitions
- the Persona binding in force

Cognitive intermediate state — scratchpads, chains of thought, token-level traces — is out of
scope and is not written to round topics.

**This is ADR-0004 carried to its conclusion, not a new restriction.** Once control-plane facts
are separated from cognitive payloads, replay over the control plane *is* boundary replay. What
is decided here is to stop treating the other half as deferred work.

### Three properties follow

- **Denials are part of the record.** Without them the trail shows a clean system and cannot
  distinguish *behaved* from *was prevented* — the only evidence containment did anything. This
  is the clause most likely to be dropped by an implementer optimizing topic volume, and it is
  the one that must not be.
- **The Observer and replay read the same stream.** No privileged channel exists, so replay
  cannot show an investigator something the Observer did not have at detection time. Detection
  latency `L` stays an honest number rather than one measured against hindsight. ADR-0019's
  narrow form exists to preserve this.
- **Erasure blast radius shrinks.** Less cognitive payload in round topics means ADR-0003's
  crypto-shredding ⟨E1 · pending⟩ covers less and ADR-0006's obligations get smaller.

### Consequence for Persona

Determinism class (ADR-0016) is recorded metadata for inventory and incident context, never an
admission precondition. Non-reproducible Personas are therefore admissible and the Thinker seat
stays open to any model class.

---

## Consequences

### The cost, stated plainly

Root-cause analysis can establish *what* a principal did and never *why*.

That is the same concession the NIST AI RMF Playbook names in MANAGE 3.2's own rationale —
opacity in pre-trained models hinders root-cause analysis. It also **sharpens** the existing
MANAGE 2.4 forensic-preservation contradiction rather than easing it: there is now less to
preserve by design. This must be carried into ADR-0013 as a constraint on any forensic floor,
not discovered there.

Defensible, because the thesis was never that the occupant is legible — detection is a boundary
property. But it is a concession, not a win, and the article should use those words.

### Convergence worth naming

ADR-0018's residual arrives at the same place from a different direction: a decoder that
mistranslates one admissible action into another is undetectable by any total validator, so the
record keeps *this principal emitted admissible action Y* and loses *the model meant X*.

Two independent derivations of the same limit is evidence that boundary-only replay is not a
local compromise but where the system's epistemic boundary actually sits. State it once, as a
property, rather than twice as two apologies.

---

## Revisit triggers

Scoped deliberately. Reopen on any of:

- a regulatory or contractual demand to attribute intent rather than action;
- an Observer detection strategy requiring pre-effect cognitive signal to meet `L`;
- ADR-0013 producing a forensic floor that cannot be discharged without reasoning.

---

## Open

- **Raw emission retention** (shared with ADR-0018). Retaining it allows after-the-fact
  verification that the typed lift was faithful; not retaining it shrinks the erasure surface.
  Between ADR-0003 and ADR-0006, and still unassigned.
