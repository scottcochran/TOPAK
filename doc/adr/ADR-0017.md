# ADR-0017 — Archetype admits only in-house pre-trained Personas

**Status:** Accepted (genesis) · 2026.09.12
**Status note:** Ratified with a named obligation, see Ratification note.
**Depends on:** ADR-0016 (Persona), ADR-0008 (Observer fault-domain independence)
**Closes:** the deployment-axis question open since 2026.09.11

---

## Context

Deployment profiles have been agreed in shape — an archetype as design target, lower rungs as
documented subtractions — and stalled on the axis. Every candidate axis so far described a
*consequence* of a rung rather than the thing being subtracted.

ADR-0016 supplies the missing variable. Persona provenance is a recorded field, and it is the
field that governs whether the model is an artifact you possess or a service you call. That
distinction determines effect exclusivity, which determines which theorems apply — so it is not
merely an ordering, it is the ordering the theorem inventory already responds to.

Persona provenance as an ordered axis re-derives supply-chain levels already standardized in SLSA
(Supply-chain Levels for Software Artifacts) and in-toto attestations. TOPAK's contribution is
applying them to model lineage and binding them to seat eligibility, not the ordering itself. ⟨E1⟩

---

## Decision

**The archetype admits only Personas pre-trained in house. The deployment axis is Persona
provenance, and every external model is a documented subtraction at a lower rung.**

### The axis

| Rung | Provenance | Principal class |
|---|---|---|
| Archetype | In-house pre-train, two independent lineages | `Contained` |
| 1 | In-house fine-tune on external base | `Contained`, with a lineage debt stated |
| 2 | Attested external — provider signs a version statement | `Mediated` |
| 3 | Asserted external — endpoint and version string only | `Mediated` |

Lower rungs may only subtract. This is the fourth appearance of the monotone principle
(checkpoint §3): archetype is to rungs as floor is to bundles.

### External model implies `Mediated`

Prompt egress to a provider is an out-of-band effect. Effect exclusivity is therefore false by
construction, and the Thinker leaves the set over which safety properties quantify. Attribution
and authorization survive; *no forbidden state is reached* stops covering the cognitive seat.

This is **derived, not asserted** — it follows from the 09.12 `Contained`/`Mediated` split with
no new machinery, which makes rung 2's cost a statement about the theorem inventory rather than
a risk acknowledgement. It is also the second independent motivation for that split, after the
human-as-LTRO case, which is the best evidence that split is load-bearing rather than an
accommodation.

### Two independent lineages at the archetype

In-house does not mean one training run. A single lineage across all LTRO seats reproduces the
monoculture blind spot inside the rung that is supposed to sacrifice nothing — and would make
an external Observer *strictly safer* than the archetype on the one property Observers exist
for.

The archetype therefore requires two lineages with separate corpora and separate architecture
ancestry: the model-layer analogue of ADR-0008's fault-domain independence, and the same
argument — correlated failure defeats the Observer regardless of topology. The seat-distinctness
predicate of ADR-0016 §5 is what enforces it.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

- The deployment line stops reading *pending, axis unresolved*.
- Rung cost becomes computable rather than rhetorical: each rung states which theorems it keeps.
- The third-party governance cluster — MANAGE 3.1, MAP 4.1, GOVERN 6.1 — goes quiet at the
  archetype by construction rather than by control.
- Decode observability (ADR-0019) is available only at upper rungs, since external providers
  frequently expose no logprobs at all. That gives ADR-0017 a *capability* argument for the
  ordering rather than a trust argument, which is a much better one.

### Negative

- **Almost nobody can afford the archetype.** Say so in the article. A design target that no
  reader can reach is honest only if it is labelled as a target and every rung below it is
  specified as carefully.
- **Two lineages doubles the most expensive line item**, and is the clause most likely to be
  quietly dropped. Its defense is ADR-0008's, restated: it is not redundancy, it is
  independence.
- **Trusting trust relocates rather than terminates.** The regress moves from the provider to
  training data, training code, and hardware. Thompson applies to the trainer. The honest narrow
  claim is that the untrusted parties move *inside* the declared axiom set.
- **MEASURE burden rises.** With no provider producing a model card, MANAGE 3.2's continual
  monitoring points entirely inward, joining the unmeasured Observer detection latency `L`.

### Neutral

- A training run is an artifact-producing effect inside `Build(Contain)`, and Build's membrane
  has no effect stream (checkpoint §1). The archetype makes that open item substantially
  heavier — a training run is a very large effect — which is now the strongest argument for
  closing it.

---

## Ratification note

**Independent lineage is ratified as a requirement with its criterion outstanding, and the
criterion is assigned rather than deferred.** Two training runs over overlapping public corpora
are not independent in the sense the Observer needs; without a test, the archetype's headline
requirement cannot be checked and the strongest claim in the pattern rests on an unfalsifiable
clause.

Obligation, owner required at assignment: a decidable or auditable criterion for lineage
independence, sufficient to reject the overlapping-corpora case. Until it exists, the archetype
may be *described* but no deployment may be *declared* at archetype rung.

The deferral pattern from ADR-0006 and ADR-0009 against an unwritten ADR-0013 is the one being
avoided here: a debt filed against nobody is how the highest-risk item on the board came to
exist.

---

## Open

- **What counts as an independent lineage.** Criterion assigned above; the record stays open
  until it is written.
- **Is rung 1 one rung or two?** Fine-tuning an external base retains external lineage in the
  weights while keeping the artifact in hand. `Contained` is defensible, but the debt differs
  from the archetype's and may deserve its own row.
- **Rung declaration authority** — who may declare a deployment at a given rung. Same shape as
  ADR-0014.
