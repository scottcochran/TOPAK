# ADR-0024 — Promotion requires a unanimous quorum

**Status:** Proposed · 2026.09.15
**Deployment position:** Archetype (ADR-0023)
**Amends:** ADR-0002 (gate description; A1 in ADR-0002 History)
**Narrows:** ADR-0014, to proposal eligibility
**Depends on:** ADR-0025 (roster issuance), ADR-0026 (bounds R1, R2, R9 on quorum parameters) ⟨R1⟩, ADR-0010 (policy floor),
ADR-0007 (gates), ADR-0022 (`Vote`)

---

## Context

ADR-0002 places the Build promotion gate on a human signing key outside `Contain`. Three
commitments made since contradict it.

- **Grants need consensus** (P2). Break-glass is quorum-witnessed (ADR-0009), and human approval is
  an unprivileged grant (checkpoint §5). The single promotion key is the last privileged human
  grant, and the most consequential one.
- **Build is the hostile domain.** One coerced or compromised approver can promote alone.
- **Promotion is a Build consensus operation**, per the 09.13 floor-rollout session. This was
  concluded but never recorded.

Human-in-the-loop (HITL) latency is affordable in Build under PC/EC. A stricter grant rule costs
throughput, not safety.

---

## Decision

### 1. Promotion is a grant

Any artifact crossing from `Build` to `Run` requires promotion. This covers operational bundles,
mechanism releases (including policy-floor and rules-floor changes), and Personas.

### 2. Quorum rule (read from `rules.quorum`; archetype value unanimity, bounded by ADR-0026 R1 and R9) ⟨R1⟩

The mechanism reads `quorum` and `minGrant` from the issued rules. `quorum` is either `"all"` or a
grant-vote threshold *k* ∈ ℕ. The former `"any"` value is withdrawn: it is *k* = 1, and S6-2 showed
it tolerates no Byzantine approver, so it was never the k-of-n degeneracy it appeared to be. ⟨R1⟩
**Author recusal is not a parameter.** It is a mechanism constant (§6), enforced by the ADR-0022
`Vote` envelope, so `recuse` is not an issued value and ADR-0026 R3 is withdrawn as vacuous
(S6-3). ⟨R1⟩ Hard-coding the values that *are* issued makes
ADR-0026 R1, R2 and R9 vacuous (non-vacuity check, 09.15). At the archetype, promotion requires a grant vote from **every** grant seat on the promotion roster at rules epoch
*e*. Any grant-seat deny or abstention is a denial. **A timeout is never a grant.** This is a
mechanism constant, not a parameter (formerly ADR-0026 R4, withdrawn as vacuous). A veto-seat deny is a denial; a
veto-seat silence is not. Requiring an Observer attestation of "no finding" would make semantic
judgment an enabling condition, which ADR-0007 forbids. The roster is an anchor issuance
(ADR-0025 §1a). This quorum cannot change its own rules.

### 3. Votes bind to a triple

A vote binds to (artifact digest, gate-evidence digest, rules epoch). Any rules-epoch change voids
in-flight votes. This is fencing, in the same shape as leases.

### 4. Gates enable voting; they do not vote

`Vote` is enabled only after two checks pass:
- ADR-0007's totality, non-vacuity, and discrimination gates;
- `bundle ⊑ floor` (ADR-0010).

### 5. Seat classes

| Class | Holder | Alphabet |
|---|---|---|
| **Grant seat** | Human `Mediated` principal with a named organizational role | `Vote(grant)`, `Vote(deny)` |
| **Veto seat** | The independent Observer replicated group (ADR-0008); never an agent in the author's fault domain | `Vote(deny)` only |

Under ADR-0007, semantic judgment is admissible as a deny and inadmissible as a grant. Unanimity
makes every seat a veto, so a veto seat is simply one whose alphabet lacks `grant`.

### 6. Author recusal

The principal that performed `Submit` (ADR-0022) holds no seat for that artifact. **This is a
mechanism constant, not an issued value** ⟨R1⟩: the ADR-0022 `Vote` envelope refuses an author's
vote, so no issuance can admit one, in the same shape as "a timeout is never a grant." If recusal
leaves fewer grant seats than the rules-floor minimum (ADR-0026 R2) ⟨R1⟩, promotion is denied.

### 7. Promotion is a round

A promotion runs as a pipeline invocation of the promotion stage, keyed by artifact digest. Where
Build rounds emit to an effect stream, they do so under ADR-0021; **this record does not depend on
that**, and nothing here requires ADR-0021 to be ratified first ⟨R2⟩. What promotion requires is
§8's record of the vote, which is a consensus-log fact and not a property of any topic scheme
(S6-8).

### 8. Votes are non-erasable ⟨R2⟩

Votes are control-plane facts (ADR-0004), boundary events, and outside erasure scope. Review
commentary is payload and remains erasable.

**The authoritative vote is the admit decision in the consensus log** (ADR-0015), which is the
object ADR-0028 F3 retains. A voter's round-topic emission carries the vote together with a
hash-and-pointer to its commentary in the separately governed store (ADR-0004); the commentary is
what erasure removes. This is the split ADR-0004 already makes for the archive, applied to votes.

Without it, §8 and ADR-0003 contradict each other: the sealed topic is the unit of erasure, so a
topic holding both a vote and its commentary is either erasable as a unit, which loses F3, or
permanently unerasable, which loses the commentary clause above (S6-5). Neither the topic nor the
emission is the record; the log is.

### 9. Runtime subtraction is not promotion

E-stop, lease expiry, and `bundle(deny=all)` remain local and attributed.

### Enabling condition

```tla
PromotionEnabled(a) ==
  /\ GatesPass(a) /\ Refines(a.bundle, PolicyFloor)
  /\ LET r == Rules[rulesEpoch]
         G == r.grantSeats \ {Author(a)}
         Granted == {s \in G : votes[a][s] = <<"grant", a.digest, a.evidence, rulesEpoch>>}
     IN  /\ Cardinality(G) >= r.minGrant
         /\ IF r.quorum = "all" THEN Granted = G ELSE Cardinality(Granted) >= r.quorum
         /\ \A s \in r.vetoSeats : votes[a][s] # <<"deny", a.digest, a.evidence, rulesEpoch>>
\* Only recorded votes count; a timeout is never a grant (mechanism constant).
\* Author recusal is a mechanism constant: the author is removed from the counted
\* seats here and the ADR-0022 Vote envelope refuses the vote itself. ⟨R1⟩
```

⟨R1⟩ replaces the revision-4 snippet, which read `recuse` from the issued rules and expressed the
non-unanimous case as `Granted # {}`. The struck snippet is under History.

## Why this rule is modeled, while anchor custody is declared

The promotion quorum's archetype values live **inside** the rules floor (ADR-0026), and so inside the verified object.
Degrading it changes what the mechanism admits, which reopens the promotion theorem. Anchor
composition lives **outside**, instantiating an opaque `Authentic`. Degrading it reopens nothing.

The consequence: **a promotion degeneracy is a mechanism release, and an anchor degeneracy is a
declaration.**

---

## Degeneracies (ADR-0023)

| Record | Rule | Premise falsified | Sacrificed | Gained |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D-0024-1** | k-of-n grant seats | No promotion without every grant seat | Tolerance of n−k Byzantine approvers | Liveness under n−k absent approvers |
| **D-0024-2** | Single signer (the pre-0024 status quo) | Grants need consensus | All approver fault tolerance; reintroduces a privileged human grant | Throughput |

**D-0024-1 is `quorum = k`** for 1 < *k* < |counted grant seats|, which relaxes R1. **D-0024-2 is
`quorum = 1` with a single-seat roster**, which relaxes R1, R9 (the threshold floor) and R2 (the
roster minimum). Both are changes to issued `quorum` and `minGrant` values, which requires relaxing
ADR-0026 entries, not changing this record. ⟨R1⟩ They require an anchor issuance plus a promotion under the rule being
replaced. Approver fault tolerance and anchor recoverability are separate axes and are declared
independently.

## Consequences

**Positive**
- Promotion obeys P2 like every other grant. The last privileged human grant is gone.
- No strict subset of grant seats can promote.
- Voters are principals on every custody rung, because the anchor evicts them. Promotion enters
  `Build(Contain)` enforcement coverage.
- The Observer gets a principled promotion role: veto.

**Negative**
- **Liveness equals the availability of every grant seat.** This includes Persona and
  incident-driven bundle promotions.
- **Correlated review failure is untouched.** Unanimous humans reading the same vacuous gate output
  fail together (Moon & Varshney §4.6; Knight & Leveson). MEASURE 2.13 and the floor-fitness gate
  stay open.
- **One compromised seat is a veto** until the anchor reissues the roster.
- Voters are `Mediated`. Promotion gains attribution and authorization properties, not safety
  properties.

---

## Open obligations

- **Clock model.** Vote timeout, now a mechanism constant, is the third mechanism, after leases and fencing, resting on
  the unstated clock model.
- **Independent evidence.** At least one grant seat reviews against a check not derived from the
  author's artifacts. Candidate ADR-0026 entry.
- **Seat diversity** across organization and role: the human analogue of the cross-family Observer
  rule.
- **Liveness assumption.** Weak fairness on `Vote` for grant seats. It is an assumption about
  people and must be declared as one.
- **Veto-seat outage.** An unavailable Observer vetoes nothing. Whether promotion should require
  Observer *liveness*, a syntactic heartbeat rather than a judgment, is open. It is admissible
  under ADR-0007.
