## Context

Erasure (ADR-0006) and break-glass (ADR-0009) are both ratified. Neither can be expressed as a
subtraction from the policy floor. Erasure is privileged, and break-glass adds authority. Both are
floor exceptions: verified-object carve-outs that change at deploy cadence. Every carve-out works
against ADR-0010's minimality, and minimality is what makes floor review trustworthy.

## Decision (skeleton)

### 1. One grant-authorization model

Every grant, including the two exceptions, is a quorum decision on an anchor-issued roster
(ADR-0025 §1b), bounded by the rules floor (ADR-0026). Erasure and break-glass differ only in
roster and envelope, not in authorization model.

### 2. Exceptions are declared floor entries

An exception is a named, versioned entry in the policy floor. Each entry carries:
- its action envelope;
- its roster reference;
- its non-composition obligations (§4).

Adding an exception is a floor relaxation, which requires an anchor issuance plus a promotion
(ADR-0026 §5).

### 3. Exception-count bound

The maximum number of exception entries is **ADR-0026 R6**. Current count: 2.

### 4. Non-composition properties

To be written as TLA+ invariants, each with a non-vacuity mutation.

| ID | Property | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **NC-1** | Break-glass chained with erasure never yields an effect that is authorized, erased, and unattributable | To draft |
| **NC-2** | Erasure never removes a record in a forensic-floor data class (ADR-0028), under any composition with break-glass | To draft; depends on 0028 |
| *(BG × promotion, BG × issuance)* | *Discharged by ADR-0026 R7; verified in the bootstrap spec* | Moved |

### 5. Open — Demand type 5

"Hide that a record ever existed" conflicts with NC-2, because the erasure event itself is in the
forensic floor. There are two possible outcomes:

- **Declare type 5 unsupported at the archetype.** This is the likely answer.
- **Admit it as a legibility degeneracy (ADR-0023)** that removes erasure events from the declared
  reader set, without destroying them. This mirrors SCI compartmentation.

Destroying the erasure record is not an available option under ADR-0028.

## Consequences (expected)

- **ADR-0006 and ADR-0009 debts discharge** when NC-1 and NC-2 are checked.
- **The exception mechanism reuses ratified machinery:** floor entries, rosters, and two-key
  relaxation. It adds no new primitive.
- **The record shrinks to its 09.12 scope,** which is reviewable at post-bootstrap promotion
  cadence.

## Open obligations

- Draft NC-1 and NC-2 in TLA+, each with a mutation that must fail.
- Resolve demand type 5 (§5).
- Confirm that R6 = 2 is not already at its bound for known future exceptions.
