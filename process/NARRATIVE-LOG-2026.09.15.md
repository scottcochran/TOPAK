### S6-2. D-0024-1 is not reachable from ADR-0024 revision 4

ADR-0024 §2 reads `quorum`, `minGrant`, and `recuse` from issued rules. The enabling condition is
`IF r.quorum = "all" THEN Granted = G ELSE Granted # {}`. `minGrant` bounds the number of **counted seats**, not the
number of **grants**. `RulesFloorNV.tla` encodes the same shape, with `quorum \in {"all", "any"}`.

So relaxing R1 does not yield k-of-n. It yields *any-of-n*: one grant from a roster of at least `minGrant` seats
promotes. That tolerates no Byzantine approver, which is D-0024-2's fault tolerance with a larger roster. No
issued-rules value expresses "k grants of n seats."

Three statements become false as a result:
- ADR-0024 Degeneracies: D-0024-1 is "a change to issued `quorum` and `minGrant` values."
- ADR-0026 §2: "A promotion degeneracy is a change to R1–R3, not to ADR-0024."
- Under ADR-0023 §4, D-0024-1 fails the admission test. By that record's own rule, this is a finding that the
  archetype mechanism is incomplete, not a rejection of k-of-n.

**Disposition (proposed).** Either add a grant-threshold parameter (`rules.minGrantVotes`, with R1 restated as
`minGrantVotes = |counted seats|`) or withdraw D-0024-1. Either way the mechanism text changes, so the 0026 §6
check must be rerun (0026 open obligations say so). A `k` parameter also needs its own floor entry, or R1 stays
the only guard and R2's empty-roster finding needs rechecking against it.

### S6-3. ADR-0022 hard-codes recusal, making R3 vacuous against the alphabet

ADR-0026 §6 finding 1: R1–R3 are vacuous if the mechanism hard-codes their values. ADR-0024 was revised to read
them from rules. ADR-0022 was not:
- §1, `Vote` envelope: "Not the artifact's author."
- §7: "the principal holding `Submit` and the principals holding `Vote` are disjoint."
- Consequences: "unanimous `Vote(grant)` from grant seats."

The action envelope is part of the verified mechanism (0007, 0010). If the envelope refuses an author's vote,
dropping R3 admits nothing new, and the `rev4` mutation that passed for R3 would hold vacuously once the envelope
is modeled. The 0026 §6 check modeled 0024's snippet only.

**Disposition (proposed).** Pick one:
- **Parametric.** The envelope reads `rules.recuse`; §7 and the Consequences bullet say "per issued rules." R3
  stays.
- **Constant.** Recusal is a mechanism constant, like "a timeout is never a grant." R3 is withdrawn as vacuous,
  and 0024 drops `recuse` from the rules. This matches the fact that neither D-0024 record relaxes R3.

The constant option is smaller and removes a parameter no degeneracy uses. Rerun the check either way.

### S6-4. Genesis rules are "checked at promotion" but genesis is never promoted

- ADR-0027 §2: genesis rules must satisfy the rules floor, "checked at the promotion of the release carrying them."
- ADR-0029 §5, ceremony step 4: the genesis bundle "is the only artifact never promoted."
- At epoch 0 there is no roster to promote under except the one the genesis rules define.

The check that actually exists is 0029 §5's `GenesisSatisfiesFloor`, in module `Genesis`.

**Disposition (proposed).** Revise 0027 §2: genesis rules satisfy the rules floor, discharged by
`GenesisSatisfiesFloor` at bootstrap (ADR-0029 §5); later mechanism releases carrying resident rules are checked
at their promotion.

### S6-5. Non-erasable votes are emitted onto erasable topics

- ADR-0024 §7: votes are principal emissions on voters' round topics.
- ADR-0024 §8: votes are non-erasable; review commentary is payload and erasable.
- ADR-0003.2: the sealed topic is the unit of erasure. Erasure drops the container.
- ADR-0028 §2: forensic classes, including F3 votes, are policy-floor denials on the erasure envelope.

A voter's round topic carrying both a vote and commentary is either erasable as a unit (F3 violated) or
permanently unerasable (0024 §8's commentary clause violated). ADR-0004 resolves the same shape for the archive by
keeping hash and pointer in the control-plane record and plaintext elsewhere, but 0024 does not invoke it.

**Disposition (proposed).** Revise 0024 §7–§8 to say where the authoritative vote record lives. The candidate
that fits the corpus: the vote is an admit decision in the consensus log (0015), which is what F3 retains; the
topic emission carries the vote and a hash-and-pointer to commentary in the 0004 store. 0028 F3 should cite the
same location.

### S6-6. ADR-0025 §1: "the floor" is ambiguous, and rules-floor changes are not in the rules set

§1 ends: "Tightening the floor is not a rules change. It is an ordinary promotion." That is true of the policy
floor and false of the rules floor, where tightening needs two keys (0026 §4). The rules set (a)–(d) also omits
rules-floor changes, although 0026 §4 requires an anchor issuance for them.

**Disposition (proposed).** Say "the policy floor" in §1, and add (e) "any change to the rules floor (ADR-0026 §4)."

### S6-7. ADR-0002 A1: the "(HITL)" review, and a rung-specific sentence

**"(HITL)"** (S3-1, carried). After A1, the Rationale says the promotion gate is a quorum decision by principals,
followed by "(HITL)." Grant seats are human `Mediated` principals on every rung (0024 §5; both D-0024 records keep
humans). Promotion remains human-in-the-loop. **Proposed: keep it.**

**"The anchor is at the gate."** A1's replacement text states this unconditionally. It holds only at archetype
custody (A-0025). Under D-0025-1 and D-0025-2 the key-holders are principals and the gate moves to the
genesis-declared rule (0025 annex). ADR-0002 is rung-agnostic. **Proposed:** "At archetype custody (A-0025), the
anchor's key-holders are at the gate."

### S6-9. `Resign` is undefined

ADR-0022 §8 says `Issue` and `Resign` belong to the anchor's alphabet (ADR-0025). ADR-0025 defines `Issue` only.
ADR-0002 A2 says "Resignation is not eviction" but no record says what resignation does to custody. Under A-0025,
a resignation is presumably a permanent loss, which freezes the rules.

**Disposition (proposed).** Either drop `Resign` from 0022 §8, or give each custody record in the 0025 annex a
resignation row. The second is more useful and costs nothing to proofs (custody is declared).

### S6-10. Headcount: four or five?

- ADR-0026 §6 finding 5: "The genesis headcount is confirmed by construction. Four humans."
- ADR-0029 Consequences: "at least 5 humans for an archetype bootstrap." G1 §9 carries "≥ 5."

Neither states its derivation. The 0026 model has **one** anchor key-holder, which is custody (1, 0), not A-0025,
and a human author outside the roster. The corpus supports 5 for the archetype: A-0025 requires n ≥ 3 key-holders,
R2 requires two counted grant seats, and R5 keeps the sets disjoint. The author need not be human; if the author
holds a seat, R3 raises the roster to three.

Also in 0026 §6: the heading "Two mechanism variants" introduces three.

**Disposition (proposed).** Reword 0026 §6 finding 5 as "the smallest satisfying model" and fix the heading
(in place; Proposed). Record the derivation of 5 in the bootstrap specification or the 0029 open obligations.

### S6-12. ADR-0015's authority-change set omits break-glass

0015 R1 defines authority changes as exactly {promotion, anchor issuance}. Break-glass adds authority (0009
acceptance; 0013 Context). R7 stops break-glass from authorizing `Issue`, `Vote`, or promotion, so it cannot
change *rules*, but it is still a temporary authority grant. It is neither of 0015's two effects unless its
invocation is an admit decision under an exception entry. Erasure has the same classification question.

This is the C2 exhaustiveness claim, so it has to be settled before 0015 is ratified.

**Disposition (proposed).** Classify break-glass and erasure invocations as admit decisions under their floor
exception entries (0013 §2), and say so in 0015. That keeps the authority-change set at two, with "rules change"
as the qualifier. Separately, 0015's "Lower profiles … pending the profile axis" is stale since 0023; revise in
place.

### S6-13. "Every grant is a quorum on an anchor-issued roster" overreaches

ADR-0013 §1 and ADR-0025's scope carried into 0013 both say every grant is a roster quorum. ADR-0015 says
admissions are grants, and ordinary Run admits are consensus-ordered decisions against a bundle, not roster
quorums.

**Disposition (proposed).** Scope the sentence to privileged grants: promotion, break-glass, erasure. 0013 is a
Draft (queue F). 0025's carried-scope wording is not decision text, but fix it with S6-6 so B carries no
overstatement.

### S6-14. The forensic horizon ends at re-genesis

ADR-0028 §3 sets the archetype horizon to "the lifetime of the rules-epoch chain." ADR-0027 §4 starts a new chain
at re-genesis. Re-genesis follows anchor loss or capture, which is exactly when the authority record matters most.

**Disposition (proposed).** Carry into ADR-0025's re-genesis migration obligation: a successor inherits read
access to its predecessor's forensic classes. Settle before 0028's confirmation promotion.

### S6-15. Veto seats cite the Observer scoping that 0023 reverses

ADR-0024 §5 assigns veto seats to "the independent Observer replicated group (ADR-0008)," the shared scoping.
ADR-0023 reverses that scoping in effect toward per-principal Observers plus a correlator. 0024 is a bootstrap
record and cannot be confirmation-required; the 0008 supersession will be. Changing who holds veto seats after
bootstrap therefore reaches a bootstrap record through an ordinary promotion.

**Disposition (proposed).** Carry into the 0008 supersession's scope: it names the veto-seat holder. The
correlator is the natural candidate, since veto needs cross-principal visibility. No change to 0024 now.

### S6-16. "The one artifact verified rather than promoted"

ADR-0010 and ADR-0012 Consequences both say the policy floor is the one artifact verified rather than promoted,
and that Build's regress terminates on it. Since then:
- floor changes ship in promoted mechanism releases (0024 §1; 0026 §5);
- the genesis bundle is "the only artifact never promoted" (0029 §5);
- the regress ends at ADR-0025's intent axiom, bounded by two verified floors.

Both statements are in Consequences, not decision text. ADR-0012's decision says the floor is verified "rather
than promoted **as a parameter**," which stays true. Under the amendment test (amend only where decision text
becomes false), nothing needs amending.

**Disposition.** None required. The ToO narrates the resolution (rev 5 §3, §7).

### S6-17. Which rules are resident?

ADR-0027 §1 makes *genesis* rules resident. §3 says the system "runs on the last resident rules at the current
rules epoch," which assumes rules issued after epoch 0 also become resident. No record says so.

**Disposition (proposed).** Add to 0027: an admitted issuance is persisted with the same residency as genesis rules.
