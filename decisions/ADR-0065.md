# ADR-0065 — Stacks report to each other on topics promoted with the mesh: a send is permitted and never ordered, receipt is labelled ingress, and nothing a peer originates enables anything

**Status:** Proposed · 2026.11.10
**Status note:** Confirmation required under **C2**: Run's ingress is a closed list and its effects are enumerated,
and this record adds an item to the first and an unordered emission to the second. **C3** does not apply while this
is an overlay ⟨Q11⟩; ADR-0055, whose §7 it changes, is Proposed. C1 and C4 do not apply. **Nothing here is modelled,
checked or built.**
**Pending:** none.
**Deployment position:** **An overlay, not the archetype** ⟨Q11⟩. One degeneracy (§7).
**Depends on:** ADR-0055 (the mesh, and "the mechanism is the bus"; Proposed), ADR-0062 (the governor, which holds
the radio and permits), ADR-0047 (labels, `clearSet`, clear and handle), ADR-0043 (the Listener's closed list),
ADR-0058 (overlays; Proposed)
**Relates to:** ADR-0015 (an emission is an effect), ADR-0020 (recorded and not ordered), ADR-0040 (`Report`),
ADR-0054 §6 and ADR-0035 (lineage alternation), ADR-0063 (what is charged)
**Closes:** nothing.
**Carries a debt against:** nothing while this is an overlay. §8 lists what the archetype's records would owe.

---

## Context

"Vehicles coordinate through their plane, and only that way", the proposal's first revision said, after ADR-0055:
"the mechanism is the bus". The swarm reading found what that leaves out (Z1): one path for information between
vehicles, a log ordered by Byzantine agreement, and no rate for it.

The custodian asked whether stacks may talk in the autonomous layer: no admit, deny only, coordination within the
rules of engagement ⟨15⟩. **The drafter's answer was yes, with one correction: a send still needs a permit. What it
does not need is an order.** The corpus already separates the two: "Ordering is not authorization" (ToO §4.1). The
custodian took both halves ⟨16⟩ ⟨17⟩.

---

## Decision

### 1. Nothing a peer originates is ever an enabling condition ⟨15⟩

For every permit and every admit, at a plane or a governor, the same request with every item received from peers
removed is still permitted. This is ADR-0047's direction, "delivering more in the clear buys a narrower round" (ToO
§2.10), applied between vehicles.

**Three kinds of traffic share the governors' radios.**

| Traffic | What the receiver does | Why it is safe |
|---|---|---|
| **Certified facts, relayed**: fences, revocations, ticks, intents, leases, watermarks | Its governor verifies the certificate against the chain and applies it. A subtraction applies on receipt, before it is ordered anywhere | The peer is transport and originates nothing. A fence reaches any vehicle in range of one that heard it |
| **A governor's measured facts**: its position, a range | The receiving governor may refuse on them and never permits on them | A captured governor that lies can only stop others |
| **A stack's report**: tracks, plans, bids for a task | Its governor delivers it to its stack as labelled ingress | The stack is Byzantine already, and every effect it then requests is checked without reference to the report |

### 2. Sending is an effect, permitted and not ordered ⟨16⟩

ADR-0015 counts an emission as an effect, and a transmission reveals the vehicle and can carry `restricted` content.

- **A send is a `Report` to a declared topic, permitted by the governor** under the floor and the bundles. The rules
  of engagement can refuse it: silence, a rate, a label.
- **It is not ordered, and it is not charged to `K_up`** (ADR-0063), or chatter would spend the plane's authority.
  It has a count of its own per lease.
- **It is recorded by digest** on the vehicle's chain (ADR-0062 §6), as ADR-0020 records a denial.

### 3. Receiving is ingress, labelled `untrusted` and by its topic ⟨Q19⟩

The corpus makes a read of live state an `Actuate` "because the request itself leaves the membrane" (ToO §2.9). Here
no request leaves: the governor delivers what arrives on the topics its seat is declared to hear. It is a sixth
item on the Listener's list, after ADR-0054's `input`.

- **A delivered report carries `untrusted`, always, and a name the mesh declares for its topic** ⟨Q19⟩. The first
  puts it under the floor's label forbids. The second lets a mission's bundle refuse particular entries after
  particular topics.
- **A sender's governor may add names and cannot remove any.** A captured one may lie, and a label that can only be
  raised makes the lie harmless.
- **Delivered clear, it joins `clearSet`**, so `R5` to `R7` follow it. **Listening narrows.** A bundle may deliver a
  topic as a handle to a seat kind that should route it and not read it.

### 4. Topics are promoted with the mesh, and nobody is addressed ⟨17⟩

The mesh declares each topic: its schema, which seat kinds may report to it, which nodes hear it, its label and its
delivery mode. **A stack reports to a topic and names no recipient.** So "Workers never address each other"
(ADR-0055 §7) stays true.

**This is not the choreography ADR-0055 rejected.** Its objection is: "Every reading in which cells react to each
other directly puts a model's output in charge of what runs next." Here the mechanism still decides what runs next:
no guard reads a peer's report, and no report causes a lease. A stack chooses among entries its lease already
permits, which is the orchestrator's position: which, never whether. What changes in ADR-0055 §7 is one sentence,
"No variant publishes to another cell or subscribes to anything."

### 5. Nothing may rest on a report being read

An order before an effect, and an Observer coupled to it, exist so that what the effect can cause is bounded before
it lands. A report can cause nothing: §1 says so of the receiving side, and the governor's permit bounds the
transmission itself. So a send needs neither. An Observer reads the record afterwards, and its finding subtracts.

### 6. Talk settles nothing, and agreement is an ordered entry ⟨21⟩

Vehicles coordinate in two ways. **By talk**, on topics: it waits for no round of the plane, and two stacks may
claim the same task. **By order**, through an entry the bundle marks `ordered` (ADR-0062 §3): one round, and every
vehicle sees the same outcome. Where the tally wants one vehicle and no more, only the second will do. A vehicle
that is alone coordinates with nobody, and its beacon tells its peers where it is.

### 7. Degeneracy on this record

**D-0065-1: one lineage across a clear topic.** ADR-0054 §6 wants independent Thinkers at the two ends of a clear
edge, and every deployment today declares that it has them (D-0054-1). Vehicles of one build have one lineage and
cannot declare it of a topic delivered clear. Such a deployment delivers peer reports as handles, or declares this.

### 8. Overlay position, and what is reported upstream ⟨Q11⟩

Reported under ADR-0058 §6: **an emission that is permitted and not ordered**, and **ingress that arrives
unasked**.

**If the archetype takes them, its records owe:** ADR-0055 §7, the one sentence; ADR-0015, an emission with a
permit and no ordered admit; ADR-0040, a `Report` to a topic that is not the principal's own round topic; ADR-0043,
a sixth item of ingress; ADR-0047, its label.

### 9. Ratification condition

- **ADR-0055 is ratified first**, and ADR-0062 first or with this record.
- **A module**, `Peer`, states at least `PeerAddsNothing`, `ReceivedIsUntrusted` and `SendWithinTopics`, with
  `M_PEERENABLES`, `M_SENDERLABEL` and `M_ANYTOPIC`. **Not met.**
- **The floor** carries `FD12-topic-declared`, and is shown monotone in what was received from peers, as the label
  forbids are in `clearSet` (ADR-0067).
- **The reference** shows a report delivered clear and one as a handle; an entry refused after a clear report that
  is permitted without it; and a send refused by a bundle. **Not met.**
- **A reader who is not the drafter** (ADR-0029 A2).

## Options considered

| Option | Disposition |
|---|---|
| **The plane's log as the only path**, the first revision | Not taken ⟨15⟩. It has no rate for what vehicles tell each other |
| **A send with no permit** | Not taken ⟨16⟩. It is a sideways effect path, which falsifies ADR-0015's enumeration |
| **A send ordered by the plane** | Rejected. A round per message, for an effect that can enable nothing |
| **Free addressing** | Not taken ⟨17⟩. A stack would name another, which the corpus excludes for every principal |
| **A report that can enable**, a trust score that admits | Rejected. It is what ADR-0007 prohibits (the swarm reading, §2.2) |
| **`untrusted` alone as the label** | Not taken ⟨Q19⟩. A bundle could not tell one topic from another |
| **The sender's label taken as given** | Rejected. `M_SENDERLABEL` |

## Consequences

**Positive**
- **A path for what vehicles tell each other**, at whatever rate the radios carry.
- **A fence travels by relay**, to any vehicle in range of one that heard it.
- **No new authority.** The property is that removing every peer's item changes no permit.

**Negative**
- **Talk settles nothing.** A stack that claims everything stalls its peers.
- **The tally votes the joint worst case.** Stacks that can talk can act together inside their envelopes. That was
  already the premise, since stacks of one lineage may fail alike, and ADR-0063's bound is what holds it.
- **A finding has further to travel.** In a mesh, "a finding against any activation in the history closes every
  edge downstream" (ToO §2.11). A report read off the edges is not on that history. The delivery records say who
  read what, and they reach the plane only at the seal.
- **Listening narrows**, so a mission's rules decide how much a stack that listens may still do.
- **One author, one day.**
