# ADR-0005 — Kafka is neither the ordering authority nor the system of record

**Status:** Accepted (genesis) · 2026.10.04 · confirmation required (ADR-0029 C4) ⟨B27⟩
**Status note:** Held at the 09.12 review with deployment profiles; clean against the archetype, pending the Observer quarantine-gate question.
**Ratified at R2, in Batch 27, alone**, by the genesis custodian (ADR-0029 §2), with §7's condition met.
**R1 (2026.10.04)** answers the hold: the ordering residual that question was to close has since been closed by a
sequence number the log assigns (§2), so the question no longer gates this record and stays open on its own account
(Open obligations). Confirmation required under **C4**: ratification discharges the debt
ADR-0033 §2's K row carries against this record, and ADR-0015's open item 1 as it bears on ordering. C1 to C3 do
not apply: no privileged path, no confinement or exhaustiveness claim, and nothing accepted is reversed; R1 brings
the text to what ADR-0015, ADR-0032, ADR-0039 and ADR-0042 already say.
**Pending:** none.
**Deployment position:** Archetype. A deployment that reads order from the transport, or that keeps the transport as
its record, is a degeneracy of its own declaration (ADR-0023); none is declared here.
**Depends on:** ADR-0003 (a topic per principal per round), ADR-0004 (the archive), ADR-0015 (effects are admits; the
frame is the log)
**Relates to:** ADR-0039 §1 and ADR-0042 §2 (the sequence number), ADR-0032 (no timing from causality), ADR-0033 §2
(the reference's K row), ADR-0045 T-0045-5 (the transport, delegated) and T-0045-7 (the archive), ADR-0020 (denials
on the topic)
**Closes:** the debt in ADR-0033's status note (ADR-0033 B27); G9 open item 5, at the next checkpoint.

**Context.** With one topic per round, within-partition ordering no longer yields a global
timeline. With per-use-case retention and archive-as-ETL, the log is not durable in the sense
"system of record" implies.

**Decision.** The causal frame lives in ~~Paxos plus a vector clock~~ the Paxos log ⟨R1⟩. The archive is the system of
record. Kafka's guarantee is durability in the window (acks=all), not ordering across the
system.

**Consequences.** Changes what the K in TOPAK means and must be stated explicitly in the
component article rather than discovered by a reader. ~~Requires a vector clock design that does
not yet exist. Trace conformance must be shown to work against reconstructed rather than
native ordering.~~ ⟨R1⟩ *No vector clock is required, and conformance is checked over admits (§2, §4).*

*The four sections below are R1's. They state the decision above in the terms the records written since 09.12 use.*

### 1. Three roles, three holders ⟨R1⟩

| Role | Holder | Not the holder |
|---|---|---|
| **Ordering authority** | The Paxos log. An effect is an admit (ADR-0015), and an admit has one position in the log | The transport. A topic's offsets order one principal's round and nothing across rounds or principals (ADR-0003) |
| **System of record** | The archive (ADR-0004), with its governed plaintext store and the forensic horizon | The transport. Retention is per use case and a round's topic is the unit of erasure, so what it holds is not durable in the sense a record needs |
| **Transport** | Kafka, at the reference: durability within the window (`acks=all`), delivery to the readers in `W(p)` | — |

The K in TOPAK names the transport role. A substrate that gives durability in a window and per-topic delivery can
hold it; ordering and record-keeping are not asked of it (ADR-0045 T-0045-5, delegated).

### 2. What orders what ⟨R1⟩

**No vector clock is designed, and none is owed.** The 09.12 text asked for one to recover a causal frame the
transport no longer gave. ADR-0015 put admitted effects in the log, which orders them natively, and reduced the
need to one residual: effects admitted in one round and emitted at different times. That residual is closed by a
number the log assigns.

| What | Ordered by | Record |
|---|---|---|
| Admits, across the system | The log index | ADR-0015 |
| A principal's effects within a round | The sequence number, assigned when the admit is applied, so every replica assigns the same one | ADR-0042 §2 for Run; ADR-0039 §1 |
| An emission on its topic | The log index and sequence number it carries. It is written only after its admit commits, so the topic never holds an emission the log does not order | ADR-0033 §2, K row |
| A denial on a topic | **Nothing.** It is written by the deciding replica, unordered, while the topic is open and to the `after` companion once it is sealed | ADR-0020; ADR-0003 A2 |
| Intervals and deadlines | Not the frame at all: causality orders events and bounds no interval | ADR-0032 |

- **A reader orders a topic by the numbers its records carry, not by their offsets.** Offsets are the transport's and
  are evidence of arrival only.
- **Denials are the remaining gap.** A sequence number on denials is open (G9 item 44), and the correlator's first
  uses want one (ADR-0051, Proposed). It is a gap in what is recorded about refusals, not in the order of effects.

### 3. The quarantine gate no longer gates this record ⟨R1⟩

ADR-0015's open item 1 asked whether the Observer's quarantine gate is a modeled event, because if it were, emission
order could be recovered from the gate sequence, and it advised settling that before this record was ratified. The
order it was to recover is now given by §2's sequence number whichever way the question falls. **The question is
still open** (ADR-0039 finding 1; G9 item 48), for the reasons ADR-0039 gives: what a hold means when coverage
already stalls a Thinker. It is not an ordering question any more.

### 4. Conformance is over admits ⟨R1⟩

Trace conformance does not reconstruct an order from the transport. It replays admit events, which are the
specification's own actions, against `Mechanism.tla` (ADR-0033 A5; ADR-0045 T-0045-28). ADR-0015 said this reduces
the 09.12 obligation and does not discharge it. What is left of it is T-0045-28's: the harness covers tested paths
only, and stages 2 and 3 of the refinement are owed. No part of that debt is about the transport's ordering, so
this record carries none of it.

### 5. What this record leaves to others ⟨R1⟩

- **The archive does not exist at the reference** (`mechanism/` G9; T-0045-7). The archetype names the system of
  record; the reference has a per-replica projection of the log and declares so.
- **Payloads live where they were written.** Workspaces and plaintext are not replicated at the reference, which is
  the archive's gap and not the transport's.
- **Which substrate.** The reference's choice of Kafka through `rskafka` is ADR-0033 §2's, under that record's
  substitution rule.

### 6. Open obligations ⟨R1⟩

- **The quarantine hold** (§3), with ADR-0039.
- **Sequence numbers on denials** (§2; G9 item 44).
- **The component article** states what the K means, as the 09.12 consequence asked. The Theory of Operation §5
  already does; the article is the next checkpoint's.
- ~~**On ratification, dependent entries:** ADR-0033's status note and its K row drop "Proposed" and the debt; ADR-0045
  T-0045-5 drops "Proposed"; ADR-0015's open item 1 gains a pointer to §3.~~ Applied with this record's ratification
  (Batch 27) ⟨B27⟩.
- **The narrative.** The Theory of Operation §5 still says the quarantine-gate question gates this record and that
  it is Proposed. It is frozen between checkpoints and takes this record's state at the next one.

### 7. Ratification condition ⟨R2⟩

1. **The dependent entries are drafted** in ADR-0033 (status note, Relates to, the debt line, the K row), ADR-0045
   (T-0045-5) and ADR-0015 (open item 1), to be applied with ratification.
2. **No record in force requires a vector clock.** A search of the records finds the term in this record's struck
   text, in ADR-0015, which reduces it to the residual §2 closes, and in ADR-0032, which rejects it as a source of
   timing.
3. **§2's two claims about the topic are seen at the reference**: an admitted emission carries the sequence number
   the log assigned, and a denial carries neither a log index nor a sequence number.

**Met at ratification** ⟨B27⟩: (1) applied as ADR-0033 B27, ADR-0045 B27 and ADR-0015 B27. (2) as stated, on the tree
at ratification. (3) `mechanism/` `topak-node` `tests/e2e.rs` `build_round_promotion_and_fencing`, run 2026.10.04
and passing: the first admitted `Emit` returns `seq` 1, and every `Deny` record on the topic has neither `admit` nor
`seq` ("a denial is never ordered"). The reference's transport writes the sequence number as the `topak-seq` header
(`topak-transport`).

## 09.12 review notes

- **The deployment-profile idea reframes it.** As written it reads as a concession extracted by
  0003. Under the archetype framing it's a positive statement — at the archetype, Kafka is
  deliberately a transport with a durability window, and the archive is the record. Lower
  profiles may subtract toward native-log ordering.
- **ADR-0004's archive scoping strengthened it.** The archive being the system of record is no
  longer just a consequence of retention; it's where the governed plaintext store and the
  conformance material both live.
