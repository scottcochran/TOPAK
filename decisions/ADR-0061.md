# ADR-0061 — An autonomous plane is seven replicas under Byzantine agreement with signed certificates, and its membership shrinks only along a schedule its charter voted, down to a capture floor

**Status:** Proposed · 2026.11.10
**Status note:** Confirmation required under **C1**: who may form a certificate is who may grant, and §3 changes it
at run time. **C3** does not apply while this is an overlay ⟨Q11⟩ and would apply on admission: ADR-0045 declined to
require Byzantine agreement, and §3 strains ADR-0024's constant that a timeout is never a grant. **Modelled, checked and
proved in TLA+ at five replicas ⟨R1⟩ (§7); PBFT is not read and nothing is built.**
**Pending:** none.
**Deployment position:** **An overlay, not the archetype** ⟨Q11⟩. "The archetype" in §1 means the size this set of
records is written at. One degeneracy (§5).
**Depends on:** ADR-0059 §5 (commit certificates; Byzantine agreement at an exposed plane), ADR-0060 §3 (the
autonomous plane, its replicas on the governors), ADR-0058 (overlays; Proposed)
**Relates to:** ADR-0024 (a timeout is never a grant), ADR-0045 (the replica row), ADR-0064 (the plane's clock, whose
tolerance is §1's last column), ADR-0062 (a vehicle that hears no plane)
**Closes:** nothing.
**Carries a debt against:** nothing while this is an overlay. §6 lists what the archetype's records would owe.

## Context

The custodian asked whether the quorum is PBFT at 3f + 1 with f = 2 for the archetype ⟨20⟩, and took a shrink
schedule with a capture floor, which the drafter had put as a real trade ⟨19⟩.

**A plane on vehicles loses replicas for good.** With a fixed membership, one loss beyond what the plane tolerates
and no certificate can ever form again. That is the NATO reading's N3. ADR-0062 makes it sharper: every survivor
then loses its lease at once.

---

## Decision

### 1. Seven replicas, tolerating two captured ⟨20⟩

A certificate for n replicas tolerating f captured is the least whole number above (n + f) / 2, so that any two
certificates share an honest signer.

| Replicas | Captured tolerated (f) | Certificate | Silent tolerated | Captured or slow tolerated, for the duration bound (ADR-0064) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | 2 | 5 | 2 | 4 |
| 6 | 1 | 4 | 2 | 3 |
| 5 | 1 | 4 | 1 | 3 |
| 4 | 1 | 3 | 1 | 2 |

**Why seven and not four.** Four replicas tolerate one capture or one loss, and have no smaller membership to fall
back to that tolerates any capture. Seven tolerate two of either at once, and have three steps of the schedule
above a floor of one. **The number is a placeholder**, as every number in the corpus is until something is measured.

### 2. The instrument is PBFT at the reference, on two conditions ⟨20⟩

The letters name roles, not products (ToO §1). What the pattern asks of P at an exposed plane is Byzantine
agreement whose certificates a third party can check (ADR-0059 §5). PBFT (Castro and Liskov, 1999) gives agreement
at n = 3f + 1 with certificates of 2f + 1 *(recalled, not re-read)*.

- **Its messages must be signed.** A governor verifies a certificate with no plane in reach. A later form of PBFT
  replaced signatures with message authentication codes, which convince only the replica they were made for
  *(recalled)*. That form cannot supply a commit certificate.
- **It needs a reconfiguration step.** PBFT as published has a fixed membership *(recalled)*. §3 adds a step that
  changes it, and that step is where an error would be an error about authority.

Three things fit as they are. ADR-0064's rules are conditions on a replica's vote. The view change runs on timeouts,
which is liveness. A Byzantine primary chooses the order and can stall; it cannot forge, so it decides which and
never whether.

### 3. Membership shrinks along a schedule voted in the charter ⟨19⟩

The charter gains `membership`: the smaller memberships the plane may fall back to, in order, and a **capture
floor**, the least number of captured replicas it must still tolerate.

- **A step drops one replica.** Any replica may propose to drop one it has not heard for a declared number of ticks
  (ADR-0064). A replica signs the proposal only if it has not heard that replica either.
- **The membership being replaced orders the step**, by its own certificate. So a membership has at most one
  successor, and a minority cut off by a partition cannot make itself a plane.
- **An honest replica signs at most one step of a membership** ⟨R1⟩. The first rule does not give this: a replica
  that has heard neither of two others could sign both drops, and two successors follow (`M_TWOVOTES`). §2's
  agreement gives it, one entry at one log index, with a step that names the membership it replaces. Read as one
  vote per membership with no second ballot, a vote that splits stalls the schedule for good, which is safe and is
  not live (Open obligations).
- **A dropped replica does not return below the intent boundary.** Adding a replica brings an issuer into force,
  which is a promotion (ADR-0059 §3). Its vehicle may still hold a lease if it hears the plane again *(drafter's
  choice)*.
- **No step takes the plane below the floor**, and no other change of membership is allowed (`FD13`, `FD4`).

### 4. The floor is one at the archetype, and a smaller membership may carry a smaller budget ⟨Q18⟩ ⟨Q17⟩

**The schedule is 7, 6, 5, 4 and the capture floor is one** ⟨Q18⟩. At this schedule the floor adds nothing the
schedule does not ⟨R1⟩: four is the last size that tolerates one capture. The floor binds a charter whose schedule
runs past it.

| | Fixed at 7 | Schedule 7, 6, 5, 4 |
|---|---|---|
| Losses survived, one at a time | 2 | 4 |
| Losses survived at once | 2 | 2 |
| Captures tolerated before any loss | 2 | 2 |
| Captures tolerated after one loss | 2 | 1 |

**A charter may give each membership its own budget** ⟨Q17⟩. Where the tally states one, a step narrows what the
plane holds as well as lowering what it takes to capture it. Where it states none, the budget is unchanged.

**What a step spends.** This is the quorum dial of the lattice analysis: capture is how many captured holders it
takes to grant, and loss is how many silent holders still let a grant happen. That analysis draws the dial for
grant seats and keeps consensus quorums out of it, because at a commanded plane consensus is crash-fault. At an
autonomous plane the replicas are exposed and nobody else grants, so the pair applies to them. A step spends the
first to restore the second.

### 5. Degeneracy on this record

**D-0061-1: a plane of four.** It keeps agreement and tolerates one capture. It has no schedule, and one loss
beyond the first ends it.

### 6. Overlay position, and what is reported upstream ⟨Q11⟩

Reported under ADR-0058 §6: **a membership that changes at run time by a voted schedule.**

**If the archetype takes it, its records owe:** ADR-0045, the replica row for an exposed plane and the
reconfiguration step with it, which is C3; ADR-0024, a statement of how §3 stands beside "a timeout is never a
grant" (the first Negative below).

### 7. Ratification condition

- **ADR-0059 and ADR-0060 are ratified first**, or with this record.
- **A module** states at least `OneSuccessor`: every step is in the charter's schedule, at or above its floor, and
  ordered by a quorum of the membership it replaces. Mutations `M_MINORITYSTEP` and `M_BELOWFLOOR`.
  **Met for TLC and TLAPS at five replicas, and not at §1's seven** ⟨R1⟩: `spec/Membership.tla`

## Options considered

| Option | Disposition |
|---|---|
| **Four replicas** | A declared degeneracy (§5). No room to shrink |
| **A fixed membership of seven** | Not taken ⟨19⟩. It survives two losses and then ends, in the deployment where losses are ordinary |
| **A capture floor of two** | Not taken at the archetype ⟨Q18⟩. A plane of seven then has no schedule; one needs ten or more. A deployment that faces an adversary who can silence vehicles takes it |
| **A budget that always shrinks with the membership**, by a fixed rule | Not taken ⟨Q17⟩. The tally states one where it can |
| **A smaller membership adopted by the survivors on a timeout** | Rejected. Loss and partition look the same from inside, and two groups could each admit |
| **Replicas re-admitted in the field** | Rejected. It adds an issuer, which is a promotion |
| **Message authentication codes in place of signatures** | Rejected (§2) |

## Consequences

**Positive**
- **A plane outlives its first losses**: four, one at a time, where a fixed seven survives two.
- **A partition still leaves at most one plane.**
- **The instrument is named**, with what it lacks.

**Negative**
- **It strains a mechanism constant.** "A timeout is never a grant" (ADR-0024). A step adds nothing to the charter:
  no entry, no budget, no longer life. But silence is what triggers it, and what it preserves is a plane's ability
  to grant. This record accepts that because the schedule and the floor were voted, because the step is ordered by
  a quorum and not taken by default, and because the alternative is the Context's second paragraph.
- **Against an adversary that can silence vehicles, the tolerance is the floor's from the start.** Jamming honest
  vehicles until their replicas are dropped walks the plane down its schedule. Two captured replicas then capture a
  plane that tolerated two when it was seated.
- **A vote that splits can stall the schedule** (§3) ⟨R1⟩. The plane keeps its size, and a further loss may end it.
- **A reconfiguration step no published PBFT has**, in the trusted base.
- **Cost unmeasured.** PBFT's messages grow with the square of the membership.
- **One author, one day, and an instrument described from memory.**

## Open obligations

- **The reading of PBFT, the measurement and the reference** (§7).
- **Whether a split vote may be retried** (§3) ⟨R1⟩. `Membership.tla` has no ballot and no view change, so a retry
  that keeps `AtMostOneSuccessor` is neither designed nor checked. Whether PBFT's view change supplies one waits on
  the reading of PBFT.
- **Where an autonomous plane's orchestrator runs.** If it runs on one vehicle, that vehicle's loss stalls every
  decision point (the NATO reading, N3).
- **The number of silent ticks before a replica may be dropped**, as a placeholder first.
- **Whether a dropped replica's vehicle may hold a lease** (§3), which is the drafter's choice.
