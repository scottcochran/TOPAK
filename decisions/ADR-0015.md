# ADR-0015 — Effects are boundary crossings; every crossing is authorized, ordered, and auditable

**Status:** Accepted (genesis) · 2026.09.23 · confirmation required (ADR-0029 C2) ⟨B3⟩ ⟨B27⟩
**Status note:** Confirmation required under C2: both sets are claimed exhaustive, and the effect
set's exhaustiveness is unenforced while the sandbox gap stands (open item 2). The classification
of break-glass and erasure rests on ADR-0013 §2, ratified in Batch 4 the same day. Until confirmed,
anything published citing this record's guarantees says "author-ratified".
**Pending:** **A1**, drafted 2026.10.08, dependent on ADR-0054 (Proposed): `Route` is a principal emission (Decision), marked inline and not applied. E1 ratified 2026.09.25 (Batch 8).
**Deployment position:** Archetype-scoped.

---

**Context.** ADR-0005 leaves the causal frame to Paxos plus an undesigned vector clock, and leaves
trace conformance against reconstructed ordering unproven. Separately, effect exclusivity — every
externally visible effect is mediated by the typed action interface — has been carried as an
assumption in the trusted base, following Moon & Varshney, who assume rather than prove it.

Both soften once effects are enumerated rather than assumed. An agent effect is a tool invocation or
a principal emission. Nothing else crosses the boundary. Each is a typed action under ADR-0007's
alphabet, evaluated at admit against a floor-refining bundle (ADR-0010), and signed. Admissions are
grants, and grants are consensus-ordered.

ADR-0025 introduces the trust anchor, whose issuances change authority without being principal
actions. An effect enumeration covering only principals would leave an authority path unlisted,
and exhaustiveness is this record's load-bearing claim. ⟨R1⟩

**Decision.** ~~Define the effect set as exactly {tool invocation, principal emission}. Treat the
**admit decision**, not the emission, as the orderable event.~~ Define two exhaustive sets. ⟨R1⟩

- **Effects:** exactly {tool invocation, principal emission}. For each, the **admit decision**,
  not the emission, is the orderable event.
- **Authority changes:** exactly {promotion, anchor issuance}.
  - A promotion is an admit decision under ADR-0024. Its votes are principal emissions, so it is
    already covered.
  - An anchor issuance is an **environment event** under ADR-0025. It is validated by
    `Authentic` and the rules floor (ADR-0026) rather than by a bundle. It is consensus-ordered.
  - **Break-glass and erasure are not a third kind** ⟨R2⟩. Each is invoked as an admit decision
    against its declared floor-exception entry (ADR-0013 §2), so both are already effects under the
    set above. Break-glass grants authority *within* the rules in force and cannot alter them:
    ADR-0026 R7 bars a break-glass grant from authorizing `Issue`, `Vote`, or promotion. The
    qualifier that keeps the authority-change set at two is therefore **change to the rules in
    force**, not "adds authority" (S6-12).

*Pending ⟨A1⟩, not applied. **`Route` is not a third kind either.** An orchestrator's `Route(edge)` (ADR-0054 §3,
Proposed) is a typed record on its own round topic, admitted, ordered and re-checked at apply, so it is a principal
emission. What follows from it, the lease of the node the edge enters, is granted under the authority of the promotion
that seated the workflow (ADR-0054 §2), and that promotion is the authority change. `Route` chooses among edges the
mechanism has already enabled and adds nothing the quorum did not vote on, so both sets stay at two. A reviewer may
read a choice whose consequence is a grant as a third kind (ADR-0054 finding 3); that is this record's C2 question.*

Every effect therefore has a
consensus-ordered, signed, authorizing predecessor in the Paxos log, and the causal frame is read
from that log rather than reconstructed from transport metadata. Every authorization is traceable
to a consensus-ordered issuance that defined the rules in force. ⟨R1⟩

Denials are not ordered and do not need to be: a denied effect never crosses the boundary, and
absence of effect is not an event.

How ordering is carried out is ADR-0033 §4: an admit is decided, then ordered, then re-checked at
apply and void if the mechanism state it relied on has moved; and a subtraction that changes
replicated state is ordered though not authorized. ⟨E1⟩

**Consequences.**

- Ordering for admitted effects exists natively. The vector clock reduces from a required design to
  a residual for one case: effects admitted in the same round and emitted at different times, where
  the control plane never observed the sequence. A per-principal sequence number may suffice; that
  residual is smaller and cheaper than a general causal frame.
- Trace conformance operates over admit events, which are already the abstract state machine's
  actions. This is closer to the specification's own model than partition offsets ever were, and it
  materially reduces the ADR-0005 conformance obligation — though it does not discharge it.
- **Effect exclusivity becomes a mechanism claim rather than a trusted-base assumption**, conditional
  on the effect set being exhaustive. Once the confinement obligation (ADR-0022 §6)
  is discharged, that position is stronger than Moon & Varshney's and the sharpest differentiation
  available; until then the two are identical in strength ⟨E2⟩. It is entirely contingent on the
  no-sandbox gap: Kafka-as-sole-channel is asserted, not enforced, and any sideways effect path falsifies the
  enumeration rather than degrading it.
- The enumeration is now a load-bearing claim that must be defended, not stated. Every capability
  given to an agent must be shown to reduce to one of the two forms.
- **The audit chain terminates at a named axiom.** Effect → admit → bundle ⊑ floor → rules
  epoch → issuance → ADR-0025's intent axiom. No link is assumed except the last. ⟨R1⟩
- **Exhaustiveness now has two claims to defend.** A new principal capability must reduce to one
  of the two effects. A new way of changing authority must reduce to one of the two authority
  changes. ⟨R1⟩
- **The privileged paths are classified against an unratified record** ⟨R2⟩. Break-glass and erasure
  are effects only because their exception entries exist as declared floor entries (ADR-0013 §2).
  ADR-0013 is Proposed and, since 2026.09.23, ratifiable in genesis with a confirmation-required
  marker — so this record's C2 exhaustiveness claim is conditional on a record that can now be
  ratified alongside it, rather than on one held until after bootstrap. Both carry the marker, so
  both are reviewed by the first quorum.

**Open, and directly downstream**

1. **Is the Observer's quarantine gate a modeled event or an internal step?** If modeled, emission
   order is recoverable from the gate sequence and the admit-to-emit residual closes entirely —
   removing the vector clock from the open list. If internal, promoting it is the cheapest available
   fix. This should be settled before ADR-0005 is ratified. *ADR-0005 was ratified 2026.10.04 (Batch 27) with the
   question open. Its §2 and §3 say why: the residual this item was to close is closed by the sequence number the
   log assigns (ADR-0042 §2), whichever way the question falls. The question stays open as ADR-0039's, about what a
   hold means* ⟨B27⟩.
2. **Sandbox mechanism.** The enumeration's exhaustiveness is unenforced. This ADR raises the value
   of closing that gap from "tidiness" to "the difference between a proof and an assertion."
3. **Lower profiles.** Archetype-scoped. The profile axis is no longer open: ADR-0023 settles the
   variation relation, so what a reduced profile may subtract here is a degeneracy declaration on a
   named parameter, reachable from the archetype by parameter limit and admissible only under
   ADR-0023 §4. ⟨R2⟩ Which parameters this record exposes is not enumerated, and that enumeration is
   the open item (S6-12).
