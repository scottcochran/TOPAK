## Context

The corpus has said since G3 §8 that its TLA+ results are evidence and not proofs: TLC at small constants shows that
a property holds over every reachable state of a model at those constants, that each mutation of the mechanism
fails it, and that each reachability witness is violated. ADR-0026 §6 names what that is: non-vacuity and
discrimination. The 09.12 external assessment (I.8) and both critical reviews (CR-4; MAP 2.3 in the NIST mapping)
named the thing the corpus had not said: whether its claims rest on model checking or on proof, and in what
language the word "proven" is used. The Theory of Operation has carried "MAP 2.3 (TLC versus TLAPS undeclared)"
in its gaps list through six revisions.

On 2026.10.02 thirteen of the fourteen modules under `spec/` gained a proof module, each extending a frozen module
without editing it and proving the invariants that module states with the TLA+ Proof System (TLAPS), under named
assumptions, for every value of the constants those assumptions admit. 2,417 obligations are proved across them;
every proof module was also checked by mutation, in that a copy with one hypothesis flipped to the mutation it
excludes fails. `spec/proof-method.md` records how they were written and what they found. The one module without a
proof is `Mechanism.tla`, which is a trace replay over a recorded trace, not a transition system with invariants;
the meaningful target there is a refinement, which is T-0045-28's debt and ADR-0033's.

The proofs change the kind of evidence the formal layer offers, and three things follow that the corpus's rules
require a record to say: what the method now is (MAP 2.3); that the prover is a thing the proofs trust, which under
ADR-0045 §5 makes it a row; and which artifacts the claim rests on, by digest, which under the corpus's freezing
rule fixes them. Writing the proofs also found four errors in `spec/theorem-premise-binding.md`'s "Breaks under"
column and one in its parameter column, which this record corrects.

## Decision

### 1. The method is declared

The formal layer uses two methods, and the corpus says which claim rests on which.

- **TLC, at small constants** (ADR-0026 §6): for every module, that each mutation named in the module fails the
  property it targets, and that each reachability witness is violated. These are existential claims, that a
  behaviour exists, and TLC is the method for them. The results files under `spec/` are that evidence and remain
  so. A TLC "holds" row is additionally a check of the property at those constants, and for `Mechanism.tla` it is
  the whole of the evidence.
- **TLAPS, deductively**: for the thirteen modules §4 names, that each invariant the module states holds of every
  behaviour of the module, as `Spec => []P`, under the module's named assumptions on its constants and under the
  hypothesis that the mutations which break that invariant are absent. These are universal claims, and the prover
  is the method for them. The results files named `*-proof-results-*.txt` are that evidence.

The word **proved** is henceforth used in the corpus only of a claim the second method has discharged, and names
the hypotheses it was discharged under. **Checked** is used of the first. Where a narrative or checkpoint says a
property is proved of the running reference, it is wrong, and §2 says why.

### 2. What a proof claims, and does not

A proved obligation is a theorem of the proof module under its assumptions and the prover (§3). It is bounded by
those two things and not by constants: `FloorPreserved` is proved for every floor subset and every epoch bound,
`ContainedMeansConfined` for every declared rung, `UnreadBounded` for every K, R and MaxRound, where TLC ran one
value of each.

A proof is of the module. It is not a claim about `mechanism/`, about any deployment, or about the premises
ADR-0045 rows carry. **T-0045-28 stands unchanged**: every proved property holds of the running reference only on
trust in the refinement nobody has made, exactly as Annex B's inventory says of the TLC results. The premise
table's rows apply to a proved property as they applied to a checked one; what changes is one premise, T-0045-29,
which for a proved property is replaced by T-0045-31 (§3), and the "at the constants run" qualification, which a
proof does not carry.

A proof does not replace the mutation rows. The prover proves universal statements; that a mutation reaches a bad
state is existential and stays with TLC. Each proof module instead carries one lemma per mutation stating what the
mutation admits, so that the TLC counterexample and the proof's hypothesis name the same guard.

### 3. The prover is a trusted-base row *(drafter's choice: a trust root, like T-0045-29)*

Under ADR-0045 §5 a proof takes the prover as a premise it does not discharge, and the prover fails the
subtract-only test: an unsound prover accepts a false theorem, and a mechanism built to it admits what the theorem
forbids. Its identity is fixed by a digest the results files print. It is therefore a row, in the toolchain layer,
beside T-0045-29. On ratification ADR-0045's Annex A gains:

## Options considered

- **Declare the method in the narrative only, without a record.** Rejected: the prover is a row under ADR-0045 §5,
  and a row enters by record; and the digests that freeze the proof modules must be in a record for the freezing
  rule to apply.
- **Cite the proofs from each owning record by amendment, instead of from one record.** Rejected as the primary
  vehicle: twelve amendments for one event, each repeating the method declaration. Taken as dependent entries (§6),
  which is what a ratification touching another record produces.
- **Make the prover a delegated row rather than a trust root.** Rejected: ADR-0045 §2 makes a row delegated when
  its failure can only subtract. An unsound prover adds, by accepting a false theorem. T-0045-29 was sorted the
  same way for the same reason.
- **Track the prover binary in the repository, as the TLC jar is.** Not taken *(drafter's choice)*: 1.9 GB against
  2.3 MB. The digest in every results file does what tracking the jar did for T-0045-29; the open obligation below
  keeps the question.
- **Prove `Mechanism.tla`.** Not applicable: it states no invariants and replays traces. Its target is the
  refinement, which stays with ADR-0033 and T-0045-28.

## Consequences

- The corpus can say "proved" of thirteen modules' invariants, and must say under what hypotheses. It cannot say it
  of the reference, and the premise table's inventory column is unchanged by this record.
- Each proof's hypotheses are a second, independent derivation of the "Breaks under" column, and found four errors
  in it. The two columns should agree from here on; a future disagreement is a finding, not a transcription.
- The proof modules are frozen with their base modules. A change to a base module is a successor module with a
  successor proof.
- One more trust root, T-0045-31, in the toolchain layer. The trusted base grows by a row that the archetype did not
  have because it had no proofs; whether that is a growth or a swap (T-0045-29's "at the constants run" leaves)
  is the inventory's to compute, and it computes as a swap for the invariants and a growth for nothing.
- The reviews' "formal layer promissory" (CR-4) loses its method half. Its refinement half (T-0045-28) is untouched
  and is now the only half.
