## Context

**Detection fails open** (CR-2, 09.16). The Observer is deny-only, veto-seat silence is not a veto (ADR-0024 §2),
and in Run the human on the loop can only disable (boundary register B10). Put together, an Observer that is dead,
partitioned, starved of accelerator time or colluding produces the same observable as a clean system. The 09.25
review found this untouched after nine days and gave the choice plainly: couple liveness to admission, or declare
that detection fails open on the residual-risk statement. **This record couples, and declares the part coupling
cannot reach.**

Three earlier results make coupling admissible and fix its shape.

- **ADR-0007 admits a syntactic fact.** "The Observer replica set attested liveness within the last `T`" is totally
  extractable, and it is not a judgment (glossary 09.17; ADR-0024's open obligation says the same). ADR-0024 §2
  forbids the adjacent thing, an Observer attestation of *no finding*, which would make a judgment an enabling
  condition. The line between them is the line between "I am here and have read to offset *n*" and "I read it and it
  was fine".
- **ADR-0032 says which clock.** An interval whose expiry subtracts authority may be read on the mechanism's local
  monotonic clock, with a margin that elapses early (§2). No safety decision may read a clock that a principal
  supplies (§3). So liveness is measured by when the *mechanism* last admitted something from the Observer, and
  never by the Observer's timestamp.
- **ADR-0038 fixes whom to couple to.** `W(p)` is a fact in the mechanism's state. It separates the cell Observer,
  whose absence should stop one cell, from the correlator, whose absence should stop nothing.

**Presence is not enough.** A starved Observer is alive and can still send heartbeats, but it is falling behind.
ADR-0008 named accelerator contention as an availability attack on the Observer, and a heartbeat check alone cannot
see it. What detection needs is for the Observer to keep up with what it watches.

**The round already bounds part of the backlog** ⟨R1⟩. A lease covers one round (ADR-0031 §2), and the round bound
`R` caps the admits in a round (ADR-0022 §3); the reference checks it on every admit, in Build and Run alike
(`state.rs`, `RoundBoundReached`). So with no coupling at all, a Thinker has at most `R` unread emissions *in its
current round*. What nothing bounds is the backlog **across** rounds. The next round's lease opens a new topic, and
its sequence number and its round count both restart, while the Observer may still be reading the last round.
Coupling therefore has two jobs the round cannot do: tighten the in-round bound from `R` to `K`, and carry coverage
from one round to the next.

## Decision

### 1. Liveness is a heartbeat with a watermark, timed by the mechanism

An `observer` seat reports liveness by **`Emit`ting a heartbeat**: a fixed-schema record that carries two fields,
the watched round topic and its **watermark**, the sequence number in that topic through which the Observer says it
has read ⟨R1⟩. The heartbeat is a fact, not a finding. It says nothing about what was read. *Pending ⟨A1⟩, not
applied:* in Run the variant that carries it is `Report` (ADR-0043 §4); `Emit` is Build's. See History A1.

For each cell Observer `o` watching a Thinker `t`, the mechanism keeps two values in its state:

- **`heard(o)`**: the mechanism's local monotonic time at which it last admitted a heartbeat under `o`'s live lease.
- **`mark(o, r)`**: the latest watermark `o` has reported for the Thinker's round `r`. A heartbeat whose watermark
  for a round is lower than the last one for that round is refused, so each watermark only rises. Marks are kept per
  seat, not per lease, so an Observer's next lease continues where its last left off (finding 3) ⟨R1⟩.

**`live(o)` holds** when three conditions hold:

1. `o` holds a live lease.
2. Fewer than `T_obs` have elapsed since `heard(o)`, read with ADR-0032 §2's asymmetric margin (elapsing early).
3. **`seq(t) − mark(o, r) < K`** for `t`'s current round `r` ⟨R5⟩, where `seq(t)` is `t`'s emission sequence number
   in that round (`mechanism/` G15; it restarts with each lease) ⟨R1⟩. The form is strict because the admit being
   decided counts: after it, at most `K` of the current round's emissions are unread by its Observer. (`≤ K` would
   admit a `K+1` backlog, which `Coupling.tla` found.) `K = 0` therefore admits nothing until the hold of finding 1
   exists. Earlier rounds are handled by §2's grant condition.
   *Pending ⟨A1⟩, not applied:* in Run, `seq(t)` counts `t`'s **effect-bearing admits**, `Actuate` and `Report`,
   and not `Consult` (ADR-0042 §2); "emissions" in this condition reads "effects". Build is unchanged. See
   History A1.

`live(o)` is computed by the typed lift from mechanism state and given to the floor as a context field. It is a
syntactic lift (ADR-0007).

**`T_obs` and `K` are policy-floor values**, like ADR-0035's θ. Relaxing either is an anchor issuance and a promotion.
Tightening either is an ordinary promotion.

**Where policy-floor values live** ⟨R3⟩. The floor is Cedar text and schema S (ADR-0030), and neither holds a number
the typed lift reads. So the floor bundle has a third file, **`floor/genesis-values.json`**, and the bundle's
identity is the digest of all three: the policies, the schema, and the values. The gate run records the three
digests together, and the mechanism links the values with the floor (ADR-0030 §3: no process, no fetch). A change to
the values file is a change to the floor, so it takes the floor's path: an issuance and a promotion to relax,
a promotion to tighten. The floor's inputs are still written only by the lift and the issued rules; the values file
is not an input, it is part of the floor that reads them. ADR-0035's θ, β and the near-duplicate tool's digest
share the file, which is the home ADR-0035 named as "policy-floor values" and did not locate.

**The placeholders** ⟨R3⟩. Every number below is a **placeholder**, chosen on 2026.09.29 so that §8's runs have a
value. None is measured. Each is a **deployment or rung choice**, declared per deployment under ADR-0023 §6, and the
archetype does not fix it; a deployment that ships these numbers unexamined is declaring them, not inheriting them.

| Value | Placeholder | Lives in | Why this number | What a deployment sets it from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `T_obs` | 5000 ms | values file | About five propagation windows `W` (ADR-0032 §4, ~1 s at the archetype), ten of the reference's lease margins (500 ms), well inside a lease TTL (120 s in the reference's smoke test). Read with ADR-0032 §2's asymmetric margin | The Observer's measured heartbeat cadence and the margin |
| `K` | 8 | values file | `T_obs` at roughly one admit per second, rounded up, so an Observer keeping pace never blocks. `K = 0` is the quarantine hold (finding 1) | The Observer's measured lag; the tolerated unread backlog |
| `R` (Build) | 200 | mechanism parameters (`mechanism/` I21; `dev-init.sh`) | The reference's existing dev value, unchanged | Termination and state-space size (ADR-0022 §3) |
| `R` (Run) | 64 | mechanism parameters, **per domain** | Run actions are effect-bearing and Run rounds are short; 64 admits at about one per second is a minute of activity, the order of a Run lease's TTL. The reference applies one value to both domains today, and this record makes it two (obligations) | The same, per Run's alphabet once it exists (G4 item 13) |

**`R` is not in the values file.** It is a mechanism parameter, not a floor value, and it stays one: ADR-0022 §3 put
it there, and the corpus has one parameter set per mechanism release (I21). The `K < R` check therefore reads across
the two: the values file's `K` against each domain's `R` from the parameters, at startup and in the gates. `K < 64`
and `K < 200` both hold for the placeholders.

**`K` must be below the round bound `R`** ⟨R1⟩. With `K ≥ R`, condition 3 can never refuse an admit that the round
bound has not already refused. The check would then be vacuous, the ground on which ADR-0026 R3 was withdrawn. `R` is
a mechanism parameter (`mechanism/` I21) and `K` is a floor value, so the check compares the two. The mechanism refuses
at startup a floor whose `K ≥ R`, as it already checks the genesis rules against the rules floor at startup
(`mechanism/` G6), and the floor gates check it too.

### 2. Run: every admit needs a live cell Observer

**An admit under a Thinker's lease in Run requires `live(o)` for at least one cell Observer `o` in its cell.**
Otherwise the admit is denied: locally, with no consensus, attributed to the Thinker's lease, and recorded with the
reason. The rule is the new floor entry **`R3-observer-live`**, a forbid on `RunInvoke` and `RunEmit` **under a
`thinker` seat** when `!context.observerLive` ⟨R5⟩; an observer seat's own emissions, its heartbeats among them, are
not coupled, or the rule would refuse the report it exists to receive. *Pending ⟨A1⟩, not applied:* the first
sentence reads "**An effect-bearing admit, `Actuate` or `Report`, under a Thinker's lease in Run** requires
`live(o)`", and `R3-observer-live` is a forbid on those two; `Consult` is not coupled (ADR-0041 §2). See History
A1. The admit is decided, ordered and re-checked
at apply like every other (ADR-0033 §4). **What apply re-checks is the deterministic half of `live(o)`**, conditions
1 and 3 ⟨R5⟩: an Observer whose lease ended, or whose watermark fell behind, between decide and commit voids the
admit. Condition 2 is a per-replica clock and is checked at decide only, as lease expiry is (ADR-0032 §2); a
heartbeat that goes stale in that window is caught at the next admit, not this one.

**Coverage carries across rounds** ⟨R1⟩. **A Thinker's lease for round `r+1` in Run is granted only if a cell
Observer's `mark(o, r)` is at or beyond the seal of round `r`'s topic** (0003.2). This is a check on the grant path,
not a floor entry, because the floor evaluates actions and a grant is not one. It sits at the grant rather than the
first admit so that a Thinker waiting for its Observer holds no lease and burns no TTL. It refuses the *Thinker's*
grant, which subtracts. ADR-0035 §5's "never at grant" concerns refusing an *Observer's* grant, which would remove
oversight, and it is not touched.

- **The cost falls on one cell.** A dead or slow cell Observer stops its own Thinker and nothing else. That is
  ADR-0038's bounded reach, used as a bound on outage.
- **The Thinker waits; it is not evicted.** A denied admit is not an eviction. When the Observer catches up, admits
  resume within the same round, or the next round is granted ⟨R1⟩. A deployment may add an eviction timeout, which
  would also subtract.
- **What this bounds.** ⟨R1⟩ `L` stays unmeasured (ADR-0032 §4), and this record does not measure it. It bounds
  something next to it. Uncoupled, the round bound already limits a Thinker to `R` unread emissions in its current
  round, and nothing limits the backlog across rounds. Coupled, **at any moment, at most `K` of a Thinker's
  emissions, all of them in its current round, have not been read by an Observer that has reported in within
  `T_obs`**, because every earlier round was read to its seal before the next was granted. That is the first
  mechanism-enforced limit on what can happen inside `L`. It is a count, not a time, and it holds only for an
  Observer whose watermark is honest (§5).

### 3. Build: the gate needs coverage, not each admit

Build's gate blocks, and detection only has to finish before the gate clears (ToO §5.2). So Build does not couple
authoring admits. It couples the promotion instead:

**A promotion completes only if the author's cell Observer has a watermark at or beyond the seal of the author's
round topic set** (ADR-0021 §3). In other words, the Observer has reported reading the whole round. This is added to
ADR-0035 §5's tally condition, and it belongs in ADR-0024 as an amendment, carried as a debt with ADR-0035's and
ADR-0038's.

- **Coverage is not a "no finding" attestation.** It says the Observer read to the seal. Whether it objected is
  still expressed only by `Vote(deny)`, and its silence is still not a veto (ADR-0024 §2).
- **Withholding coverage stalls the promotion, and adds nothing.** An Observer that never reports coverage acts like
  a veto. A veto seat can deny anyway, so this gives it no new authority. It is a stall, which is the PC/EC cost
  ADR-0035 §5 already accepts.
- **ADR-0024's veto-seat-outage obligation is discharged.** "Whether promotion should require Observer liveness, a
  syntactic heartbeat rather than a judgment" is answered: it requires coverage, which is stronger than a heartbeat
  and still syntactic.
- **Build needs no cross-round rule** ⟨R1⟩. Build's only egress is promotion (ADR-0022), and promotion already
  requires coverage of the rounds it promotes. An authoring round that is never submitted has no egress, so an unread
  backlog in it reaches nothing.

### 4. The correlator is never coupled

**No admit and no promotion waits on a correlator.** Coupling to it would make one principal's liveness a
precondition for every admit in its domain. That is the availability attack ADR-0008 named, moved from a device to
a seat, and it is the fleet-wide stall ADR-0038 §1 exists to avoid.

**The absence of a correlator costs cross-cell detection, which is D-0038-2.** Its liveness is still reported, by
the same heartbeat, so an absent correlator is *visible*. That visibility is the whole of what this record gives it.

### 5. What coupling does not cover

Coupling separates a present, keeping-up Observer from an absent or lagging one. **It cannot separate an honest
Observer from a colluding one.** A colluding Observer sends heartbeats, reports a watermark, and finds nothing, and
all three are its own assertions. Of CR-2's four cases, coupling closes **dead**, **partitioned** and **starved**,
and leaves **colluding** open.

Collusion is covered, as far as the corpus covers it, by lineage independence (ADR-0016 §5, ADR-0035), and it is
measured, if at all, by ADR-0035 §6's correlated-miss test, which only subtracts. Honeypot validation at seat
admission (LP-4) is not decided anywhere. **The residual-risk statement must say: detection fails open against a
colluding Observer.** This record puts that sentence on the statement.

**A Byzantine Observer can stop its own cell** by withholding heartbeats. That is availability, within one cell.
Evicting it and seating another Observer is the remedy, and in the meantime its Thinker makes no admits, which is the
direction that fails closed.

### 6. The Observer is now on the hot path, and the cost is stated

Coupling puts the cell Observer on Run's admit path for *liveness*, not for judgment. That has three costs:

- **Latency.** When the Observer lags by more than `K`, the Thinker waits, and a new round waits until the last one
  has been read ⟨R1⟩. With `K` large enough to cover the Observer's normal lag, an admit waits only when the Observer
  is actually behind. `K` cannot exceed `R` (§1), so the in-round slack is at most a round.
- **Availability.** The cell's availability is the product of its Thinker's and its Observer's. ADR-0038 §5's
  extra Observers raise it, because any one live Observer is enough (§2).
- **Throughput.** Heartbeats are admits, one per `T_obs` per Observer, on the consensus log.

None of these costs is a term in `W + L + D < T_irrev`. They cost throughput and availability, not time to
detection.

### 8. Ratification condition

- **ADR-0038 is ratified first, or together with this record**, since `W(p)` is its relation.
- **The floor.** RunCtx gains `observerLive: Bool`, and the forbid `R3-observer-live` is added. The floor gates are
  rerun, with results in a dated file. Generated tests: a `RunInvoke` with `observerLive = false` is denied, and with
  `true` it falls through to the existing permits.
- **The values file** ⟨R3⟩: `floor/genesis-values.json` exists with the placeholders of §1, the gate run records its
  digest beside the floor's and the schema's, and the reference loads it with the floor.
- **The `K < R` check** ⟨R1⟩: a floor with `K ≥ R` for either domain is refused at startup and by the floor gates ⟨R3⟩.
- **A TLC check**, as a module with one Thinker, one cell Observer and one correlator, over at least two rounds
  ⟨R1⟩:
  - **`NoAdmitWithoutLiveObserver`**: no Run admit occurs while `live(o)` is false for every cell Observer.
    Mutation `M_NOCOUPLE`: the check is dropped. It must fail.
  - **`UnreadBounded`**: with an honest Observer, the Thinker's unread emissions, summed over **all** its rounds, never
    exceed `K` ⟨R1⟩. Mutation `M_MARK`: the check tests the heartbeat time and not the watermark. It must fail, and
    the failure shows a starved Observer passing. Mutation **`M_ROUNDS`** ⟨R1⟩: coverage is not carried across rounds,
    so the only check compares the current round's sequence with its mark. It must fail, with a trace in which the
    Thinker is granted round 2 while round 1 is unread. **`M_ROUNDS` is this record's text before R1** (History).
  - **`CorrelatorNeverGates`**: a state predicate using `ENABLED`. With a live cell Observer, the Thinker's admit
    stays enabled whatever the correlator's state. Mutation `M_CORR`: the correlator is coupled. It must fail.
- **The reference** (with an ADR-0033 addendum): the heartbeat record's schema; `heard` and `mark` in mechanism state,
  with a falling watermark refused; the lift computing `observerLive`; and a scripted cell Observer, in the shape of
  ADR-0036's occupant, that sends heartbeats. **Four refusals, each seen to fire:** a stale heartbeat, a backlog
  over `K`, no cell Observer lease, and a grant for round `r+1` while round `r` is unread ⟨R1⟩. Plus one Build promotion stalled for lack of coverage and completed after it.

## Consequences

**Positive**
- **CR-2 gets a record**, and three of its four cases close.
- **The first mechanism-enforced bound touching `L`**: at most `K` unread emissions per Thinker, across all its
  rounds, given an honest Observer ⟨R1⟩.
- **ADR-0024's veto-seat-outage obligation is discharged**, by coverage at the gate.
- **An absent Observer becomes visible**, correlator included, because every `observer` seat sends heartbeats.
- **The residual-risk statement gains a precise line**: fails open against collusion, and only against collusion.

**Negative**
- **The Observer is on Run's hot path** for liveness, with the latency, availability and throughput costs of §6.
- **The `K` bound depends on the Observer's honesty.** The watermark is its own assertion, so under collusion the
  bound is empty, which is why §5 says collusion is not covered.
- **A Byzantine Observer can stop its cell.** It is contained to one cell, but it is a new way to cause an outage.
- **Two more floor values to choose.** `T_obs` and `K` have no measured basis yet, just as `L` has none. `K` is at
  least bounded above, by `R` ⟨R1⟩.
- **A Thinker's next round waits on its Observer** ⟨R1⟩. A slow Observer now delays grants as well as admits.
- **One author, and no options discussion.**

## Findings

1. **The quarantine gate is still undecided.** The 09.10 design had the Observer "gating emission from quarantine".
   That *hold* is a stronger form of coupling than §2: nothing is emitted until an Observer has read it, which is
   `K = 0` with a hold instead of a wait. This record does not take it. `K = 0` is expressible as a floor value, and
   the hold's semantics (G12; the glossary's "Observer quarantine gate", open) should be decided against §1's facts.
2. **Run's alphabet is provisional** (G4 item 13), so `R3-observer-live` is written against the provisional
   `RunInvoke` and `RunEmit`. Every Run variant a future record adds needs the same forbid. *Pending ⟨A1⟩:*
   answered by ADR-0041 §2, in the negative for `Consult` and by restating R3 for `Actuate` and `Report`.
3. **A heartbeat is an admit, and the Observer's round is a lease.** An Observer's heartbeats stop when its round
   ends. In Run, a cell's Observer rounds must be granted so that they overlap its Thinker's rounds, or `live(o)` has
   gaps. How rounds are laid out is not specified anywhere, and this record makes it matter. *Since R1 the watermark
   survives the gap, because marks are kept per seat (§1). The heartbeat's timing does not survive it* ⟨R1⟩.
4. **The first draft bounded the wrong backlog** ⟨R1⟩. Its check, `seq(t) − mark(o) ≤ K`, was scoped to the current
   round's topic. There the round bound already limits the backlog to `R`, so the check added little, and only when
   `K < R`. Both values restart with each lease, so the check passed at the start of every new round, whatever the
   Observer had left unread. The backlog that needed bounding, the one across rounds, was bounded by nothing. R1 adds
   the cross-round grant condition (§2) and the `K < R` requirement (§1), and turns the first draft's behavior into
   mutation `M_ROUNDS` (§8). The same shape appeared in ADR-0037's finding 1: a rule keyed one level too low.
