# ADR-0067 — Rules of engagement live in three places: standing rules in the one Cedar floor, a mission's rules in a promoted Wasm bundle, and judgments about the world nowhere in the admit path; the floor gains a `federation` stage

**Status:** Proposed · 2026.11.10
**Status note:** Confirmation required under **C1**: the stage's entries are forbids on privileged paths, and two
existing floor entries are relaxed (§4), each of which routes as a relaxation (ToO §3.2). C2, C3 and C4 do not apply
on the drafter's reading while this is an overlay ⟨Q11⟩. **No floor is ratified, so nothing here can be in force**
(§5). **Nothing here is written as policy, gated, modelled or built.**
**Pending:** none.
**Deployment position:** **An overlay, not the archetype** ⟨Q11⟩. No degeneracy of its own.
**Depends on:** ADR-0059 to ADR-0066 (what the entries forbid), ADR-0030 (bundles cannot add authority; the floor on
a verified evaluator), ADR-0007 (no semantic judgment is a lift), ADR-0058 (overlays; Proposed)
**Relates to:** ADR-0041 (`R3`, and `R5` to `R7`), ADR-0029 A4 (floor fitness), ADR-0047 (labels), ADR-0049 (the
allowlist entry)
**Closes:** nothing.
**Carries a debt against:** nothing while this is an overlay.

## Context

The custodian put two things: rules of engagement as a promoted Wasm bundle ⟨5⟩, and the Cedar floor in force with
them ⟨7⟩. A mission's rules are of three kinds, and the corpus already has a place for two of them and a reason to
refuse the third.

---

## Decision

### 1. Three places

| Part of the rules | Where it lives | Why there |
|---|---|---|
| Standing rules that hold for every mission | The Cedar floor, the same at every plane and every governor (ADR-0059 §2) ⟨Q8⟩ | It is the verified object, and no bundle can weaken it |
| A mission's rules that can be lifted mechanically | A promoted bundle, compiled to Wasm | Voted by digest, able only to refuse, and evaluated the same way at a plane and on a vehicle |
| Rules that need a judgment about the world | Nowhere in the admit path | ADR-0007: no semantic judgment is an enabling condition |

### 2. A mission's rules are a promoted Wasm bundle ⟨5⟩

**A bundle can express what a mechanical lift can supply**: areas; time windows, as ranges of the plane's ticks
(ADR-0064); which entries a seat kind may use; counts, within a vehicle or across vehicles; which entries are
`ordered` (ADR-0062 §3); a refusal of a report to a peer topic, by seat kind, rate or label; a refusal of an entry
once the stack has read a peer's report in the clear (ADR-0065 §3); and lists that come from a declared source and
carry its label.

- **A commanded plane tightens by promoting a bundle**, which is appended (ADR-0059 §2). A commander tightens
  faster by narrowing a value in the next intent (ADR-0060 §2).
- **Loosening needs a promotion** at a plane whose charter allows it, and otherwise goes up the tree. That
  asymmetry is the one the corpus already has between subtraction and grant.
- **At an autonomous plane and on a governor the Wasm engine is the only one.** The reference has the path:
  `topak-bundle` loads a signed bundle into an in-process engine bound to the lease's revision, as the fallback
  when the sidecar cannot answer (`mechanism/README.md`).
- **A stale bundle on a vehicle is safe in the direction that matters**: it can refuse too much and cannot allow
  what the floor refuses (ToO §3.1).

### 3. The floor gains a `federation` stage ⟨7⟩

The stage would follow `workflow` in `floor/proposed/README.md`.

| Id | Kind | Says | From |
|---|---|---|---|
| `FD1-charter-narrows` | forbid | No seating of a plane, and no intent, whose charter exceeds its bound in any field | ADR-0059 §1 |
| `FD2-lease-within-charter` | forbid | No grant whose lease exceeds the granting plane's charter: an entry outside its alphabet, a `ttl` above its `ttlMax`, a count above its `countMax`, an expiry after its own | ADR-0059 §1 |
| `FD3-budget-spent` | forbid | No grant, and no seating of a plane, once the charter's budget is spent | ADR-0059 §1 |
| `FD4-autonomous-adds-nothing` | forbid | At an autonomous plane: no promotion, no seating, no operator's request, no bundle change, no grant but a node's lease under a live intent, and no change of membership but a step of its charter's schedule | ADR-0060 §3 |
| `FD5-irreversible-release-only` | forbid | No `Actuate` of an irreversible entry under a seat whose kind is not `release` | ADR-0066 §1 |
| `FD6-irreversible-from-commanded` | forbid | No admit of an irreversible entry without a commanded plane's commit certificate for that act, unused and fresh | ADR-0066 §4 |
| `FD7-local-marked` | forbid | No permit by a governor of an entry its bundle revision does not mark `local`, and none of an irreversible entry | ADR-0062 §3 |
| `FD8-local-bounds` | forbid | No permit by a governor with the lease's count spent, past its `ttl` in ticks, or under a lease that has ended | ADR-0062 §2, §7 |
| `FD9-release-seating` | forbid | No `release` seating of a key that is not on the anchor-issued roster, none at an autonomous plane, and none at a plane whose charter lacks `release` | ADR-0066 §2 |
| `FD10-outstanding-bound` | forbid | At an autonomous plane: no grant and no `ordered` admit that would take the outstanding charge to `K_up`. At a governor: no lease accepted, and no `ordered` entry carried out ⟨R1⟩, that would take its own to `K_up` | ADR-0063 §3 |
| `FD11-alone-permits-nothing` | forbid | No permit by a governor whose vehicle is alone, but the beacon within its count | ADR-0062 §5 |
| `FD12-topic-declared` | forbid | No report to a peer topic the promoted mesh does not declare for the seat, and none that names a recipient | ADR-0065 §4 |
| `FD13-step-in-schedule` | forbid | No step of membership that the charter's schedule does not hold, and none below its capture floor | ADR-0061 §3 |
| `FD14-count-within-K` | forbid | No lease whose count exceeds `K`, or its charter's `countMax` | ADR-0062 §8 |

**Every new entry subtracts.**

### 4. Two existing entries are relaxed, and the schema grows

- **`B10-only-thinkers-author` gains an exception** for a `release` seat's `Actuate`, as it has for an `emergency`
  seat (ADR-0066).
- **`R3-observer-live` is scoped to admits a plane makes**, with a `local` entry covered at its lease's grant
  (ADR-0062 §8).

Each relaxation routes as one (ToO §3.2).

**The schema gains** the kind `release`; the entities `Plane`, with its charter and kind, and `Topic`; `effect` on
an entry; and, in the Run context, who is permitting, whether the vehicle hears its plane, whether the count is
spent, the outstanding charge and the tick. **The lift supplies these and the floor trusts its inputs**, as
`floor/proposed/README.md` says of every stage. On a vehicle the lift is the governor's.

### 5. "In force" is a precondition this record cannot meet

**No floor text is ratified.** The floor is ratified when a bootstrap reviewer finds it fit, and no test for fitness
is written (G11 §2.2 and item 87; ADR-0029 A4). For this stage, whether the standing rules are the right ones is a
legal judgment as much as a technical one. A grant seat with that named role is suggested *(drafter's suggestion)*.

### 6. What no policy here expresses

Identification, intent, necessity and proportion are judgments about the world. A classifier's output enters the
system only as a finding, and a finding may subtract authority and never add it (ADR-0007; ToO §2.2). So these parts
of a mission's rules are enforced by nothing in this set of records. For an irreversible effect they are the
judgment of the person at the release seat (ADR-0066 §5). For everything else they are, at most, something an
Observer may object to after the fact.

### 7. Ratification condition

- **ADR-0059 to ADR-0066 are ratified first**, or with this record, each for the entries it is the source of.
- **The entries are written in Cedar and pass the three gates**: totality, non-vacuity and discrimination, as every
  stage does. **Not met: no entry is written.**
- **The stage is shown monotone in what a vehicle has received from its peers** (ADR-0065 §1), as the label forbids
  are in `clearSet`.
- **The two relaxations are routed** as ToO §3.2 has a relaxation routed.
- **A floor is ratified**, which is ADR-0029 A4's and not this record's.
- **A reader who is not the drafter** (ADR-0029 A2).

## Options considered

| Option | Disposition |
|---|---|
| **All of a mission's rules in the floor** | Rejected. The floor would change with every mission, and it is the one artifact that is verified |
| **A mission's rules through a sidecar on the vehicle** | Not taken. The in-process Wasm engine is the reference's fallback and the only engine a vehicle needs |
| **A classifier in the admit path**, for identification or proportion | Rejected by ADR-0007 |
| **A floor per plane** | Not taken ⟨Q8⟩ (ADR-0059 §2) |
| **Rules enforced by the stack** | Not a rule. The stack is Byzantine |

---

## Consequences

**Positive**
- **A mission's rules are voted by digest and can only refuse**, on a vehicle as at a plane.
- **Fourteen entries, all forbids.**
- **What cannot be enforced is said**, and not left to be assumed.

**Negative**
- **The parts of a mission's rules that matter most are enforced by nothing here** (§6).
- **Nothing is in force** until a floor is, and floor fitness has no test (§5).
- **Two relaxations** of entries the floor already has.
- **The governor is the lift on a vehicle**, so the floor there trusts what one component measures.
- **One author, one day, and no policy text.**
