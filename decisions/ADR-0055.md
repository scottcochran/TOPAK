# ADR-0055 — A workflow may be a mesh: cycles are allowed under an activation budget the promotion votes on, the activation is the unit of attributes, routes and findings, and the mechanism tracks while the orchestrator holds no state

**Status:** Proposed · 2026.10.07
**Status note:** Confirmation required under **C1**, because the grant path ADR-0054 §2 opens now issues leases out of
a budget, and the orchestrator's rounds are drawn from it (§2, §6). C2 does not apply: Run's ingress and alphabet
are ADR-0054's and gain nothing here. C3 does not apply: ADR-0054 is Proposed, and its acyclicity rule is kept for
the default kind and widened by declaration (§1). C4 does not apply on the drafter's reading. **Every clause
subtracts** except the budgeted grant, whose authority is the promotion's, as in ADR-0054.
**Pending:** none.
**Deployment position:** Archetype, as an opt-in widening of ADR-0054. No degeneracy of its own (§9).
**Depends on:** ADR-0054 (the workflow, its grant path, `Route`, attributes, inputs and labels, the re-check at
apply), ADR-0031 §2 (one live lease per principal; a lease is round-coterminous), ADR-0005 (Kafka is transport),
ADR-0047 (labels; `clearSet` only grows)
**Relates to:** ADR-0035 (lineage independence), ADR-0039 (coverage), ADR-0048 (what a cell is told, and the opening
record), ADR-0038 and ADR-0051 (the correlator), ADR-0045 (the trusted base), ADR-0046 (the method for §11's module)
**Closes:** nothing. It widens ADR-0054.
**Carries a debt against:** ADR-0054 §1 (a workflow declares its kind; a `mesh` loads with a budget, §1), §2 (the
grant is charged to a budget, §2), §4 (attributes per activation; a guard's read set is a causal history, §3), §8
(one route per activation, §3), §9 (completion and failure for a mesh, §8) · ADR-0035 §5 (the tally checks that a
mesh's clear edges are 2-coloured, §5) · ADR-0033 (an addendum: a scripted mesh) · ADR-0045 (the interpreter's
row grows, §10)

---

## Context

ADR-0054 made a workflow a DAG ⟨K1⟩, and the DAG bought four things without stating them. **It ends because of its
shape**, so a checker reaches every path and no liveness argument is needed. **Its guards are settled**: every
attribute is written once per node and a guard reads only nodes that must already have run. **A node is one round**,
so a decision point is consumed once and a finding against a node names one round. **Its joins have a fixed
meaning**, because each branch runs at most once.

The custodian asked whether TOPAK can support an event-driven workflow: a mesh in place of a DAG, promoted to Run and
run by the mechanism, its workers of alternate lineage, and an orchestrator that tracks the work and routes as it is
needed. **A loop whose number of turns is decided at run time** is the case a DAG cannot express without unrolling
it in advance: draft and review until the review passes, retry until a check succeeds, gather until enough has been
gathered.

Two readings of "event-driven" exist, and only one survives the havoc premise. In **choreography**, workers publish
events and other workers subscribe to them, and no one decides what runs next. Nothing in TOPAK supports it, and
this record does not add it (§7). In **orchestration over a log**, the events are entries the mechanism orders, and
the mechanism decides what they enable. That is ADR-0054's shape, and the question is what it costs to let the
graph have cycles.

---

## Decision

### 1. A workflow declares its kind, and a mesh is a declared widening ⟨M1⟩ ⟨M5⟩

A workflow (ADR-0054 §1) declares a **kind**, `dag` or `mesh`.

- **`dag` is the default**, and ADR-0054 applies to it unchanged: a `dag` whose graph has a cycle does not load.
- **A `mesh` may have cycles, self-loops included.** It does not load without a budget (§2). Everything ADR-0054
  says of a workflow applies to a mesh, read per activation where this record says so.

A mesh is a widening, so it is declared and visible in the artifact the quorum votes on. A deployment that wants
only DAGs refuses the kind in its bundle's load check *(drafter's choice)*.

### 2. The promotion votes on a budget, and every lease is charged to it ⟨M2⟩

A mesh carries an **activation budget**: a bound `N` on the leases the workflow may ever issue, an optional bound
`N(v)` per node, and the time bound `T_wf` ADR-0054 §1 already has. The quorum votes on the budget with the graph and
the seatings, and **the promotion grants at most `N` leases**.

- **A unit of budget is one cell-round**: a worker's or the orchestrator's lease, with its cell Observer's
  *(drafter's choice: Observers ride with their cell and are not charged separately)*.
- **The orchestrator's rounds are charged** (§6). A budget that charged only workers would bound workers and leave
  the orchestrator's leases unbounded. `M_ORCHFREE` is that.
- **A grant the budget cannot pay is refused**, and the activation fails. **Running out of budget fails the
  workflow**, never completes it, as a timeout never grants (ADR-0024 §2).
- **The budget is what makes a mesh end.** Every behaviour of a budgeted mesh issues at most `N` leases, so "it
  ends" stays a **bounded safety property** a model checker decides at small `N`, and needs no fairness assumption.
  Without a budget the promotion would be a standing grant of unbounded leases, which "promotion is a grant" cannot
  mean.

**A budgeted mesh is a DAG the quorum did not have to unroll.** Its behaviours are exactly those of the mesh unrolled
to depth `N`. ADR-0054's safety argument therefore holds per activation, and what the mesh adds is compactness and
an iteration count decided at run time within the bound. The cost falls on the reviewers, who approve every
unrolling at once. **The gate computes, as evidence for the vote** *(drafter's choice)*: the set of activations the
mesh can reach within `N`, and every edge whose guard is identically true. Neither refuses; both say what is being
voted on.

### 3. The activation is the unit of attributes, routes and findings ⟨M2⟩ ⟨M4⟩

A node may run more than once, so ADR-0054's per-node rules are restated per **activation**, a pair (node, *k*), one
round of one cell.

- **Attributes are per activation and written once** (ADR-0054 §4). `found` is still the one attribute that changes
  after the end, and only from false to true.
- **Every activation records its trigger**: the activation it was entered from, or the fan-out it belongs to (§4).
  Its **causal history** is itself and its trigger's history. The log holds both, so the history is a fact.
- **A guard reads the triggering activation and its causal history, and never a node's latest value.** A node's
  latest value can change between a route's decide and its apply, when the node runs again; an activation in a
  history cannot. That is ADR-0054's settled read set, with the dominators of a DAG replaced by the history of an
  activation. ADR-0054's `BasisStable` carries over for that reason; `Mesh.tla` does not restate it.
- **The decision point is the activation's end, the decision event**, and one route consumes it. A route names the
  activation it was decided for, and is void at apply if that activation's event has been consumed or has failed
  (ADR-0054 §8). An older event stays routable until it is consumed, fails, or a finding closes it.
- **A finding closes everything downstream of it.** An edge out of an activation is disabled by a finding against
  any activation in its causal history, not only against itself. On a loop, a finding against the worker's round
  stops the route out of the decision node's next round, though that round is clean. `M_SOURCEONLY` is the rule
  without the history.
- **One live activation per node** *(drafter's choice)*. A node's seating names one principal, and a principal holds
  one live lease (ADR-0031 §2), so a node's next activation waits until its previous one has ended. A node meant to
  run concurrently names a **pool**, a set of seatings, each charged to the budget when used.

### 4. Joins are scoped to a fan-out

An `any` join is unchanged. **An `all` join waits for the branches of one fan-out activation**, matched by that
activation's identity, which every branch carries as its trigger. "All of b and c" has no meaning when b and c may
each run several times; "all branches of this fan-out" does. A branch that can no longer complete is skipped, as in
ADR-0054 §1, and the skip is scoped the same way.

### 5. Lineages alternate on a 2-coloured clear graph ⟨M3⟩

ADR-0054 §6 alternates Thinker lineages along every clear edge. On a mesh, **the clear edges must form a 2-colourable
graph** under the deployment's two lineages, and the promotion tally checks it.

- **An odd cycle of clear edges, or a clear self-loop, cannot be seated with two lineages**, and does not pass the
  tally. A workflow fixes it by making one edge of the cycle a handle edge, or by giving the node on a self-loop two
  seatings of different lineages, used by activation parity *(drafter's choice: both are allowed)*.
- **The check is static**, over the graph and the seatings. Its runtime consequence is that every activation entered
  by a clear edge differs in lineage from its trigger.
- **ADR-0035 is Proposed**, so this is declared and not enforced, under ADR-0054's D-0054-1.

**A cycle repeats what alternation cannot stop.** Alternation keeps a trigger from crossing one clear hop into its
own lineage; it does not stop it reaching that lineage again two hops on, and a loop offers that every turn. Labels
are what bound it. An input's label is its reporter's `clearSet` (ADR-0054 §5), and a `clearSet` only grows, so
**once untrusted content is read on a clear cycle, every later activation on it is labelled untrusted**, and the
routing rule closes the cycle's outward edges for the rest of the run. That is the right direction to fail, and an
honest loop that reads untrusted content once loses its outward reach for good.

### 6. The mechanism tracks; the orchestrator holds no state ⟨M4⟩

"The orchestrator tracks" is met by the mechanism, and the orchestrator is delivered the track.

- **One orchestrator round per decision event**, granted from the budget when the event opens, ended when its route
  applies or its lease lapses. Events are served in log order *(drafter's choice)*. A lapsed round fails its event
  and takes no default edge (ADR-0054 §8).
- **Its Listener delivers the track**: the activation history that bears on the event, as typed facts with their
  labels, the triggers that link them, and handles for every output. **Never bytes**, as in ADR-0054 §3. The
  mechanism writes the delivered track to the round topic as the round's opening record, as ADR-0048 §2 does for
  the tool list, so the orchestrator's Observer reads what the orchestrator was given.
- **The orchestrator keeps nothing between rounds.** Three reasons, each sufficient:
  - **A lease is round-coterminous and lasts at most `T_persona`** (ADR-0031 §2, A2). A long mesh outlives any one
    lease, so a tracking orchestrator would need successor grants inside the workflow, which ADR-0054 §2 excludes.
  - **State carried across rounds is persistent agent memory**, which the corpus puts outside the pattern.
  - **A long-lived orchestrator makes routes ambiguous.** A route decided on one event and committed after the next
    has opened would land on the wrong activation unless it names its own. `M_TRACKER` is that: one lease across
    events and routes that name the node. A route here names both its activation and its round, so it is void if
    either has moved on.

### 7. Event-driven means the mechanism is the bus

The events a mesh reacts to are **entries in the consensus log**: an activation's end and the attributes lifted with
it, a finding, a lease lapse, a fence, a budget refusal. The mechanism applies each one and evaluates what it
enables, in the same apply. **Kafka carries the records and orders nothing** (ADR-0005).

**Workers never address each other.** No variant publishes to another cell or subscribes to anything; a cell's
ingress is the Listener's closed list (ADR-0043 §1, with ADR-0054's `input`); a round topic is read only by the
seats in its watched-by relation. Choreography, in which cells react to each other's events with no one deciding
what runs next, is therefore not supported, and Options says why.

### 8. Completion and failure

A mesh **completes** when an activation of a sink has ended. It **fails** when the budget refuses a grant, when
`T_wf` passes, or when it is **quiescent**: no activation ready or running, no decision event open, no route in
flight, and no sink ended. ADR-0054 §9's other failures apply per activation. Completion still says the graph was
traversed and nothing about whether the work is right.

### 9. Degeneracy on this record

None of its own. ADR-0054's two apply per activation. A deployment that runs only `dag` workflows is not degenerate
on this record; it has not taken the widening.

### 10. The trusted base

The interpreter of ADR-0054 §11 grows by activation records, causal histories, a budget counter and the
orchestrator's track. They enter under the same mechanism row, for the same reason: every decision they make either
subtracts or executes a grant the quorum made, and the budget is what bounds the grant.

### 11. Ratification condition

- **ADR-0054 is ratified first**, or with this record.
- **The dependent entries are drafted**: ADR-0054 §1, §2, §4, §8 and §9, read per activation and with the kind and
  the budget; ADR-0035 §5 (the 2-colour check).
- **A module states the properties and its mutations break them**, by the method of ADR-0046. At least:
  `BudgetBound`, `CompletedOnlyBySink`, `RouteOnItsEvent`, `NoActivationAfterFinding`, `LabelCoversTruth`,
  `TaintedNeverOutward` and `ClearHopsAlternate`, with mutations `M_OVERRUN`, `M_ORCHFREE`, `M_BUDGETDONE`,
  `M_TRACKER`, `M_SOURCEONLY`, `M_FORGETTAINT` and `M_NOLINEAGE`, and witnesses that the loop runs, completes after
  a loop, exhausts its budget, carries taint round the loop and closes the exit, and voids a route on a finding
  upstream and on a stale round.
  **Met for TLC at drafting** ⟨R1⟩: `spec/Mesh.tla` (`a5bc64953c38c9a9`), results
  `spec/Mesh-results-2026.10.07.txt` (`c58fa4b2e8bcc555`), 23 rows from `spec/Mesh-rows.txt`. The seven properties
  hold together (2,142,317 states); the seven witnesses are reached; each mutation breaks the property it is about,
  `M_FORGETTAINT` two (`LabelCoversTruth`, `TaintedNeverOutward`); and every mutation is shown to break nothing
  else. **Two of those remainder rows are bounded.** Under `M_ORCHFREE` and `M_TRACKER` the budget buys more worker
  rounds, and both rows were checked under the state constraint `CapOnePast` (`nAll ≤ N + 1`), the reach
  `M_OVERRUN`'s row has with none: 13,624,749 and 452,432,065 states. Unconstrained, `M_ORCHFREE` had reached 282
  million states with 125 million still queued after eight hours, and was stopped.
  **Not met:** fan-out, scoped joins and pools; serialisation per node; a self-loop with seatings by parity; the
  static 2-colour check, which belongs to the gate; proofs.
- **The gate** computes the reachable activations within `N` and the edges with identically true guards, as evidence
  (§2), and refuses a mesh without a budget and one whose clear edges cannot be 2-coloured.
- **The reference**, with an ADR-0033 addendum: the module's mesh run by scripted cells. **Behaviours, each seen:**
  the loop turns twice and exits; the budget refuses a grant and the workflow fails; an orchestrator round is
  charged; a finding against the worker's round voids the decision node's next route; a route decided in one round
  and committed after it ends is void; untrusted content read on the loop disables the exit.
  **Met in part** ⟨R2⟩: ADR-0033 A16 (pending), the reference at `11aaf6a` (`SHA256SUMS-REF-2026.10.08`). Every
  behaviour is seen as a test of the replicated state named after `Mesh.tla`'s properties, on the module's mesh. **Not
  met**: no mesh is run end to end, and the cells are the tests'; fan-out-scoped joins, pools and parity seatings are
  not built (a mesh with an `all` join does not load). `mechanism/` I49.
- **A reader who is not the drafter** (ADR-0029 A2).

## Options considered

| Option | Disposition |
|---|---|
| **Revise ADR-0054 to a mesh** | Not taken ⟨M5⟩. The DAG stays the default and the simpler record; the mesh is a widening a deployment opts into |
| **Choreography**: cells publish events and subscribe to each other's | Rejected. No variant subscribes, a round topic is read only by its watchers, and no component that is not a model would decide what runs next |
| **Cycles with no budget**, bounded by `T_wf` alone | Rejected. A time bound bounds wall-clock, not leases; the promotion would be a grant of as many leases as fit in `T_wf`, and termination would become a liveness property needing fairness |
| **A budget on workers only** | Rejected. The orchestrator's rounds would be unbounded. `M_ORCHFREE` |
| **Unroll every loop into a DAG**, as ADR-0054 requires | Kept as the default. The mesh is the same behaviours without the unrolling, and the gate's reachable-activation set restores what unrolling showed |
| **Guards that read a node's latest attributes** | Rejected. Not settled: a node that runs again changes them between decide and apply |
| **A finding that closes only its own activation** | Rejected. A loop routes round a finding one hop downstream. `M_SOURCEONLY` |
| **A tracking orchestrator with one long lease** ⟨M4⟩ as first read | Not taken. It outlives `T_persona`, carries memory across rounds, and makes routes ambiguous (§6). `M_TRACKER`. The tracking is kept, in the mechanism |
| **Concurrent activations of one seating** | Rejected by ADR-0031 §2. A pool of seatings is the way (§3) |
| **Alternation by a third lineage on odd cycles** | Not required. It is allowed where a deployment has one; with two, a handle edge or parity seatings fix the cycle (§5) |
| **Unscoped `all` joins** | Rejected. Not well defined when a branch can run more than once |
| **Charging Observers separately** | Not taken *(drafter's choice)*. They ride with their cell; a deployment that wants principal counts multiplies by two |

## Consequences

**Positive**
- **Loops whose length is decided at run time** can be expressed without unrolling, inside a bound the quorum
  voted on.
- **ADR-0054's safety argument carries over** per activation, and no property needs fairness.
- **The delegation position holds**: no principal seats another, and the budget bounds how many seatings a
  promotion can produce.
- **Taint cannot be laundered round a loop**: it only accumulates, and the exit closes.
- **The orchestrator is stateless and still informed.** What it was shown is on its round topic for its Observer.

**Negative**
- **Reviewers approve every unrolling at once.** The gate's evidence helps; it does not make a large mesh easy to
  read.
- **Budget burning.** A Byzantine worker that always reports `retry`, or an orchestrator that always loops, spends
  the budget and fails the workflow. Safe, and paid for in principals.
- **Many principals**: two per activation and two per orchestrator round, up to `N` of each kind of round.
- **Honest loops that read untrusted content lose their outward edges** for the rest of the run (§5). ADR-0054
  finding 6, made stronger.
- **Two lineages constrain the graph further**: odd clear cycles need a handle edge or parity seatings.
- **The mechanism grows again**, on the path every Run grant travels.
- **One author, one day**, and the module is the drafter's.

## Findings

1. **A budgeted mesh is a DAG unrolled to depth `N`.** The budget is not an add-on to the mesh; it is what lets the
   DAG's argument apply to it at all.
2. **The budget must charge the orchestrator.** A bound on worker leases alone leaves the one cell that runs at
   every decision unbounded.
3. **The DAG's settled guards were a property of dominators, and dominators do not survive a cycle.** Causal history
   is what does: the activations that led to this one, recorded by the log and immutable.
4. **"The orchestrator tracks" pulls toward a long lease, and a long lease is three problems at once**: `T_persona`,
   memory across rounds, and routes that land on the wrong activation. Moving the tracking into the mechanism solves
   all three. A route here names both its activation and its round; `M_TRACKER` removes both, and the module does
   not show which alone suffices.
5. **Taint on a loop only grows.** Labels from `clearSet` make that automatic. It is the reason alternation's
   two-hop gap on a cycle is bounded, and the reason honest loops will find their exits closed.
6. **Event-driven and havoc are compatible only with the mechanism as the bus.** Every reading in which cells react
   to each other directly puts a model's output in charge of what runs next.
