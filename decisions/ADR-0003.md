# ADR-0003 — Topic per principal per round, sealed on emit, as the unit of erasure

**Status:** Accepted (genesis) · 2026.09.11 · confirmation required (ADR-0029 C1, for A2 only) ⟨B5⟩
**Status note:** Bundling check run 2026.09.15 — clauses annotated, no split ⟨C1⟩.
**Pending:** none. A1 and A2 ratified 2026.09.25 (Batch 5).

---

**Context.** Erasure obligations require that specific content be destroyed, per subject, at
arbitrary times, provably. Replay requires that nothing ever be removed. Retention expiry
deletes by age rather than by subject; tombstones break gapless ordering and are themselves
disclosures; crypto-shredding leaves a verifying envelope over unreadable ciphertext — a hole
no auditor can distinguish from suppression.

**Decision.** One Kafka topic per principal per epoch/consensus round **[0003.1]**, locked on emit completion
**[0003.2]**. *A topic is identified by its rules-epoch chain (ADR-0027 §4) as well as by principal and
epoch/round. Re-genesis starts a new chain at epoch 0, so a successor chain never writes to, reopens or reuses
the name of a predecessor's topic.* ⟨A1⟩ *A round has one companion topic, `after`, which receives only the
mechanism's records of actions denied under that round's lease after its topic is sealed. It never receives an
emission. It is part of the round's unit of erasure: read under the same ACL (0003.3), retained with the round
(0003.4), and dropped with it. The mechanism bounds how many records it writes to it per round and marks where
it stopped.* ⟨A2⟩ Access by ACL **[0003.3]**. Retention scoped per use case **[0003.4]**. Separate read-only
principals for audit. Replay explicitly authorized or refused **[0003.5]**. Masking and suppression enforced
by wrapper Rego at write time **[0003.6]**. No tiered storage **[0003.7]**. Archive is its own ETL per use
case. ⟨C1⟩

**Consequences.** The topic becomes the unit of erasure and lines up with the unit policy is
pinned to. Deletion is dropping a sealed container, so signatures and hash chains are never
edited. A masking miss is now recoverable with a blast radius of one round. Forces ADR-0005.
Topic count scales with principals × rounds — broker metadata pressure is a real operational
cost and needs a lifecycle story.

**A1 note (ratified 2026.09.25).** The reference build of 2026.09.24 showed this gap live. Two dev resets each started a chain
at epoch 0, round 1, and both wrote into the same Kafka topic (ADR-0033 §7, finding 4). 0003.1 names a topic by
principal and epoch/round, and ADR-0027 §4 restarts both at re-genesis, so on the present wording a successor's
first round names its predecessor's first round. That breaks 0003.2: writing to a sealed topic reopens the unit of
erasure. It also undercuts the re-genesis migration obligation (ADR-0025, G2 §7 item 10). A successor cannot
inherit read access to its predecessor's forensic classes if it cannot tell their topics from its own.

A1 adds a sentence and strikes none. It follows from 0003.1 and 0003.2 read together and narrows neither
clause. It names no scheme: the reference uses the genesis-rules digest as the chain id in a readable name
(`mechanism/` I17), and the HMAC-to-base32 opaque naming in the recovery finding above would carry the chain
equally well. Naming belongs with that recovery candidate and with ADR-0021. Ratifying A1 does not discharge the
migration obligation. It removes one precondition of it.

**A2 note (ratified 2026.09.25).** ADR-0020 makes "actions attempted, including those denied" part of a round's replayable
record, and 0003.2 seals the round's topic when the round ends. The two meet at an action attempted after the seal:
a principal whose round was submitted, expired, revoked or fenced, still holding its token and still trying. That
attempt is the evidence ADR-0020 exists to keep, "the only evidence containment did anything", and after a
revocation it is the most valuable the round has. Under the accepted text it has nowhere to go: the topic is
sealed, and reopening it would undo 0003.2's unit of erasure.

A2 keeps the seal and adds a companion. The companion is scoped to the same subject and round, so erasure still drops
one round's containers and never edits a sealed one; blast radius is unchanged. It carries denials only, so nothing
a principal does can write to it, and it has no seal of its own: it grows only while something keeps trying under a
dead lease. That growth is why A2 carries a bound. A misbehaving agent could otherwise turn its own revocation into
an unbounded write. The bound is a mechanism parameter; past it, one marker records that attempts continued.

Rejected alternatives, recorded 2026.09.25: ordering post-seal denials in the consensus log contradicts ADR-0015;
one forensic topic per chain mixes subjects and breaks the per-subject unit of erasure; local logs only lose the
record where it matters. An attempt with no session names no principal and no round, so it is outside this record:
it is an authentication event at the mechanism's socket, for the replica's security log.
