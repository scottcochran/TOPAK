# ADR-0003 — Topic per principal per round, sealed on emit, as the unit of erasure

**Status:** Accepted (genesis) · 2026.09.11
**Status note:** Bundling check run 2026.09.15 — clauses annotated, no split ⟨C1⟩.
**Pending:** A1 (topic identity spans the rules-epoch chain), drafted 2026.09.25. Not in force; see History.

---

**Context.** Erasure obligations require that specific content be destroyed, per subject, at
arbitrary times, provably. Replay requires that nothing ever be removed. Retention expiry
deletes by age rather than by subject; tombstones break gapless ordering and are themselves
disclosures; crypto-shredding leaves a verifying envelope over unreadable ciphertext — a hole
no auditor can distinguish from suppression.

**Decision.** One Kafka topic per principal per epoch/consensus round **[0003.1]**, locked on emit
completion **[0003.2]**. Access by ACL **[0003.3]**. Retention scoped per use case **[0003.4]**. Separate
read-only principals for audit. Replay explicitly authorized or refused **[0003.5]**. Masking and
suppression enforced by wrapper Rego at write time **[0003.6]**. No tiered storage **[0003.7]**. Archive
is its own ETL per use case. ⟨C1⟩

*Pending ⟨A1⟩, not applied:* 0003.1 would read as scoped to one rules-epoch chain. A successor chain's
topics are distinct from its predecessor's. See History A1.

**Consequences.** The topic becomes the unit of erasure and lines up with the unit policy is
pinned to. Deletion is dropping a sealed container, so signatures and hash chains are never
edited. A masking miss is now recoverable with a blast radius of one round. Forces ADR-0005.
Topic count scales with principals × rounds — broker metadata pressure is a real operational
cost and needs a lifecycle story.

**A1 note (pending).** The reference build of 2026.09.24 showed this gap live. Two dev resets each started a chain
at epoch 0, round 1, and both wrote into the same Kafka topic (ADR-0033 §7, finding 4). 0003.1 names a topic by
principal and epoch/round, and ADR-0027 §4 restarts both at re-genesis, so on the present wording a successor's
first round names its predecessor's first round. That breaks 0003.2: writing to a sealed topic reopens the unit of
erasure. It also undercuts the re-genesis migration obligation (ADR-0025, G2 §7 item 10). A successor cannot
inherit read access to its predecessor's forensic classes if it cannot tell their topics from its own.

A1 adds a sentence and strikes none. It follows from 0003.1 and 0003.2 read together and narrows neither
clause. It names no scheme: the reference uses the genesis-rules digest as the chain id in a readable name
(`mechanism/` I17), and the HMAC-to-base32 opaque naming in the recovery finding above would carry the chain
equally well. Naming belongs with that recovery candidate and with ADR-0021. Ratifying A1 does not discharge the
migration obligation. It removes one precondition of it.
