# ADR-0035 — Persona lineage is a projection of the Build log, and independence is an exclusion rule over it

**Status:** Proposed · 2026.09.25
**Status note:** Writes the criterion ADR-0017's ratification note assigned. Confirmation required under **C4**: it
resolves debts owed by ADR-0017 (the criterion), ADR-0031 (lineage versions) and ADR-0016 §5 (how seat distinctness
is enforced). None of C1–C3 applies to the record as drafted. §5 makes promotion harder and reverses nothing, the
same ground on which ADR-0022 A1 was not C3. **R2 (2026.09.28) is confirmation-required on its own account**, by
custodian instruction: the bounded-share construction of §2 and §3 has no precedent in the corpus, and it is to be
reviewed against best practice for measuring training-corpus overlap before ratification (Open obligations; §8
condition 5). The closest ADR-0029 criterion is C2, since §2 now makes a soundness claim over everything a round
could have read.
**Pending:** none.
**Deployment position:** Archetype. A lineage that can only be declared, not projected, is D-0035-1 (§9).
**Depends on:** ADR-0016 (Persona), ADR-0017 (provenance axis), ADR-0015 (effects are admits), ADR-0022 (`Read`,
corpus root, `Invoke`), ADR-0024 (promotion), ADR-0034 (confinement; Accepted 2026.09.28)
**Relates to:** ADR-0007 (findings subtract), ADR-0008 (fault-domain independence), ADR-0023 (declarations),
ADR-0025 §1(d) (relaxing the policy floor is an issuance), ADR-0030 (the floor and its gates), ADR-0031 (lease)
**Closes:** ADR-0017's ratification-note obligation · ADR-0031's lineage-version question · boundary register §8's
lineage-version item
**Carries a debt against:** ADR-0034, which must be in force before any lineage is better than declared · ADR-0024
(the tally condition, §5) · the archive (lineage retention)

## Context

**What is owed.** ADR-0017 requires two independent lineages at the archetype and ratified that requirement with its
criterion outstanding: "a decidable or auditable criterion for lineage independence, sufficient to reject the
overlapping-corpora case. Until it exists, the archetype may be *described* but no deployment may be *declared* at
archetype rung." ADR-0016 §5 makes seat distinctness a policy predicate "over Persona provenance", but provenance is
a free-text row in ADR-0016 §1's table. ADR-0031 and register §8 leave open whether two versions of one lineage are
independent.

**Why a manifest is not enough.** The obvious criterion compares two training manifests. The SLM session proposes
exactly that: a manifest per run with corpus hash, tokenizer hash, code commit, config and seed. But Build is where
the corpus puts the Byzantine threat. A manifest is written by the party that ran the training, so a gate over
manifests checks what the author claims. That is the vacuous-gate failure one level down. ToO 09.10 §3 already had
the answer: "Lineage is the only state that matters … the lineage DAG is a projection of [the promotion log]."

**What the SLM session showed.** Planning a rehearsal on real public corpora surfaced two ways model influence
reaches a lineage *without* touching its weights:

- **Content generated by a model.** TinyStories was generated by GPT-3.5 and GPT-4 (Eldan & Li, 2023). A model
  trained from scratch on it has in-house weights and external content. It is a distillation.
- **Content selected by a model.** FineWeb-Edu was filtered from FineWeb, which is drawn from Common Crawl, by a
  classifier trained on annotations from Llama-3-70B-Instruct. No token was generated by a model, but a model decided
  which tokens were kept.

Weight ancestry alone would pass both, so the criterion has to see all three routes: weights, generated content and
curation.

**What independence is for.** It serves the Observer. ADR-0017 wants it so that correlated failure cannot defeat
detection. Detection carries no safety theorem (ADR-0016 §2: suitability, not verification), so this record makes a
*precondition* checkable. It does not make an Observer effective, and structural independence does not imply
uncorrelated failures. §6 is the only part of this record that looks at failures, and it can only subtract.

## Decision

### 1. The lineage record is projected from the log, not authored

A **lineage record** is computed by the Build gate from the consensus log. It is not submitted by a principal. Every
Persona record's provenance field (ADR-0016 §1) becomes the digest of its lineage record.

A lineage is the artifact DAG under the Persona's weights. Every node is a Build round that produced an artifact:
corpus preparation, tokenizer training, pre-tokenisation, pre-training, fine-tuning. Each node's record has these
fields:

| Field | Content | Source | Strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| `root` | The digest of the from-scratch initialisation this lineage descends from | The `Invoke` inputs of the pre-training round | Projected |
| `parents` | Records of the artifacts the round consumed | Artifacts `Read` into the workspace, each with its own record | Projected |
| `ingress` | The content-addressed objects the round **could** read | The corpus root in force for the round's admits (the parameters digest carried by every admit), plus the `ingress` of every parent | Projected, over-approximated (§2) |
| `consumed` | The content-addressed objects the round **did** read ⟨R2⟩ | The `ingress` objects `Read` into the workspace, plus authored drafts into the data directory, plus the `consumed` of every parent | Projected, never over-approximated (§2) |
| `generatedBy` | Models whose outputs are in `ingress`, with each one's share | Parents' records; for external objects, the declaration made at corpus admission | Projected, or declared at admission |
| `curatedBy` | Models that selected or filtered `ingress` | The same | Projected, or declared at admission |
| `tokenizer` | Tokenizer digest | A parent artifact | Projected |
| `family` | Architecture family tag, such as `decoder-transformer`, `ssm` or `rwkv` | The trainer configuration | **Declared.** The code digest is projected, and the tag is a claim about it |
| `trainer` | Tool digest, configuration digest, seed | The `Invoke` admit (tool identity per `mechanism/` I13) | Projected |
| `rung` | The lowest confinement rung over every round in the DAG | The leases of those rounds (ADR-0031 `confinement`) | Projected |

**An authored manifest is kept as a cross-check.** The gate compares it with the projection. If they diverge, the
lineage is **Contested** in ADR-0034's sense: the signature is valid and the claim diverges from the evidence. The
Persona is refused promotion, and the evidence is attributed.

### 2. Over-approximation is sound, and weakest-link is the strength

**The projection may over-approximate ingress and must never under-approximate it.** Every exclusion rule in §3 is
monotone in `ingress`, `generatedBy` and `curatedBy`: adding an object a round could have read can turn a pass into
a fail, never a fail into a pass. A trainer that could read the whole mounted corpus root is charged with all of it,
which is conservative. A round whose ingress cannot be bounded at all is not projectable, and §9 applies.

**Ingress enters only numerators** ⟨R2⟩. As first drafted, E3 and the distillation rule were shares *of ingress*, and
a share whose denominator grows with ingress is not monotone: charging a round with more root diluted it (finding 5).
Every share the gate computes is now an **upper bound** on the true one. Matched and generated bytes are measured
over `ingress`, what the rounds could have read; the denominator is the smaller `consumed` set, what the rounds did
read. Adding ingress raises the numerator and leaves the denominator alone. A round with no consumed set has no
bound, is not projectable, and §9 applies. What the bound cannot see is a round that reads more than it trains on.
That is a declared residual (§7), not a soundness gap.

**A lineage is only as strong as its weakest round.** This numbers the ToO's candidate *weakest-link provenance*
(rev 7 §2.3):

- The record's `rung` is the lowest confinement rung over its DAG.
- **A round is ingress-bounded** ⟨R1⟩ when its lease is at rung ≥ 1 on an Attested verdict, under a profile that
  meets ADR-0034 P1 and P2: one channel and no network device, and nothing mounted beyond the workspace and the
  pinned corpus root. Those two clauses are what bound what a round could read. A native tool (P4) or a shell (P3)
  inside such a guest reads only what the guest mounts.
- **Any round that is not ingress-bounded makes the whole record *declared*.** A trainer outside that cage can read
  things no `Read` names, so its projection is attribution, not a bound.
- **The bound is the cage, not `Contained`** ⟨R1⟩. `Contained` requires this record to be in force (ADR-0034 §4 R2),
  so a projection that required it could never be made, and §8's ratification condition could never be met
  (finding 4).
- A projected record therefore needs ADR-0034 in force. Until then, every lineage is declared.

### 3. Independence is an exclusion rule over two records

Lineages *A* and *B* are **independent** only if every clause holds:

| Clause | Rule | Rejects |
|---|---|---|
| **E1** Weights | `root(A) ≠ root(B)`, and neither DAG contains the other's weights as a parent | Fine-tunes, merges, and **every pair of versions of one lineage** (ADR-0031, register §8) |
| **E2** Generators | No model appears in both `generatedBy` sets, and neither lineage's generators include a model of the other lineage | Two lineages distilled from a common teacher, and one distilled from the other |
| **E3** Corpus overlap | Exact overlap, measured over `ingress` and taken as a share of the smaller `consumed` set ⟨R2⟩, is at most θ<sub>exact</sub>. Near-duplicate overlap, computed by a pinned gate tool at a declared granularity over the same sets, is at most θ<sub>near</sub>. Both shares are upper bounds (§2) | **The overlapping-corpora case** ADR-0017 names: two crawls of the same web |
| **E4** Architecture | Different `family` tags and different tokenizer digests | The same design trained twice |
| **E5** Curators | No model appears in both `curatedBy` sets | Two corpora selected by the same classifier |

**Distillation is a provenance fact.** If the known-generated bytes in `ingress` are more than θ<sub>gen</sub> of
`consumed` ⟨R2⟩, an upper bound on the generated share (§2), the lineage is a distillation of its generator. On ADR-0017's axis it sits at rung 1, "in-house weights, external
content", and carries a content-lineage debt alongside rung 1's weight-lineage debt. It stays `Contained` (no prompt
leaves the guest), but it cannot be the archetype.

**Properties of the relation.** It is symmetric. It is not reflexive: nothing is independent of itself. It is **not
transitive**, since A ⊥ B and B ⊥ C say nothing about A and C, so every seat pair is checked on its own.

**Where the thresholds live.** θ<sub>exact</sub>, θ<sub>near</sub>, θ<sub>gen</sub>, the near-duplicate tool's digest
and its granularity are **policy-floor values**. Relaxing any of them is an issuance and a promotion (ADR-0025 §1(d)).
Tightening one is an ordinary promotion.

### 4. The gate computes; the admit path looks up

The rule is computed **once, in Build**, at Persona promotion:

1. The gate recomputes the lineage record from the log and checks it against the authored manifest (§1).
2. It evaluates E1–E5 and the distillation rule against every promoted lineage.
3. It emits an **independence table**: the set of lineage-digest pairs that pass, each marked projected or declared.
   The table is a promoted artifact.

Nothing semantic reaches the admit path. A check there is a lookup of a pair of digests in a promoted table, which
is a syntactic lift (ADR-0007). This is the division ADR-0030 already uses: decidable analysis in the gate, and
evaluation in the admit path.

**Narrowing the table is subtraction.** Removing a pair is an ordinary promotion. Adding a pair happens only through
a recomputation by the gate.

### 5. Independence is enforced where refusal stalls, never at grant

**Not at grant.** Refusing an Observer its lease because it is correlated with the author leaves the seat empty. An
unavailable Observer vetoes nothing (ADR-0024 open obligations), so the refusal would *remove* oversight and add
authority to the author. ADR-0016 §5's "enforceable rather than advisory" cannot be delivered at grant.

**Enforcement sits at the two places where a refusal stalls:**

- **In the promotion tally (Build).** A promotion round completes only if at least one veto seat was leased for the
  round with a Persona whose lineage is independent of the author's, according to the table. Otherwise the promotion
  stalls: PC/EC, and unavailability costs a stalled pipeline. This also discharges part of ADR-0024's veto-seat
  outage obligation, since an *independent* veto seat must be seated, not just defined. **The text belongs in
  ADR-0024** as an amendment. This record states the rule and carries the debt. Whether the rule lives as a mechanism
  constant or a floor entry is settled there.
- **In the deployment declaration.** Archetype provenance can be declared only if every (Thinker, Observer) seat
  pair allowed by the Personas' seat bindings (ADR-0016 §1) is in the table as *projected*. The check runs at the
  Build promotion gate that already reads declarations (ADR-0023 §7b). A refused declaration is a lower claim, not
  lost authority.

**Run has no tally.** Observer independence in Run is enforced only through the declaration until something in Run
can stall on it. That is stated as a limit, not deferred.

### 6. The behavioral test only subtracts

Structure stands in for what the Observer needs: failures that are not correlated. A **correlated-miss test**
measures that directly, on a seeded suite of planted defects:

- The suite is a Build artifact with its own lineage record. **It must satisfy E3 against both lineages under test**,
  so that "held out" is checked rather than asserted. The projection makes this possible.
- A pair **fails** when its joint miss rate exceeds β times the product of the individual miss rates. β is a floor
  value.
- A failure is a finding. It removes the pair from the independence table, which is a subtraction (§4).
- **A pass grants nothing** (ADR-0016 §3). A finite suite can refute independence and cannot establish it.

### 7. Declared residuals

These are outside the criterion and are stated on its face:

- **Unlabeled model text in web crawls.** Web text after about 2022 contains model output that no dataset labels.
  `generatedBy` sees only declared generators, so E2 is blind below the declaration.
- **Shared human feedback.** Preference data and annotator pools are not modeled. Two lineages aligned by the same
  annotators can be correlated in exactly the judgments an Observer makes. A `feedback` field is an open obligation.
- **Shared priors below the dedup granularity.** Language, facts and conventions common to all text. No threshold
  removes these, and none should try.
- **Shared training stack.** The framework, kernels and hardware. MLX on Apple silicon for both lineages of the
  rehearsal is an example.
- **Reading more than is trained on** ⟨R2⟩. The denominator of every share is what a lineage's rounds `Read`. A pair
  whose rounds read objects they never train on grows both consumed sets and lowers the bound; both lineages must be
  padded, since the smaller set is the denominator. The log shows the reads and not the training, so the criterion
  cannot see it, and only §6 can, by subtraction. The rehearsal shows how often the move helps on random records
  (`slm/gate-suite-results-2026.09.28.txt`). The alternative it cannot move, absolute bytes, was not taken (Options).

### 8. The rehearsal, from the SLM session

The SLM session sized from-scratch training on an M4 with 24 GB at 10–50M parameters on 0.2–1B tokens, from hours to
a few days per model. That is enough to rehearse this record end to end. It is **evidence for the pipeline and the
thresholds, not for the guarantee or for seat capability.** An M4 is not a confidential VM, so the best it can reach
is rung 1 if the trainer runs under a launcher that asserts a profile, and every record is otherwise declared (§2).
Nothing at this size is a usable Observer.

Suggested lineages. Each row is a pass that must pass or a fail that must fail, like the gate suite's cases:

| Pair | Lineages | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| **Pass** | L-A: Llama-style, FineWeb-Edu slice, own 16k BPE · L-B: SSM or RWKV, a non-web, non-synthetic corpus such as public-domain books, own tokenizer | Independent (E1–E5 hold) |
| **E1** | L-A and L-A′, a fine-tune of L-A | Fail |
| **E3** | L-A and L-C: an unfiltered FineWeb slice, of which FineWeb-Edu is a subset | Fail. This is the overlapping-corpora case, reproduced on real data |
| **E4** | L-A and L-D: Llama-style on the books corpus | Fail on E4 alone, while E3 passes |
| **E2** | Two lineages on synthetic sets that name a common generator | Fail |
| **Distillation** | L-T on TinyStories | Provenance rung 1 (content from GPT-3.5 and GPT-4) |
| **E5** | Two lineages on corpora filtered by the same classifier | Fail |

**The thresholds need corpora, not training.** E3's overlaps can be measured without training anything: FineWeb
against FineWeb-Edu, and FineWeb-Edu against the books corpus. Those measurements are the first evidence about what θ
values real corpora allow. That evidence should come *before* any number is written into the floor.

**The projection needs the mechanism, but not big models.** Runs of about 1M parameters for about 10 minutes are
enough, if each run goes through the reference as Build rounds: `Read` shards into the workspace, `Invoke` the
tokenizer and trainer, `Submit`. The projected record is then compared with the authored manifest. The reference
supports these actions today. The MLX trainer is a native tool, so these principals are `Mediated` (ADR-0034
D-0034-3), but that alone does not make the records declared ⟨R1⟩: under a rung-1 launcher whose profile meets P1
and P2, the rounds are ingress-bounded (§2) and the records are projected. Run at rung 0, as on a bare M4, they are
declared. The rehearsal still tests every step except the guarantee.

**Ratification condition:**

- Gate-suite cases for E1–E5 and the distillation rule, over synthetic records, each seen to fire.
- A property check that adding to `ingress`, with `consumed` fixed, never turns a fail into a pass (§2) ⟨R2⟩.
- The E3 overlap measurements above, reported with the θ values proposed from them.
- At least one lineage whose record is projected from the reference's log and matches its manifest.
- A review of the bounded-share construction (§2, §3) against best practice for measuring training-corpus overlap,
  by someone other than its author, recorded in this record ⟨R2⟩.

## Options considered

| Option | Disposition |
|---|---|
| **Authored manifests, checked at the gate** (the SLM session's shape) | Rejected as the evidence, kept as a cross-check (§1). The author of a manifest is the Byzantine party of the inversion |
| Weight ancestry only | Rejected. It passes TinyStories and FineWeb-Edu, where a model is in the lineage through data |
| Behavioral correlation as the criterion | Rejected as a precondition (ADR-0016 §2, §3). Kept as a subtract-only finding (§6) |
| Enforce at grant, refusing a correlated Observer | Rejected (§5). An empty veto seat vetoes nothing, so this adds authority to the author |
| A transitive independence relation, such as lineage classes | Rejected. Independence is pairwise, and classes would hide a failing pair inside two passing ones |
| One overlap threshold, exact hashes only | Rejected. Near-duplicates are how crawls overlap. Exact overlap is kept as the cheap first clause |
| Unknown generator treated as shared with everything | Rejected. All web text would fail E2. Kept as a declared residual (§7) |
| Thresholds as mechanism constants | Rejected. They are policy, and relaxing them must be an issuance |
| Architecture family derived from code | Not taken. No syntactic test says two codebases are "the same design". The tag is declared, and the code digest is projected |
| Shares of the smaller ingress set and of ingress (as first drafted) ⟨R2⟩ | Withdrawn. Not monotone in ingress: the rehearsal's property check turned 13 of 2,000 E3 fails and 241 of 2,000 distillation fails into passes by adding ingress (finding 5) |
| Absolute matched and generated bytes against byte thresholds ⟨R2⟩ | Not taken. Monotone and immune to padding (§7), but not scale-invariant: the rehearsal's cases separate by orders of magnitude as shares (1.0, 0.11, 0.002, 0) and a byte threshold set at one corpus size says nothing at another. Kept in the gate suite as `--mode absolute` |
| A bounded share with an absolute backstop ⟨R2⟩ | Not taken. It would close the padding residual at the cost of three more floor values. The custodian chose the bound with the residual declared |

## Consequences

**Positive**
- **ADR-0017's archetype becomes declarable in principle**, and the overlapping-corpora case is rejected by a named
  clause.
- **Lineage stops being free text.** ADR-0016 §1's provenance field is a digest of a record the author cannot write.
- **The version question is closed.** Two versions of one lineage are never independent (E1).
- **Model influence through data is visible**: generation and curation, not only weights.
- **"Held out" is checkable** for any evaluation suite, not only §6's.
- **Nothing semantic reaches the admit path.** It sees a table lookup.

**Negative**
- **The archetype may be unreachable in practice.** Pre-training corpora overlap heavily through Common Crawl. A θ
  strict enough to mean something may exclude every affordable pair. That is the honest result, and ADR-0017 already
  says almost nobody can afford the archetype.
- **Every lineage is declared until ADR-0034 is in force.**
- **The rule is only as good as its residuals** (§7), and shared human feedback is a large one.
- **The bound can be diluted by reading more than is trained on** (§7) ⟨R2⟩. The log cannot tell the two apart.
- **Promotion can stall** when no independent veto seat is seated. That costs availability, by design.
- **Run is enforced only by declaration** (§5).
- **Training rounds are long.** A multi-day pre-training round needs lease renewal throughout and a round bound
  large enough for it.

## Findings

1. **ADR-0016 §5 cannot be enforced where it reads as if it would be.** Seat distinctness as a grant-time policy
   predicate removes oversight when it refuses. Enforcement moves to the tally and the declaration (§5).
2. **ADR-0017's open question "is rung 1 one rung or two?" has a second dimension.** External lineage can enter
   through weights (a fine-tune) or through content (distillation). This record puts both at rung 1 with separate
   debts. ADR-0017 may want to split them.
3. **The SLM session's manifest is the right shape in the wrong role.** As evidence it is authored. As a cross-check
   against the projection, it is what produces a *Contested* lineage.
4. **As first drafted, this record could not be ratified** ⟨R1⟩. §2 made any round by a `Mediated` trainer declared,
   and ADR-0034 §4 (R2) makes every principal `Mediated` until this record is in force. So no lineage could be
   projected before ratification, and §8 requires one. §2 now bounds a round by its cage, P1 and P2 at rung ≥ 1,
   which is what the over-approximation argument needed all along: what a round could read depends on the cage, not
   on the occupant.
5. **As first drafted, §2's monotonicity claim was false for E3 and the distillation rule** ⟨R2⟩. Both were ratios
   with ingress in the denominator, so the over-approximation §2 called conservative helped a pair pass. The
   rehearsal's property check found it (`slm/README.md` finding 1, `slm/gate-suite-results-2026.09.25.txt`): 13 of
   2,000 random E3 trials and 241 of 2,000 distillation trials turned fail into pass by adding ingress. R2's bound
   restores the claim. The 2026.09.28 rerun holds over 2,000 trials in `bounded` mode and still fails in `ratio`
   mode. R1's cage argument rested on the same claim and was written the same day without checking it against the
   rehearsal, which had already refuted it.

## Open obligations

- **The θ values, θ<sub>gen</sub> and β.** Set from the §8 measurements, not before.
- **The near-duplicate tool.** Choice, parameters, granularity and digest, as a pinned gate tool.
- **ADR-0024 amendment** carrying §5's tally condition, and the bootstrap `Promotion` module modeling it.
- **Corpus admission.** The declaration schema for `generatedBy` and `curatedBy` on external objects, and who may make
  the declaration.
- **A `feedback` field** for preference data and annotator pools (§7).
- **Run-side enforcement** of Observer independence, beyond the declaration.
- **Lineage retention.** Records and the log slices they are projected from belong in the archive (`mechanism/` G9).
- **The §6 suite.** Construction, the planted-defect classes, and how often it runs.
- **Best-practice review of the bound** ⟨R2⟩. The bounded-share construction is the custodian's design, not drawn from
  published practice. Before ratification, review it against how training-corpus overlap and evaluation
  contamination are measured in practice (n-gram decontamination of the kind used for GPT-3's evaluations, the
  MinHash deduplication FineWeb uses and the near tool already borrows, and the deduplication literature, Lee et al.
  2022 among it), and record whether a standard, monotone measure exists that should replace or accompany it. A
  ratification condition (§8).
