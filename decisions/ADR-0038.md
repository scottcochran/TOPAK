## Context

ADR-0008 was accepted on 2026.09.12 with two decisions in one record. The first, its title, is about colocation:
Observer replicas never share a device with the Thinker or with each other. The second, ratified by its acceptance
note, is about scoping: the Observer is "a shared replicated group rather than per-agent", which gives about 9 model
instances instead of about 40.

ADR-0023 then found that neither scoping dominates. Shared Observers see cross-principal timelines that per-principal
Observers cannot. Per-principal Observers bound detection latency and injection blast radius, and a shared group
cannot. Under ADR-0023 §4, a variation that has a capability the archetype lacks shows that the archetype is
incomplete, so the archetype has to have both: **per-principal Observers plus a cross-principal correlator.**
ADR-0023 §5 therefore reversed ADR-0008's scoping in effect, and said a superseding record was required. G4 item 6
carries it as unwritten.

Four things have kept it unwritten, and ADR-0037 settles or enables three of them.

- **"Per-principal" had no referent.** Until ADR-0037, an LTRO was said to be one principal with an Observer inside
  it. Now a cell is a Thinker plus its `observer` seats, each a principal. "Per-principal Observers" means one
  `observer` seat per cell.
- **The correlator was a noun.** The 09.16 review: it has "no record, no alphabet, no placement rule, no seat on the
  promotion roster, and no statement of what it reads". ADR-0037 §6 gives it a kind to use, `observer`, with no new
  machinery.
- **Replicas assumed a quorum.** ADR-0008's consequences argue that "a Byzantine quorum with co-resident replicas is
  arithmetic fiction". This makes sense only if Observer replicas vote to reach a verdict. Since ADR-0007 and
  ADR-0024 §2, no Observer output admits anything. A deny needs no quorum: denials are local.
- **The veto seats have to be re-homed.** ADR-0024 §5 gives veto seats to "the independent Observer replicated group
  (ADR-0008)", which is the group being superseded. ADR-0024 is a bootstrap record. ADR-0023's carried-scope row
  warns that after bootstrap, changing the holder would reach a bootstrap record through an ordinary promotion.
  **Genesis has not ended, so the change can still be made as a genesis amendment**, and doing it now avoids that
  path.

## Decision

### 1. The archetype has an Observer per cell and a correlator across cells

| Observer | Seat | Watches | Reads | Scope of a compromise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cell Observer** | One `observer` seat per cell (ADR-0037 §2) | Its cell's Thinker | The Thinker's round topic, payloads included, under the ACL (0003.3) | One cell's detection |
| **Correlator** | One or more `observer` seats that belong to no cell | Every cell in its domain | §3 | Cross-cell detection only; every cell Observer still works |

**The correlator is ADR-0008's shared group under a new name and with a narrower job.** It keeps the cross-principal
view that made the shared group incomparable with per-principal Observers (ADR-0023). It no longer carries each
cell's own detection, which is the part that made one injected Observer a fleet-wide blind spot.

Both are principals with leases, topics and Personas (ADR-0037 §1). Both hold `observer` alphabets (ADR-0037 §4).
Both reach the world only through findings that the mechanism turns into denies, quarantines or evictions (ADR-0007).
**A correlator finding may name any principal in its domain.** That is the one thing it can do that a cell Observer
cannot, and it is still deny-only.

**The correlator's round.** A lease is granted for one round and ends with it (ADR-0031 §2). The correlator's round
is a **correlation window** that the deployment declares, leased and topic-scoped like any other round. Its state
across windows is its own derived store (finding 3).

### 2. The watched-by relation is the ACL

For a principal `p`, **`W(p)`** is the set of `observer` seats whose lease holds a read authorization on `p`'s
current round topic (0003.3). `W(p)` contains `p`'s cell Observer and the correlators of `p`'s domain. It is a fact
in the mechanism's state, evaluated by lookup, and it is syntactic in ADR-0007's sense.

This is the relation ADR-0039 is written against. Because ADR-0039 depends on the relation rather than on how it is
populated, a later change to scoping does not reopen coupling, and a change to coupling does not reopen scoping.

### 3. What the correlator reads

At the archetype the correlator reads **everything the shared group could**, since ADR-0023 §3 makes the archetype
the maximum:

- **Facts** from every cell in its domain: admits and denials with their principal, lease, epoch and per-principal
  sequence number (`mechanism/` G15's `topak-seq`, extended to denials, finding 2); cell Observers' findings as
  control-plane facts (ADR-0018, ADR-0004); **the Reader stage's facts, the conformance verdict and the
  distributional distance, for every Persona that declares `topk_logprobs`** (ADR-0037 §3 R3) ⟨R1⟩; and lease grants,
  renewals, revocations and seals. The correlator is in `W(p)` for every `p` in its domain, so it receives what the
  cell Observer receives; a cross-cell drift in distance is the substitution signal no single cell can see.
- **Typed action envelopes**: the variant, its reference, target or tool, and the principal. Which fields of `Draft`
  and `Invoke` fall on the control-plane side is still ADR-0022's open follow-up. The correlator reads whatever that
  follow-up places there.
- **Payloads** of every cell's round topics, under the ACL.

**A facts-only correlator is a permitted narrowing**, recorded as the mitigation on D-0038-2's declaration (ADR-0023
§6). It gives up cross-cell semantic detection, the fragmentation case of Makins et al. (the 09.16 review's CR-3).
In exchange, an injection in one cell's content cannot reach the correlator except through a fixed-schema fact. A
deployment may take that trade. The archetype does not, because the archetype does not give up capability.

### 4. Placement: ADR-0008's colocation rule, carried forward

ADR-0008's decision sentence stands, restated for seats: **no `observer` seat shares a device with a Thinker it
watches, and no two `observer` seats in `W(p)` share a device.** The reason no longer rests on a quorum. It is ADR-0008's
first reason: a shared device means shared driver, firmware, memory error domain, thermal and power events, and host
kernel. That is correlated failure plus an effect path through device memory that the model does not include, and
both hold whether or not anyone votes.

**The Reader stage may still colocate** (ADR-0037 §2). It was never an independent fault domain, and it is no longer
a seat.

### 5. Observers combine by any-of, and there is no Observer quorum

"Consensus for grants, local for denials." Every Observer output is a denial or a trigger for one, so **several
Observers watching one principal combine by any-of**: a finding from any member of `W(p)` takes effect, and no
agreement is sought. ADR-0008's replicas become additional `observer` seats in a cell or additional correlators,
each a principal with its own lease.

- **One cell Observer per cell is the archetype value** (ADR-0037 D-0037-1 counts down from it). More is a
  deployment choice. Each additional seat can raise detection if its lineage is independent, and each also adds
  another way to raise a false denial. **More Observers never lower safety and may lower availability.** That is the
  monotone direction.
- **A Byzantine Observer can deny, quarantine or evict inside its reach and nothing more.** For a cell Observer the
  reach is one cell. For a correlator it is its domain, which is why its findings are attributed and why ADR-0024's
  consequence "one compromised seat is a veto until the anchor reissues the roster" applies to it as written.

### 6. The veto roster holds cell Observers and correlators

**ADR-0024 §5's veto-seat holder becomes: every cell Observer seat and every correlator seat in the Build domain.**
This record states the rule and **carries the amendment to ADR-0024 as a debt**, as ADR-0035 §5 did for its tally
condition. Both amendments should be made together and ratified during genesis (Context).

- A veto seat's alphabet is unchanged: `Vote(deny)` only (ADR-0024 §5; ADR-0037 §4's tightened B6).
- **ADR-0035 §5's tally condition** ("at least one veto seat leased for the round with a Persona independent of the
  author's") is met by the author's cell Observer, by construction under seat distinctness, or by a correlator whose
  lineage is independent of the author's.
- A veto seat's silence is still not a veto (ADR-0024 §2). Whether the author's cell Observer must have *read* the
  round before promotion completes is a coupling question, and it belongs to ADR-0039 §3.

### 7. Degeneracy on this record

| Id | Kind | Parameter → deployed value | Premises falsified | Theorems voided | Type changes (ADR-0023 §6b) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D-0038-1** | Declaration | Cell Observers: one per cell → none, with the correlator kept. **This is ADR-0008 as ratified** | Every cell has an Observer (ADR-0037 D-0037-1, fleet-wide) | **No safety theorem.** Each cell's detection falls to the correlator, so one compromised correlator blinds every cell; the bounded injection blast radius is lost. Under ADR-0039, coupling has nothing per cell to couple to | No participant changes type |
| **D-0038-2** | Declaration | Correlators: 1 or more → 0 | Some Observer reads across cells | **No safety theorem.** Cross-principal detection is lost: the fragmentation and multi-agent coordination cases (CR-3) | No participant changes type |

**Composition note (ADR-0023 §7a).** D-0038-1 with D-0038-2 leaves no Observer at all. No theorem is lost to
composition beyond the union, since neither degeneracy voids a safety theorem. Every detection claim is lost, and in
Build nothing promotes (ADR-0035 §5). D-0038-1 is D-0037-1 declared for every cell with a correlator retained. It
does not need a separate note against D-0037-1.

**On the schema** ⟨R2⟩. Every row above writes "none" under *theorems voided* and states its loss in prose, because
ADR-0023 §6's schema has no field for a checkable claim that is not a safety theorem. ADR-0037 finding 6 names the
gap and proposes a *properties lost* field; it is routed there, and this record does not amend the schema.

### 8. Ratification condition

- **ADR-0037 is ratified first, or together with this record.** This record's seats, kinds and cells are ADR-0037's.
- **The ADR-0024 amendment (§6) is drafted**, and ADR-0035 §5's tally amendment is drafted with it.
- **A TLC check**, as a module with small constants (two cells, one correlator):
  - **`DenyByAnyOf`**: a finding from any member of `W(p)` blocks `p`'s next admit. Mutation `M_QUORUM`: a finding
    takes effect only with a majority of `W(p)`. It must fail, and the failure shows that a quorum lets one
    compromised majority suppress an honest finding.
  - **`ReachIsBounded`**: a cell Observer's finding never blocks an admit outside its cell. Mutation `M_REACH`: cell
    Observers get domain-wide reach. It must fail.
- **The reference** (with an ADR-0033 addendum, sharing ADR-0037's): `SeatDecl` of kind `observer` gains an optional
  cell, which is empty for a correlator. The dev rules seat one cell Observer per author seat and one correlator, and
  both are on the veto roster. A test shows a correlator's `Vote(deny)` counts as a veto and its `Vote(grant)` is
  refused.

## Consequences

**Positive**
- **ADR-0008 is superseded, not just reversed in effect.** One of the two supersessions in G4 item 6 is written.
- **The correlator is no longer only a noun.** It has a seat kind, an alphabet, a placement rule, a place on the
  veto roster and a stated read set. What it *computes* is still unspecified (finding 1).
- **A compromised Observer's reach is bounded and stated**: one cell for a cell Observer, and cross-cell detection
  only for a correlator.
- **The quorum argument is retired.** It had become false once findings were deny-only, and any-of is what "local
  for denials" gives.
- **The veto roster is re-homed during genesis**, avoiding the post-bootstrap path the carried-scope row warned about.

**Negative**
- **ADR-0008's instance economics are gone.** Its acceptance note counted about 9 shared instances against about 40
  per-agent ones. The archetype pays for the per-agent count plus correlators. It is the archetype, and nobody ships
  it (ADR-0023, "Paid").
- **The archetype correlator reads every cell's payloads**, so it is the one place where an injection in any cell
  can arrive. The bound on blast radius is that a compromised correlator costs only cross-cell detection, not that
  it cannot be compromised.
- **Any-of raises the false-denial rate** with every Observer added. False denials cost availability, never safety.
- **C3, author-ratified until confirmed**, like ADR-0023 whose reversal it completes.
- **One author, and no options discussion.**

## Findings

1. **The correlator has inputs but no function.** This record says what it reads and what it may do. It does not
   say what it looks for: the probe-then-exploit signature, fragmented intent, or a chain-depth term (CR-3). That is
   detection engineering, and like every Observer it is deny-only, so it can be specified later without changing
   anything here.
2. **ADR-0015 says denials need not be ordered, and a correlator needs their order per principal.** One agent tests
   the boundary and another uses what it learned. A per-principal sequence number on denials is enough. It is not
   consensus ordering, and ADR-0015's claim about effects is untouched. The reference already numbers admitted
   emissions (G15). Routed to ADR-0015 as an addition.
3. **The correlator's derived store crosses erasure units.** A round topic is the unit of erasure (ADR-0006). The
   correlator's state, built from many rounds' payloads, holds shadows of all of them, the same concern ADR-0019
   raised for retained logprobs. Routed to ADR-0006: either the store is keyed by round and dropped with it, or it
   holds facts only.
4. **ADR-0021 §5 cites ADR-0008 for a "replicated" Build Observer.** Under §5 here, replication is any-of seats.
   ~~ADR-0021 is Proposed and should be revised in place when it is next opened.~~ Revised at R3 (2026.09.29) and
   R5, and ratified 2026.09.30 (Batch 18) ⟨B18⟩.
