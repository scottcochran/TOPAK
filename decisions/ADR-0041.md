## Context

The draft floor has carried Run since 2026.09.24 in five entries: two permits (R1, R2), one coupling forbid (R3, added
by ADR-0039), the break-glass exception (X1), and the seat-kind forbid (B10, added by ADR-0037). Every one is written
over `RunInvoke` or `RunEmit`. ADR-0040 retires those names and adds a variant the floor has never bounded.

Two questions are this record's to answer. **What bounds `Consult`?** A variant with no envelope is a permit-all on
its action, which ADR-0030 §10 finding 2 showed the non-vacuity gate catches per action and only per action. **Does
the coupling cover it?** ADR-0039 finding 2 assumed every Run variant would be an effect. `Consult` is not one.

### 2. Coupling is on effects, and `Consult` is exempt

**`R3-observer-live` refuses `Actuate` and `Report` under a `thinker` seat when no cell Observer is live. It does not
name `Consult`.**

- **What R3 protects is a bound on what an Observer has not yet read.** `live(o)` tests a backlog (ADR-0039 §1,
  condition 3). A `Consult` adds nothing to it: it emits nothing and nothing crosses outward.
- **The Observer loses nothing by reading it late.** The admit is in the log and the object is content-addressed, so
  what was consulted can be reconstructed at any later time from the digest.
- **Build already works this way.** Build's `Read` is never coupled; only its promotion is (ADR-0039 §3).
- **A principal with a lagging Observer can still read and cannot act.** That is the direction that fails closed, at a
  lower cost in liveness than refusing both.

**The exemption rests on the pinning, and the two are one decision.** A `Consult` that could reach a live source would
be egress and would need the coupling. So the gate run carries a negative control: a floor whose `R4` omits the
Run-root condition must be caught (§7), ~~by the non-vacuity gate~~ **by the routing check, which classes it a
relaxation** ⟨R1⟩. Non-vacuity cannot see it: with the root conjunct gone, the domain and alphabet conjuncts still
refuse requests, so `always-allows` stays refuted.

**ADR-0039 §2's first sentence is amended to match**: "an admit under a Thinker's lease in Run" reads "an
effect-bearing admit, `Actuate` or `Report`, under a Thinker's lease in Run". The amendment is a debt of this record.

### 3. Break-glass reaches `Actuate` and nothing else

X1 adds authority: under a quorum on the break-glass roster, a Run seat may `Actuate` a tool outside its allowlist and
inside the issued break-glass envelope. **The envelope is `Actuate` only.** No break-glass grant reaches `Consult`,
`Report`, `Vote`, `Submit` or `Erase` (ADR-0026 R7; ADR-0013's mutation M2).

**R3 applies to break-glass too**, as it does today. A forbid denies whatever permit is satisfied, so a break-glass
`Actuate` with no live cell Observer is refused. Break-glass widens what may be actuated. It does not widen who may go
unobserved.

*Pending ⟨A2⟩, not applied. **X1 is retired, and break-glass is an emergency seating** (ADR-0052, Proposed; the
custodian's decisions, 2026.10.04). This section is replaced. There is no break-glass permit: a quorum on the
break-glass roster seats a named person on a seat of kind `emergency` for one round, on a bundle revision whose
allowlist is the emergency envelope, and that seat's `Actuate` is admitted by `R1-actuate-allowlisted` like any
other. The floor's exceptions are one, erasure.*

- *`R3-observer-live` is unchanged, and does not reach an emergency seat: it is a forbid on a `thinker` seat. The
  custodian's decision that break-glass does not wait on a dead Observer is kept with no exemption in R3.*
- *`B10-only-thinkers-author` reads "the seat's kind is not `thinker`, and, for `Actuate`, not `emergency`".*
- *An emergency seat holds `Consult`, `Actuate` and `Report`. It cannot vote, draft or submit, which is what
  ADR-0026 R7 asked of a break-glass grant.*

*Pending ⟨A1⟩, not applied. **R5 to R7 apply to break-glass too**, for the same reason R3 does: a forbid denies
whatever permit is satisfied. Break-glass does not widen what an injected Thinker may send out. For the forbids to
have something to read, a tool in the issued break-glass envelope is an entry in ADR-0049's form (drafter's choice);
one that is not is treated as outward, its result as `untrusted`, and every input as a destination.*

*Pending ⟨A2⟩, not applied. With X1 retired the paragraph above reads over the emergency seat: R5 to R7 are forbids
on `Actuate` whatever the seat's kind, so they bind the person holding it; the envelope is an ordinary allowlist of
ADR-0049 entries, so the fallback for a tool with no entry has nothing to apply to. This sentence follows ADR-0052
and takes force only if ADR-0047's A1 is in force too.*

### 4. The schema

Schema S names three Run actions in place of two.

- `Actuate` applies to a `Seat` and a `Tool`; `Report` to a `Seat` and a `Topic`; `Consult` to a `Seat` and a
  `CorpusObject`. Each takes `RunCtx`.
- `RunCtx.corpusRoot` is renamed `runRoot` *(drafter's choice)* and is read by `R4`. No other field changes:
  `toolAllowlist`, `breakGlass` and `observerLive` stay as they are.
- The schema is the typed lift's type (ADR-0030 §4). A request naming a retired action is a validation error, and any
  error is a deny (ADR-0030 §1).
- *Pending ⟨A1⟩, not applied. `RunCtx` gains `clearSet` and `inputLabels` (ADR-0047 §4), and the lift supplies, for
  the tool named, whether it is outward and, for its destination inputs, which are literals and the labels of those
  that are handles (ADR-0030 A2). `Report` takes `clearSet` and no entry reads it yet.*

### 5. The bundle tier narrows, and only subtracts

The floor bounds which tools. It does not bound their arguments. **Constraints on a tool's inputs belong to the bundle
tier**, where `tool_invocation.rego` already matches them, and where a policy can only subtract (ADR-0030 §1). The
bundle's input names the action by the new verbs.

This is where an argument-level vocabulary goes, if a deployment wants one. It is a narrowing inside `Actuate`'s
envelope, so it cannot add authority, and it needs no floor change.

*Pending ⟨A1⟩, not applied. **The bundle tier also carries the label declarations and the delivery table**
(ADR-0047 §1, §2; ADR-0049 §7). Suppressing an item only subtracts. Delivering more in the clear is not bounded by
`bundle ⊑ floor`, which is decided over variants; it is bounded by R5 to R7, which read a larger `clearSet` and
refuse more (ADR-0047 §8). A bundle may add rules over `clearSet` and `inputLabels`, and can only narrow.*

### 6. What is not in the floor, and what is inherited

| Not in the floor | Where it is | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Epoch fencing | The mechanism, ahead of the floor (ADR-0022 §2) | It is not a request property |
| The round bound and the sequence | The mechanism (ADR-0042 §2) | Counts, not envelopes |
| The `Actuate` record | The mechanism (ADR-0042 §3) | An effect of an admit, not a condition of one |
| A tool's declared channel | The allowlist entry, and the Run profile (ADR-0042 §6) | An obligation on each entry, as hermeticity is in Build |

**D-0039-1 is inherited.** A deployment that drops the coupling drops `R3-observer-live` and the cross-round grant
condition together, and loses `UnreadBounded`. After ADR-0042 §2 that property counts invocations as well as reports,
so the loss is larger than ADR-0039 §7 states it. The row is ADR-0039's to restate.

| Option | Disposition |
|---|---|

## Consequences

**Positive**
- **Every Run action has an envelope**, and the non-vacuity gate has three actions to check in place of two.
- **ADR-0039 finding 2 is answered**, with a reason for the one variant that is not coupled.
- **A principal whose Observer lags can still read.** The stall falls on effects only.
- **Argument-level policy has a home** that cannot add authority.

**Negative**
- **`R4` is a new permit, and a new permit is a relaxation.** The same check that classes the unpinned `R4` a
  relaxation (§7) is the one that would route this permit once a floor is in force ⟨R1⟩. Once a floor is in force, adding it is an anchor
  issuance and a promotion under ADR-0030 §5's routing. Before genesis it is an edit to a draft.
- **An accepted record's quantifier is amended** (ADR-0039 §2).
- **The exemption is only as good as the Run root.** A root that admits an object whose content is a live pointer
  reopens the read the pinning closed. What may enter the root is not decided here.
- **One author.**

## Findings

1. **R3's id names a forbid and R1, R2 and R4 name permits.** The numbering is by order of arrival and not by kind. It
   is left alone because ids are cited.
2. **ADR-0039 §2's title says "every admit".** The title of an accepted record is not amended by a dependent entry;
   the amendment is to the section's first sentence, and the title is read with it.
3. **The floor cannot tell a read-only tool from any other.** Both are `Actuate` and both are coupled. A deployment
   that wants cheaper reads of live state has no entry to relax, which is intended.
