# ADR-0012 — The default-deny bundle is the policy floor: pre-blessed, resident, verified

**Status:** Accepted (genesis) · 2026.09.12
**Status note:** Amended same day to reflect its status under ADR-0010.

---

**Context.** Accepted ahead of its parent (ADR-0010), on an independent availability argument:
fail-closed has to work under partition and at cold start, which rules out any dependency on a
bundle-fetch path. That argument stands regardless of where verification locus landed. ADR-0010
subsequently made the resident bundle the verified floor, which is a promotion in status rather than
a change of mechanism — the same artifact, now carrying a proof obligation it did not previously
have.

**Decision.** The default-deny bundle is pre-blessed, staged and resident: instantiate rather than
fetch. It is the policy floor of ADR-0010 — verified with the enforcement mechanism rather than
promoted as a parameter, and shared by Build and Run through the same `Contain` instantiation.

**Consequences.**

- Fail-closed is reachable with no network dependency, at cold start and under partition.
- The floor is the one artifact verified rather than promoted, so Build's recursion terminates on
  it. Its staging obligation in Build is therefore not ordinary artifact promotion — see the
  floor-authorship record (item 4 under ADR-0010).
- It must stay minimal, per ADR-0010. Pressure to push operational policy into the resident bundle
  for availability reasons is pressure on the floor, and must be resisted on those grounds rather
  than on size.
- Vacuity, totality and discrimination gates apply to it directly (ADR-0007, extended). A vacuous
  resident bundle is the silent-failure case with the widest blast radius in the system.
