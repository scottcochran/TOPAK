# ADR-0062 — The lease pre-authorizes: a governor on each vehicle permits each entry under it against an exact count, every entry declares an extent, and a vehicle that stops hearing its plane loses its lease at once

**Status:** Proposed · 2026.11.10
**Status note:** Confirmation required under **C1**, because a governor's permit is a path every effect below the
intent boundary travels, and under **C2**, because §1 claims the governor is the only path to a vehicle's actuators
and radio, which is effect exclusivity for a vehicle. **C3** does not apply while this is an overlay ⟨Q11⟩ and would
apply on admission: §2 gives up ADR-0015's ordered admit before every effect, and §6 gives up ADR-0033 §4's void,
for the entries a governor permits. **Modelled, checked and proved in TLA+ ⟨R1⟩ (§11); nothing is built.**
**Pending:** none.
**Deployment position:** **An overlay, not the archetype** ⟨Q11⟩. One degeneracy (§9).
**Depends on:** ADR-0059 (the chain a governor verifies), ADR-0060 (the autonomous plane and its one grant),
ADR-0061 (its replicas), ADR-0064 (ticks, by which a vehicle hears its plane and an extent is counted), ADR-0031
(the lease), ADR-0049 (the allowlist entry), ADR-0058 (overlays; Proposed)
**Relates to:** ADR-0015 (an ordered predecessor for every effect), ADR-0033 §4 (void), ADR-0034 A2 (channel
fidelity), ADR-0039 and ADR-0041 (coupling), ADR-0024 (a timeout is never a grant), ADR-0063, ADR-0065, ADR-0066
**Closes:** nothing.
**Carries a debt against:** nothing while this is an overlay. §10 lists what the archetype's records would owe.

---

## Context

The custodian gave the vehicles a disconnected alphabet ⟨6⟩, then shrank what a vehicle keeps when it is alone ⟨14⟩,
then asked whether the time cost of Byzantine agreement is paid only for elective coordination, with pre-authorized
effects always available ⟨21⟩. As the proposal's first revision stood, the answer was no: the autonomous plane
ordered every admit before its effect, so every act waited for a round. The custodian chose, among three, that **the
lease pre-authorizes**, and that going alone ends it ⟨22⟩.

**"Fully Byzantine" holds of the stack and cannot hold of the vehicle.** Something on board must check a chain and
gate the actuators, and that part is trusted.

---

## Decision

### 1. A vehicle is a governor and a stack

The **stack** is the autonomy software: planning, perception, any model. It is the Thinker, and it is Byzantine.
The **governor** is a trusted component between the stack and the vehicle's actuators and radio. It is mechanism and
not a principal. The governor:

- verifies the chain (ADR-0059 §5) and holds the lease and the vehicle's key;
- hosts the vehicle's replica of the autonomous plane (ADR-0060 §3);
- evaluates the floor in Cedar and each bundle on the chain as a Wasm module (ADR-0067);
- **permits each `local` entry under the lease, and counts it** (§2);
- **lifts its own facts**: elapsed time, position, the lease's count, an entry's extent. No safety decision reads a
  clock a principal supplies (ADR-0032 §3), and by the same rule none reads a position the stack supplies;
- hosts each tool as a Wasm component with one capability, its declared channel, which for a vehicle is an
  actuator interface (ADR-0034 A2);
- **holds the radio.** The link to the plane and the peer topics (ADR-0065) are both on its side, so a stack
  cannot make its own vehicle alone;
- writes the record (§6), and carries out hold and the terminal behaviour (§7).

### 2. One round of the plane per lease, and none per entry ⟨21⟩ ⟨22⟩

The plane orders the grant: the vehicle, the alphabet, the envelope, a **count** of entries and a `ttl` in ticks.
The governor then permits each entry under the lease, against the floor, the bundles, the envelope and the count,
with no further round. **The count is exact.** It is the governor's own arithmetic over its own acts.

| Waits for a round of the plane | Waits for none |
|---|---|
| A lease, once | A `local` entry under a live lease |
| An entry marked `ordered` (§3) | A report to a peer topic (ADR-0065) |
| A tick, once per period | Listening, and applying a relayed fence or revocation |
| A seal, a heartbeat, a finding or a revocation, for the record | Any refusal |
| A step of the shrink schedule | Hold, and the terminal behaviour |

**An effect's ordered predecessors are the intent and the lease.** Two things still depend on the plane completing
rounds: a lease is usable only while ticks are heard (§5), and a vehicle's next lease is a round. Neither is on the
path of a pre-authorized act.

### 3. An entry is `local` or `ordered`, and the archetype marks none `ordered` ⟨Q20⟩

The allowlist entry (ADR-0049) gains a sixth part, `effect`:

| Field | Says |
|---|---|
| The class, with its `T_irrev` | `reversible` or `irreversible` (ADR-0066) |
| How it is permitted | `local`, by the governor; or `ordered`, by the plane before the effect |
| Its extent | §4 |
| Whether it is the beacon | §5. At most one entry per seat kind |
| While unattended ⟨R2⟩ | For an entry whose `T_irrev` is finite: `permit` or `refuse`, while the plane is unattended (ADR-0060 §5). On that section's third option, an entry that does not declare it does not load |

**The bundle marks an entry `ordered` where the tally wants agreement across vehicles**: a count that spans
vehicles, an admit that can be void, an Observer checked at each act. That entry waits for a round. **The archetype
marks none** ⟨Q20⟩, and the tally marks them per mission. No irreversible entry is `local`.

**The class and `T_irrev` are both declarations, and nothing relates them** ⟨R2⟩. An entry is `reversible` because
the bundle says so, as an effect class is a declaration (A15), and no rule derives the class from `T_irrev` or checks
one against the other. No floor entry reads `T_irrev` itself. `FD15` reads what an entry declares for unattended
operation (ADR-0067 §3), and the load check ties that declaration to a finite `T_irrev`. What ADR-0032 §4's budget
means for a `reversible` entry while the plane is unattended is ADR-0060 §5's open question, and the drafter
recommends its third option, under which the `While unattended` part decides, entry by entry.

### 4. Every entry declares an extent, and the governor holds when one ends ⟨18⟩ ⟨Q14⟩

An **extent** says how far one entry runs: one act; a number of ticks; or a distance or a region, which the governor
measures.

- **An entry with no extent does not load**, as a bundle whose allowlist is not well-formed does not (ToO §2.10).
  Without an extent a count bounds nothing: one entry could run for ever.
- **When an extent ends and no entry follows, the governor holds** ⟨Q14⟩. Hold is fixed at seating, as the terminal
  behaviour is. The stack does not choose it, and the lease stays live.
- **An extent that is not a count of ticks is a measurement**, and the governor's position source is a trust root
  for it (ADR-0068).
- **An extent is a declaration**, as an effect class and a label are (A15). One declared shorter than the act is
  seen by nothing here.

### 5. A vehicle that stops hearing its plane loses its lease at once ⟨14⟩ ⟨22⟩ ⟨Q15⟩

A vehicle **hears its plane** when it has applied the plane's log up to a tick that, by its own clock, is not yet
overdue (ADR-0064 §5). A vehicle that does not is **alone**.

- **Going alone ends the lease.** The governor ends it where it stands, and the plane orders the end when it learns
  of it, as ADR-0031 E1 has expiry: local, and then ordered.
- **A vehicle that is alone keeps three things**: its terminal behaviour (§7); **listening**, so that a relayed
  fence or revocation still applies and a tick heard again ends the state; and **a beacon**, where the rules of
  engagement permit one, a report of fixed schema, of facts the governor measures, counted per lease.
- **It permits no actuation.** So a stack gains nothing by being alone.
- **The beacon is an effect** ⟨R1⟩ (ADR-0015, ADR-0065 §2). After a lease ends a vehicle holds or carries out its
  terminal behaviour, and may send a beacon within its count. The proposal's "the only effects are hold and the
  seating's terminal behaviour" (its §10) is true of actuation, and is read that way here.
- **A vehicle that hears its plane again may be leased again** under the same intent, charged to its budget ⟨Q15⟩.

### 6. The record, and what can be void

- **Under a lease** the governor writes each entry it permits or refuses, and each report it sends or delivers, to
  a chain on the vehicle: hash-linked, signed with the vehicle's key, numbered by the per-lease sequence (ToO §4.2).
- **At the lease's end, the seal.** The governor closes the chain, and the plane orders it as one entry and runs the
  mechanism checks at that position.
- **Void works for an `ordered` entry** (ADR-0033 §4). **Nothing a governor permitted is void**: the effect has
  happened. A check that fails at the seal, or above, is a **finding**, and it can only subtract.
- **A captured vehicle's chain is not evidence.** It says what its captor signed.

### 7. An ending grants nothing

"A timeout is never a grant" (ADR-0024). A body in motion still has to do something when its lease ends.

- **A lease ends** when its count is spent and its last entry's extent has run, at its `ttl`, when the plane ends or
  revokes it, when a fence arrives, and at once when the vehicle becomes alone.
- **The terminal behaviour is part of the seating**, voted with it: hold, return on a voted route, land or stop.
  The stack does not choose it and does not carry it out. A recovery function in the governor's trust domain does.
- **Each vehicle class declares a safe state**, and reaching it in bounded time is an obligation on whoever builds
  the vehicle. "A stall that never ends is safe" (ToO §12 item 21) is false of a vehicle in flight.

### 8. Coupling moves to the grant, and where an Observer sits is declared ⟨Q9⟩

`R3-observer-live` refuses `Actuate` under a `thinker` seat with no live cell Observer (ADR-0041).

- **For an `ordered` entry it applies as written.**
- **For a `local` entry it applies at the grant.** A lease's count is at most `K`, and a vehicle's next lease is
  granted only once its cell Observer has read the last one to its seal (ADR-0039). So at most `K` of a Thinker's
  effects are unread by its Observer at any moment, which is the bound ADR-0039 states. What is given up is the
  refusal in the middle of a lease.
- **`K` is ADR-0039's, and bounds a count, not a time** ⟨R2⟩. It is the policy floor's value, not a new one. It
  bounds how many of a Thinker's effects are unread, and not how long: the Observer reads at the seal, so detection
  latency `L` runs at least as long as the lease. Here a lease's count plays the part of ADR-0039's round bound `R`,
  and it is bounded by `K` instead of lying above it, so ADR-0039's `K < R` check has no counterpart for a lease.
  The plane's own bound, `K_up` (ADR-0063 §3), must exceed `K`.
- **This bound is stated and not modelled** ⟨R2⟩. No module states that a lease's count is at most `K`, or that the
  next lease waits for the Observer's read to the seal: `spec/Intent.tla` and `spec/Alone.tla` take the count as a
  constant, and `FD14-count-within-K` is not written (§11).
- **The mesh's promotion declares where each cell's Observer sits** ⟨Q9⟩, and the claim follows the declaration:

  | Declared | What it gives | What it costs |
  |---|---|---|
  | **On another vehicle** | No hardware shared with the Thinker it watches | The governors' radios carry the round's payloads to it, and the loss of that vehicle holds up the watched vehicle's next lease |
  | **On the same vehicle** | No dependence on a radio or on another vehicle | It shares hardware and fate with its Thinker, and no claim rests on it |

### 9. Degeneracy on this record

**D-0062-1: a stack that reaches an actuator or the radio by a path that is not the governor.** The stack is
`Mediated`, and every property in ADR-0062 to ADR-0066 is void for that vehicle. This is A1 for a vehicle.

### 10. Overlay position, and what is reported upstream ⟨Q11⟩

Reported under ADR-0058 §6: **a trusted component that permits, away from the replicas**; and **an entry's extent**.

**If the archetype takes them, its records owe:** ADR-0015, its sentence on predecessors, and ADR-0033 §4, void,
each for a `local` entry, which is C3; ADR-0039 and ADR-0041, coupling at the grant; ADR-0049, the `effect` part;
ADR-0031, a count in the lease and an ending when its holder stops hearing the mechanism; ADR-0034 A2, the radio as
a declared channel.

### 11. Ratification condition

- **ADR-0059 to ADR-0061, and ADR-0064, are ratified first**, or with this record.
- **A module** states at least `EveryEffectHasALease`, `AlonePermitsNothing`, `ExpiryGrantsNothing` and
  `EntryEndsAtExtent`, with `M_NOCOUNT`, `M_ALONEACTS`, `M_STACKCHOOSES`, `M_NOEXTENT` and `M_NOWATCHDOG`. And one

## Options considered

| Option | Disposition |
|---|---|
| **An ordered admit per entry** | Not taken ⟨22⟩. It keeps void and coupling at each act, and makes a round of the plane the tempo of every vehicle |
| **A lease usable when alone** | Not taken ⟨22⟩. Its duration would rest on one clock again, and nothing could reach it |
| **An alone alphabet with actuation entries**, the first revision's `disconnected` mark | Not taken ⟨14⟩ |
| **A fixed class of `ordered` entries at the archetype** | Not taken ⟨Q20⟩ |
| **End the lease when an extent ends with no successor** | Not taken ⟨Q14⟩. The governor holds |
| **A vehicle that was alone stays out until the plane is linked** | Not taken ⟨Q15⟩ |
| **An Observer always on another vehicle** | Not taken ⟨Q9⟩. It is declared per mission |
| **A governor checking `R3` at each act**, against the marks in the plane's log | Not taken. A Thinker would wait on its Observer's heartbeat, which is a round of the plane, on the path of a pre-authorized act |
| **The radio on the stack's side** | Rejected. The stack would decide when its vehicle is alone |

## Consequences

**Positive**
- **A pre-authorized act waits for no round.**
- **The count is exact**, so what a lease can do is bounded with no clock (ADR-0063).
- **Being alone gains a stack nothing.**
- **The grain of an entry is stated**, which the swarm reading's Z1 asked for.

**Negative**
- **No ordered admit before every effect.** Nothing a governor permitted can be voided, and no Observer is checked
  in the middle of a lease.
- **Any act by a vehicle that is alone is given up.** An adversary that cuts one vehicle from its plane gets its
  terminal behaviour within a tick period.
- **The trusted base grows on every vehicle**, and it now makes every `local` permit.
- **An Observer on the same vehicle supports no claim**, and one on another vehicle can stall its Thinker by
  vanishing (the swarm reading, Z4).
- **One author, one day.**
