# ADR-0002 — Apply the membrane in `Build` as well: `Build(Contain) → Run(Contain)`

**Status:** Accepted (genesis) · 2026.09.12
**Status note:** Amendment 2 ratified 2026.09.15 ⟨A2⟩.
**Pending:** none. E1 ratified 2026.09.23 (Batch 1); A1 ratified 2026.09.23 (Batch 2).

## 09.11 proposed text

**Context.** The published inversion is that the Byzantine threat lives in Build, where agents
author the envelope they will later be judged against. Build therefore has contained agents.
Moon & Varshney ~~§4.2~~ §4.6 and Appendix B ⟨E1⟩ provide the empirical failure: silent abstention, vacuously true
predicate, green gates.

**Decision (pending).** Either apply `Contain` at both domains, making it a reusable component
rather than a third domain and making the Build recursion structural; or assert that Build's
containment differs because its commit point is a human signing key rather than an emission,
and the regress terminates at a declared axiom set.

**Consequences.** Option 1 unifies the pattern and strengthens the recursion claim already made
("Build is a DAG with its own TOPAK"), at the cost of a larger proof surface and a harder
story about who signs. Option 2 is defensible but must be stated as a claim; left implicit it
reads as an oversight. Silence is the only wrong answer.

## 09.12 acceptance

**ADR-0002 — Accepted (2026.09.12).** Decision: apply `Contain` at both domains.
`Build(Contain) → Run(Contain)`, with Build's commit point ~~a human signing key~~ a quorum decision
by principals (ADR-0024), on rules issued by the trust anchor (ADR-0025) and bounded by the rules
floor (ADR-0026) ⟨A1⟩, and the
trusting-trust regress terminating at a declared axiom set rather than a proof.

Rationale at review: Build at Gated HITL and Contain at HOOTL are not competing answers — they
describe different things in Build. The agents drafting specs and Rego inside Build are under
mechanism enforcement (HOOTL, `Contain`); ~~the *promotion* gate above them is a human signing key~~
promotion is a quorum decision by principals (ADR-0024), on rules issued by the trust anchor
(ADR-0025) and bounded by the rules floor (ADR-0026). At archetype custody (A-0025), the anchor's
key-holders are at the gate ⟨A1⟩
(HITL). Option 1 and Option 2 were never exclusive.

Consequences recorded at acceptance:

- **ADR-0001's decision text is now stale.** Amend it to the symmetric form before ratifying.
- **ADR-0007's totality checks are promoted.** They were a work item; with contained agents in
  Build they're a required Build gate. Same for Moon & Varshney's G1/G2/G3 — those become the
  enforcement mechanism *inside* Build's membrane, not just promotion gates above it.
- **`Contain` is now a component, not a domain.** The mapping table needs it listed once and
  instantiated twice.
- **Build's membrane has no effect stream.** ADR-0003's round-topic scheme is the unit of erasure
  in Run. Build's unit is an artifact, not a round. New open-list material rather than a blocker.

## Amendment 2 — Evictability test ⟨A2⟩

**Ratified 2026.09.15** (Batch A, standalone). Added; nothing in this record is struck.

**A principal is anything evictable by an authority other than itself. Whatever is not is at the
gate.** Resignation is not eviction.

**Rationale.** The unqualified test misclassifies any participant removable only with their own
consent. The motivating case is a unanimous anchor key-holder (A-0025), but the correction stands
without it. This amendment is also the carrier for the system-under-test / system-testing cut at
B14 (floor-rollout session, 09.13).

> **Recovery finding.** The evictability test ("if you can be evicted, you are a principal; if you
> are the authority that evicts, you are at the gate") appears in the 09.12 checkpoint §1 and the
> principal-roles session, **not** in this record's accepted text. Amendment 2 (ratified 09.15)
> therefore adds the restated test rather than replacing a sentence in this record.
