# ADR-0049 — The allowlist entry has one schema: identity, inputs, interface, labels and channel, each marked checked or claimed

**Status:** Proposed · 2026.10.03
**Status note:** Confirmation required under **C4**: it discharges ADR-0040's open obligation on the entry's schema
and the records the reference's I13 and I33 say are owed. **C2 applies to §5** ⟨R1⟩: the entry is the one statement of the channel a Run tool is held to, which is part of how
ADR-0034 A2's channel fidelity is realized. The rest of the record makes no confinement claim.
C1 and C3 do not apply: no privileged path is touched, and nothing ratified is reversed; ADR-0040 §3 and ADR-0022 §3
say what an entry must declare, and this record says in what form.
**Pending:** none.
**Deployment position:** Archetype. No new degeneracy: an entry that lacks a part is the degeneracy of the record that
asks for the part (D-0047-1, D-0048-1; ADR-0034 A2's D-0034-5 and D-0034-6).
**Depends on:** ADR-0040 §3 and ADR-0022 §3 (what an entry is), ADR-0030 §4 (bundle data and schema S), ADR-0044 (the
bundle's signature)
**Relates to:** ADR-0034 A2 and ADR-0042 §6 (the channel and who holds a tool to it), ADR-0047 (labels, destinations,
handles), ADR-0048 (the interface and the tool list), ADR-0045 T-0045-24 and T-0045-25 (the declared channel; the
tool host), ADR-0041 (the bundle tier's checks)
**Closes:** nothing until ratified. On ratification: ADR-0040's open obligation on the entry's schema; `mechanism/` I13
and I33; the two-declarations half of G19.
**Carries a debt against:** ADR-0040 §3 and ADR-0022 §3 (dependent entries pointing here) · ADR-0047 and ADR-0048 (each
defers to this record for the entry; done at their R7 and R2) · ADR-0034 A2 (ratified 2026.10.04, Batch 30, at its R4, which states §5's rule in A2's own text ⟨R3⟩: its "declared target" is this
record's `channel.target`, read from the entry, §5) · ADR-0033 (an addendum: the bundle data and the two bundle policies move to
this form)

---

## Context

ADR-0040 finding 4: "The allowlist entry is where the vocabulary grew. A three-variant alphabet moves Run's variety
into what an entry declares. That declaration has no schema yet." Since then the entry has been asked for more, by
three texts, and the reference invented a form to keep going (I33).

| Asked by | What it asks the entry for | State |
|---|---|---|
| ADR-0022 §3, ADR-0040 §3 | A name and a digest; for Run, the effect channel it opens | Accepted; the form is open |
| ADR-0034 A2 | The declared target a Wasm tool's one socket is bound to | Pending |
| ADR-0047 | The result's label; what it releases; which inputs are destinations; what each input accepts by handle | Proposed |
| ADR-0048 | Purpose text and a note per input | Proposed |

Two things are already loose at the reference.

- **"Allowlisted by name and digest" names no digest** (I13). The reference uses the digest of the binary's digest
  joined with the canonical input schema, and no record says so.
- **A Run tool's channel is declared twice**, in bundle data and in the node's tool configuration, and nothing checks
  that the two agree (G19; CR-33).

Three amendments to the same two sections would each restate the entry. This record states it once.

## Decision

### 1. One entry, five parts, stated here and nowhere else

**This record is the definitive statement of the allowlist entry** ⟨R1⟩ (the custodian's decision). Its fields, their
forms, what the digest covers and the checks that make an allowlist well-formed are stated here. Another record says
what it needs of the entry and cites this one. Where another record's text describes a field, a form or a load check
differently, this record governs, whichever was ratified first.

An allowlist is a map in bundle data from an entry's key to its entry. **The key is `name@digest`**, as at the
reference.

| Part | Fields | Domain | Asked for by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Identity** | `name`, `digest`, `domain` | Both | ADR-0022 §3; ADR-0040 §3 |
| **Inputs** | For each input: `type`, `required` | Both | The bundle tier (ADR-0041 §5) |
| **Interface** | `purpose`; for each input, `note` | Both | ADR-0048 §1 |
| **Labels** | `result.label`, `result.releases`; for each input, `destination`, `accepts` | Run | ADR-0047 §1, §3, §7 |
| **Channel** | `channel.kind`, `channel.target` | Run | ADR-0040 §3; ADR-0034 A2 |

- **An entry is for one domain** *(drafter's choice)*. `domain` is `build` or `run`. An `Invoke` naming a Run entry
  and an `Actuate` naming a Build entry are refused. Today one map serves both and the domain is implied by whether a
  channel is present.
- **A Build entry has no channel and no labels.** A Build tool is hermetic (ADR-0022 §3), so a declared channel on a
  Build entry is a contradiction and the bundle does not load. Labels follow if Build's `Read` is ever labelled
  (ADR-0047 §9); the part is defined for both domains so that nothing here has to change then.

### 2. Identity: what the digest is

**An entry's `digest` is the SHA-256 of the tool artifact's digest joined with the canonical form of the entry's
inputs part** *(drafter's choice: this adopts the reference's I13)*. It is re-checked on every call.

- **It covers what the tool is and what it takes.** A change to the artifact or to an input's name, type or
  `required` is a new digest and a new key.
- **It does not cover the interface, the labels or the channel.** Those are declarations about the tool. They are
  covered by the bundle's revision and signature (ADR-0044), which every grant names. A corrected description or a
  changed label is a promotion and not a new tool.
- **So a Thinker's call is decided against two things it can name or was given**: the entry's key, and the revision
  its grant carries.

### 3. Inputs: types, and the form of a handle

Inputs stay flat, as at the reference: each input has a `type` of `string`, `number` or `boolean`, and `required` is
true or false. An unknown input is refused. Nested inputs, ranges and formats are not decided here.

**A handle has one form** *(drafter's choice)*. It answers ADR-0047 finding 10.

| Names | Form |
|---|---|
| The result of an earlier `Actuate` in this round | `{"handle": {"actuate": <seq>}}` |
| A consulted object | `{"handle": {"consult": "<digest>"}}` |

- **A handle cannot be mistaken for a literal.** Every literal input is a scalar. A handle is an object with the one
  reserved key. The validator tells them apart by shape, with no judgment.
- **An input may be given as a handle only if its `accepts` is not empty**, and only if the handle's label is within
  it (ADR-0047 §3). The item a handle names must match the input's `type` when the tool host resolves it; a mismatch
  is a refused call, recorded as one.
- **A handle names an item in the same round and for the same principal.** A sequence number from another round
  resolves to nothing.

### 4. Each field is checked or claimed, and the entry says which

The value of one schema is that a reader can see, field by field, what stands behind it.

| Field | Read by | Checked or claimed |
|---|---|---|
| `name`, `digest` | The admit path; the tool host | **Checked** on every call |
| `domain` | The admit path | **Checked** at admit |
| `type`, `required` | The bundle tier | **Checked** at admit |
| `purpose`, `note` | The Listener, which renders them into the tool list | **Claimed.** Prose that passed promotion |
| `result.label` | The Listener, at delivery | **Claimed**, and bounded: an outward entry must carry `untrusted` (ADR-0047 F4) |
| `result.releases` | The label join (ADR-0047 §1) | **Claimed**, and bounded: a reserved name needs the issued rules to name the entry (F5) |
| `destination` | The lift, for ADR-0047 F2 and F3 | **Claimed.** An input that carries a destination and is not marked is not seen |
| `accepts` | The admit path | **Checked** at admit, against the handle's label |
| `channel.kind` | The tool list; the record | **Claimed.** Recorded and not enforced (ADR-0034 A2) |
| `channel.target` | The tool host | **Checked** where the host binds the tool's one socket to it (ADR-0034 A2, in force since Batch 30 ⟨R3⟩). **Claimed** for a native tool (G19) |

**"Outward" is derived and never declared.** An entry is outward when it has a channel whose target the issued rules
do not list as inside the deployment (ADR-0047 §7). A bundle cannot mark its own entry as inside.

### 5. One declaration of the channel

**The entry's `channel` is the only statement of where a Run tool may connect** ⟨R1⟩ (the custodian's decision). The tool host
takes the target from the entry of the granted revision when it instantiates the tool. Node configuration says where
a tool's module is and says nothing about its channel.

- **This closes the two-declarations half of G19.** There is no second statement to disagree with the first.
- **It does not give channel fidelity an owner.** Whether a tool is held to its target is ADR-0034 A2's, in force since Batch 30
  ⟨R3⟩. For a native tool the target remains an assertion.
- **A2's "declared target" is this field.** A2 says a Wasm tool has "a connection to its declared target". The
  declaration is the entry's, and the tool host reads it there. The reference today reads it from node configuration
  (I44), and is to follow.

### 6. What makes a bundle's allowlist well-formed

A bundle whose allowlist fails any of these does not load. Each is a check on the data's shape and needs no judgment.

| | The bundle is refused when | From |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | An entry's key is not its `name` and `digest`, or its `domain` is missing | §1 |
| **W2** | An input lacks `type` or `required`, or the type is not one of the three | §3 |
| **W3** | `purpose` is empty, an input has no `note`, or either is over the size bound | ADR-0048 §1 |
| **W4** | A Run entry has no `channel`, or a `channel` lacks `kind` or `target` | ADR-0040 §3; I33 |
| **W5** | A Build entry has a `channel` | §1 |
| **W6** | A Run entry has no `result.label`, or any label name is outside the bundle's vocabulary | ADR-0047 §1, F6 |
| **W7** | An outward entry's `result.label` lacks `untrusted` | ADR-0047 F4 |
| **W8** | `result.releases` holds a reserved name and the issued rules do not name the entry | ADR-0047 F5 |
| **W9** | An outward entry takes inputs and marks none as a `destination`, without saying it has none | ADR-0047 F6 |
| **W10** | `accepts` or `destination` is on an input the entry does not have | §3 |

- **W9 needs a way to say "none"** *(drafter's choice)*: an outward entry carries `destinations: none` at the entry's
  top level when no input is one, so that an omission and a decision look different.
- **W7 to W9 are how ADR-0047's F4 to F6 are checked.** ADR-0047 §7 says what the floor must refuse at load and why.
  This table is the definitive form of the check ⟨R1⟩.

### 7. The sibling data the entry's names refer to

An entry's label names mean nothing without the vocabulary they come from. ADR-0047 puts three things in bundle data
beside the allowlist, and this record names where *(drafter's choice)*, without deciding their content.

| Data | What it holds | Decided by |
|---|---|---|
| `labels.vocabulary` | The finite set of names; the two reserved names are always in it | ADR-0047 §1, §7 |
| `labels.delivery` | For each seat kind, the table from label to `clear` or `handle`, total over the vocabulary | ADR-0047 §2 |
| `labels.root` | The Run root's label, for the root or a named subset | ADR-0047 §1 |

### 8. An entry, as an illustration

Not decision text. The reference's two entries, in this form.

```json
{
  "search@d3": {
    "name": "search", "digest": "d3", "domain": "run",
    "purpose": "Search the internal index. Returns up to `limit` results, each a title and a snippet.",
    "inputs": {
      "query": { "type": "string", "required": true,  "note": "The search terms.", "destination": false, "accepts": [] },
      "limit": { "type": "number", "required": false, "note": "Maximum results; default 10.", "destination": false, "accepts": [] }
    },
    "destinations": "none",
    "result": { "label": ["untrusted"], "releases": [] },
    "channel": { "kind": "https", "target": "search.internal:443" }
  },
  "tlc@d1": {
    "name": "tlc", "digest": "d1", "domain": "build",
    "purpose": "Model-check a TLA+ specification against a configuration. Returns the checker's verdict and trace.",
    "inputs": {
      "spec":     { "type": "string",  "required": true,  "note": "Workspace-relative path of the module." },
      "config":   { "type": "string",  "required": true,  "note": "Workspace-relative path of the configuration." },
      "workers":  { "type": "number",  "required": false, "note": "Worker threads." },
      "deadlock": { "type": "boolean", "required": false, "note": "Whether to check for deadlock." }
    }
  }
}
```

`search` carries `untrusted` here because, with no list of inside targets in the issued rules, every entry with a
channel is outward (ADR-0047 §7). A deployment that lists `search.internal` as inside may drop the name.

### 9. Ratification condition

- **It is ratified with, or after, at least one of the records that asks for a part.** The identity, inputs and
  channel parts stand on accepted text and may be ratified alone; the interface and labels parts take force with
  ADR-0048 and ADR-0047.
- **The dependent entries are drafted** ⟨R2⟩, each pending and marked inline: ADR-0040 A1 and ADR-0022 A3 point here
  for the entry's form; ADR-0030 A2 makes W1 to W10 a mechanism check at load; ADR-0025 A2 issues the two lists W7
  and W8 read; ADR-0034 R3 points A2's "declared target" at `channel.target`.
- **The reference**, with an ADR-0033 addendum: `policies/data.json` in this form; `tool_schema.rego`,
  `tool_invocation.rego` and `actuate.rego` reading it; W1 to W10 refused at load, **each seen** with a bundle built to
  fail it; the tool host taking a Wasm tool's target from the entry (§5).
- **No module is owed for the schema itself.** Its checks are on data at load. The properties that read these fields
  are stated by ADR-0047's module and ADR-0034 A2's.
- **A reader who is not the drafter** (ADR-0029 A2).

## Options considered

| Option | Disposition |
|---|---|
| **Three amendments**, one per asking record, each to ADR-0040 §3 and ADR-0022 §3 | Rejected, by the custodian's decision that there is one schema |
| **One amendment to ADR-0040 §3**, carrying the whole schema | Not taken *(drafter's choice)*. The entry serves Build as well, so it would also amend ADR-0022 §3; two of the three asking texts are not in force, and an amendment to an accepted record that depends on Proposed ones is hard to ratify in parts. A record of its own can be |
| **Put the schema in ADR-0048**, the last of the three to be written | Not taken. The interface is one part of five, and the channel part is older than either labelling record |
| **A digest over the whole entry** | Not taken *(drafter's choice)*. A corrected description would then be a new tool, and every Persona and fixture naming the old key would be refused for a change in prose |
| **A digest over the artifact alone** | Not taken. The reference binds the inputs in (I13), so that a tool cannot be called under a schema it was not promoted with |
| **Declare `outward` in the entry** | Rejected. A bundle would mark its own entries as inside. It is derived from the issued rules (§4) |
| **Keep the channel in node configuration and check it against the entry** | Rejected at R1, by the custodian's decision. Two statements and a comparison is more to get wrong than one statement |
| **Let each asking record state its own fields, with this record as a summary** | Rejected at R1. A summary and its sources drift. This record governs (§1) |
| **Handles as a string with a reserved prefix** | Rejected. A literal string could carry the prefix, and telling them apart would need a rule about content |

## Consequences

**Positive**
- **ADR-0040's oldest open obligation has text**, and the reference's invented schema (I33) has a record to follow.
- **"By name and digest" names a digest** (I13).
- **One place says which fields are enforced and which are assertions** (§4). A reviewer at promotion can see what
  they are being asked to take on trust.
- **The channel is declared once** (§5).
- **A handle has a form** the validator can recognize by shape (§3).
- **Three records point at one schema** and stop restating it.

**Negative**
- **The entry is large.** A Run entry now carries five parts, and most of the new fields are claims.
- **Most of the labelling fields are assertions with a floor under them**, not checks. §4 says so; it does not fix it.
- **Parts take force at different times** (§9). Until ADR-0047 and ADR-0048 are ratified a reader must know which
  rows of §6 are in force.
- **§5 asks the reference to change how the Wasm tool host learns its target**, on a path that A2, in force since Batch 30 ⟨R3⟩,
  describes one way and the reference built another (CR-33).
- **Every existing bundle is in the old form.** The move is a bundle change and a policy change together.
- **One author, one day.**

---

## Findings

1. **Seven of the entry's fields are claims.** Identity, input types and `accepts` are checked. The rest is what the
   bundle's author says, bounded in three places by the floor's load checks. The allowlist is where a deployment's
   honesty is concentrated, as ADR-0040 said of the channel alone.
2. **The digest separates what a tool is from what is said about it.** That line was implicit in I13 and matters once
   prose and labels join the entry (§2).
3. **`destination` is the weakest field.** F2 and F3 depend on it, nothing checks it, and W9 can only see that an
   outward entry said something. A tool whose free-text input carries an address is the case it cannot reach.
4. **The reference already disagrees with pending text on where the target lives** (§5). The custodian settled it at
   R1: the entry. A2 was ratified on the point in Batch 30, with that answer written into it (ADR-0034 R4) ⟨R3⟩.
