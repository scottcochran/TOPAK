# ADR-0009 — Break-glass is a modeled, quorum-witnessed path, not a prohibition

**Status:** Accepted (genesis) · 2026.09.12 · confirmation required (ADR-0029 C1, C4 via 0013) ⟨S1⟩ ⟨B34⟩
**Status note:** As an instance of the exception mechanism (ADR-0013).
**Pending:** none. A1, drafted 2026.10.04, with ADR-0052: break-glass is an emergency seating and not a floor
exception, ratified 2026.10.05 (Batch 34) and applied inline ⟨B34⟩.

---

## 09.11 proposed text

**Context.** The aviation worked example exposes the limit of eviction as the sole enforcement
primitive: at 223 feet with hands on the controls, there is nothing to evict. Advisory-only
becomes another ignored warning; actuation authority replicates MCAS-class risk.

**Decision.** Provide an explicit override path that is logged, quorum-witnessed, and modeled —
living on the control/data plane split rather than being forbidden.

**Consequences.** Converts detection into a committed decision no single actor can unilaterally
override, which is the strongest thesis statement available for a general audience. Requires
TLA+ properties that permit override without the override becoming a general bypass — a hard
and worthwhile spec problem. Must never be used to justify data-plane autonomy in irreversible
domains.

## 09.12 acceptance

**Decision.** Unchanged: an explicit override path, logged, quorum-witnessed, modeled, on the
control/data plane split. Amended: because break-glass *adds* authority, it cannot be expressed as a
refining bundle. It is a verified-object exception, changing at deploy cadence.

*⟨A1⟩ ⟨B34⟩ **The path stays; its home changes.** Break-glass remains an explicit override path,
logged, quorum-witnessed and modeled. It is no longer a verified-object exception: a quorum on the break-glass roster
seats a named person on an emergency seat for one round, on an envelope prepared by promotion, and the floor carries
no exception for it (ADR-0052). "Because break-glass adds authority, it cannot be expressed as a refining bundle"
still holds; what adds the authority is a seating, as for every principal, and not a carve-out. The person acts, which
is the case this record began from. The tension noted below is relieved by one carve-out.*

**Noted tensions.** Directly against ADR-0010's minimality requirement — the floor's reviewability at
human cadence is the basis for trusting Build's HITL gate, and this is the second carve-out against
it. Must never justify data-plane autonomy in irreversible domains.
