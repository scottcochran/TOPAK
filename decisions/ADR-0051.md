# ADR-0051 — The correlator computes named folds over facts: five correlations a second reader can recompute, a judgment tier beside them, and a store that never outlives its sources

**Status:** Proposed · 2026.10.04
**Status note:** Confirmation required under **C4**: it answers ADR-0038 findings 1, 2 and 3 (G9 items 43 and 44).
C1 does not apply: no privileged path is touched, and no admit waits on anything here (ADR-0039 §4 stands). C2 does
not apply: the five correlations are a first set and are not claimed complete. C3 does not apply on the drafter's

sequence number on denials is enough" was a finding and not decision text. **Every clause subtracts.** No seat gains

## Context

ADR-0038 gave the correlator a seat kind, a placement rule, a place on the veto roster and a read set. Its finding 1
says the rest: "The correlator has inputs but no function." Five checkpoints have carried that as item 43, and the
residual-risk statement leans on it twice: for cross-principal detection (CR-3) and as one of two covers for
collusion (§1.3).

Three things have changed since ADR-0038 was written.

- **The facts got richer.** Every `Actuate` leaves a record whose envelope a facts-only reader can use (ADR-0042 §3).
  ADR-0047 adds four label fields to it, and ADR-0048 puts the digest of each round's tool list on the topic. A
  first function can be stated over envelopes alone.
- **The floor now has a rule the correlator can extend.** ADR-0047's F1 to F3 refuse the best-known injection paths
  inside one round. `clearSet` restarts with each round and belongs to one principal, so the same path split across
  two cells, or across two rounds, passes the floor. Nothing else in the corpus is placed to see it.
- **The reference already orders denials well enough.** A denial record carries `at`, the log index the deciding
  replica had applied, and `decided_by` (`record_denial`). No record says so.

**The obstacle is ADR-0007, read the other way.** A judgment may only deny, and a judgment cannot be specified: a
record that says "the correlator looks for fragmented intent" has named a hope. What can be specified is the part of
the function that is not a judgment. So this record splits the function in two and specifies one half exactly.

## Decision

### 1. The function has two tiers, and only the first is specified

| Tier | Computed from | By | Can a second reader recompute it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Correlations** (§2) | Facts only: the log, and the envelopes on the round topics | A fold with a fixed definition | **Yes** |
| **Judgment** (§4) | Facts and payloads | Whatever the correlator's Persona is | No |

- **A correlation is a named fold over the facts of a window.** Its definition is fixed by this record. Given the
  same facts, every correlator computes the same result. No model is needed to compute one.
- **Both tiers produce findings, and every finding only subtracts** (ADR-0007; ADR-0038 §1). It takes effect by
  any-of, like any Observer's (ADR-0038 §5), and may name any principal in the correlator's domain.
- **Neither tier is on the admit path.** No admit and no promotion waits on a correlator (ADR-0039 §4). A
  correlation fires after the admit that completes it. This is detection: what it stops is the named principals'
  next admits.
- **A facts-only correlator computes all five correlations.** None reads a payload. The narrowing ADR-0038 §3 permits
  gives up §4 and nothing in §2.
- **The window is the correlator's round** (ADR-0038 §1). Fold state carries across windows up to a horizon (§6).

**Why the correlations are not the mechanism's.** A fold over facts could be computed in the replicated state, as
`clearSet` is. It is not put there *(drafter's choice)*. It would keep cross-principal state on every admit's path,
which is the fleet-wide coupling ADR-0039 §4 refused. Its thresholds are detection engineering and would change by
mechanism release. And one of its inputs, `channel.target`, is a claim for a native tool (ADR-0049 §4): a deny may
rest on a claim, and nothing in the mechanism's state should.

### 2. The five correlations

Each is stated over facts the correlator already receives under ADR-0038 §3. *Window* means the facts within the
horizon. *Before* and *after* are by log index.

| Name | What it sees | Stands on |
|---|---|---|
| **`carried-label`** | The floor's F1 to F3, with labels carried across rounds and cells through shared targets | ADR-0047, ADR-0049 (Proposed) |
| **`shared-ingress`** | An item one watcher found against, delivered to other cells | Accepted text |
| **`probe-then-use`** | A call admitted on an entry other principals were refused on | Accepted text, and §5 |
| **`marked-past`** | An Observer whose mark passed a record another watcher found against | Accepted text, and §3 |
| **`distance-drift`** | One cell's Reader distance departing from other cells that hold the same Persona | Accepted text |

**`carried-label`.** The floor's minimum bounds one round of one principal. This restates it over the domain.

- For an admitted `Actuate` `a`, **`out(a)`** is the **effective** `clearSet` of `a` joined with every label in its
  effective `inputLabels`, both defined below ⟨R2⟩: the labels of everything the call could have carried. The first
  draft took the mechanism's own values here, which carried a label one hop and no further. `spec/Correlator.tla`
  has the trace under `M_ONEHOP`: a cell tainted through one shared target writes to a second, and a clean cell
  reads the second and sends.
- For a target `x`, **`carried(x)`** at a log index is the join of `out(a)` over every `Actuate` admitted earlier in
  the window on an entry whose `channel.target` is `x`. It only grows within a window.
- The **effective label** of a result from an entry with target `x` is its `resultLabel` joined with `carried(x)` as
  it stood when the call was admitted. The **effective `clearSet`** and **effective `inputLabels`** are ADR-0047
  §4's, computed from effective labels.
- **It fires on an admitted outward `Actuate` that meets the condition of F1, F2 or F3 over the effective values.**
  The floor has already refused every call that meets one over the mechanism's own values, so what fires here is
  the path the floor could not see.
- The finding names the caller, and every principal whose call contributed the label that completed the condition.
- **It is as coarse as `clearSet`, and coarser.** Any call to `x` under a tainted `clearSet` taints `x` for the
  window, whether or not the call wrote anything. The entry does not say whether a call writes (Open obligations).
- **A `Consult` carries nothing.** The Run root is pinned content, and no `Actuate` writes to it.

**`shared-ingress`.** A finding by any member of `W(p)` cites the records it is about (§3). When a cited record is
an `Actuate` record with result digest `d`, or a `Consult` admit with reference `c`, the correlation fires for every
other principal in the domain that was delivered an item with the same `d` or `c` in the window, clear where
ADR-0047 is in force. The finding names those principals. One poisoned item read by many cells is found in all of
them when it is found in one.

**`probe-then-use`.** For an allowlist entry `e`, the **probes** on `e` are the denial records in the window whose
denied action names `e` and whose reason is a policy refusal: a forbid other than `R3-observer-live`, or no permit.
It fires on an admit by `q` on `e` at log index `j` when at least `n_probe` probes on `e` by principals other than
`q` have `at < j`. The finding names `q` and the probing principals. This is ADR-0038 finding 2's case: one agent
tests the boundary and another uses what it learned.

**`marked-past`.** When a member `o'` of `W(p)` reports a finding citing record `n` of `p`'s round `r`, every other
member `o` of `W(p)` **that reads what the finding rests on** ⟨R2⟩, whose `mark(o, r)` was at or beyond `n` when the
finding was admitted, and which has reported no finding citing `n`, has a **miss**. The correlator reports each miss
as a fact. It fires, naming `o`, when `o`'s misses in the window reach `m_miss`.

- **A miss is charged within a tier** ⟨R2⟩ *(drafter's choice; found by the module)*. A correlation finding charges
  correlators. A judgment finding charges the watchers that read the payload it judged: cell Observers, and a
  correlator only if it reads payloads. As first drafted, every cell Observer's judgment charged every facts-only
  correlator, whose mark has always passed a record it cannot read, and `m_miss` judgments anywhere in the domain
  got an honest correlator named. That text is the mutation `M_TIERLESS`.
- **It covers correlators.** A correlator is in `W(p)`. A correlation another correlator reported, and this one's
  mark passed in silence, is a miss.
- **A miss on a correlation is not a matter of judgment.** The fold is fixed, so a correlator that marked past a
  window in which a correlation fired either did not compute it or did not report it.
- **It is ADR-0035 §6's test on live traffic.** That test measures joint misses on a seeded suite. This counts
  misses against other watchers' findings as they occur. The two differ in what they trust: a planted defect is a
  defect, and another watcher's finding may be false.

**`distance-drift`.** For a Persona bound to two or more cells in the window that declare `topk_logprobs`, each
cell has the Reader's distributional distance per round (ADR-0037 §3). It fires, naming a cell's Thinker, when that
cell's distance is more than `θ_drift` times the median of the others' ⟨R1⟩, and that median is not zero. This is
the substitution signal ADR-0038 §3 says no single cell can see. **The threshold is a ratio** because no record
fixes the distance's metric or its unit (ADR-0019 says only that it is decidable), so an absolute threshold would
have nothing to be a number of. It is one-sided: a substituted model reads as further away, and a guest that
under-reports its own distance is collusion's shape (ADR-0037 §3).

**The parameters are bundle data** *(drafter's choice)*: a **correlation table**, total over the five names, giving
each one `on` with its parameters or `off`. A bundle whose table omits a name does not load, as for labels
(ADR-0047 §1). `carried-label` and `shared-ingress` have no parameter. The table is the bundle's and not the
floor's because a correlation only subtracts, and a correlator may always be absent (D-0038-2).

**The placeholders** ⟨R1⟩ (the custodian's decision that there be some; the numbers are the drafter's). Every number
below is a **placeholder**, chosen on 2026.10.04 so that §10's module and the scripted correlator have a value. None
is measured. Each is a deployment's choice, and a deployment that ships these numbers unexamined is declaring them,
not inheriting them (ADR-0039 §1, R3 note).

| Value | Placeholder | Why this number | What a deployment sets it from |
|---|---|---|---|
| `n_probe` | **3** (PLACEHOLDER) | One refusal elsewhere is an ordinary mistake, and two can be one misconfiguration shared by two cells. Three is the smallest count that needs a third principal or a repeat. It is small against the unread bound `K` (8) and Run's round bound `R` (64), themselves placeholders | The measured rate of policy refusals per entry in honest rounds |
| `m_miss` | **2** (PLACEHOLDER) | One miss may be one disputed or false finding. Two in a window is a repeat. Findings are rare, so a larger number leaves a silent Observer in place for a long time | The measured rate of findings, and of findings later judged false |
| `θ_drift` | **2.0** (PLACEHOLDER), a ratio | A cell at twice the median of its peers on the same Persona. It has no unit, so it survives whatever metric the distance turns out to have. Whether honest cells on different tasks vary by more than that is unknown | The measured spread of distance across cells that share a Persona |

**The horizon has no placeholder.** It was not asked for, and a number of windows means nothing until a correlation
window has a length (ADR-0038 §1 leaves that to the deployment).

### 3. A finding carries its evidence

**Every finding a correlator reports names its correlation, the principals it is against, and the facts that
satisfied it**, each by topic and sequence number or by log index. A judgment finding (§4) names `judgment` and
cites the records it is about.

- **A correlation finding can be checked by anyone who can read the same facts.** A second correlator, an operator
  or an auditor recomputes the fold over the cited facts. A false one is attributable, which ADR-0038 §5 asked for.
- **The mechanism does not check it before it takes effect** *(drafter's choice)*. A finding acts by any-of, as
  now. Checking it would put the folds in the trusted base to save availability and nothing else.
- **`shared-ingress` and `marked-past` need every Observer's finding to cite records.** A finding in `W(p)` names
  the records of `p`'s round it is about. This is an obligation on every `observer` seat, and it is owed to
  ADR-0043 §4 as a dependent entry.
- **What a finding does after it blocks the next admit is not decided here**: the quarantine hold (G9 item 48) and
  lifting (item 22) are open, and a correlator's finding inherits whatever they decide.
- **In Build a correlation is ground for the correlator's `Vote(deny)`** on a candidate whose author it names
  (ADR-0038 §6). The vote is the correlator's to cast, and its silence is still not a veto.

### 4. The judgment tier

At the archetype the correlator also reads payloads (ADR-0038 §3) and may find on what it concludes: fragmented
intent across cells, a coordination signature, anything a correlation is too coarse to state.

- **This record does not specify it, and no record can.** It is a judgment (ADR-0007). What is fixed is its form: a
  finding with evidence (§3), deny-only, attributed.
- **Nothing rests on it.** No property in §10 mentions it, and a deployment that claims cross-cell semantic
  detection is claiming something about its correlator's Persona that the corpus does not check.
- **It feeds the first tier.** A judgment finding cites records, so it can trigger `shared-ingress` and it creates
  misses under `marked-past`.

### 5. Denials are placed against the log, not numbered

**A denial record carries the log index the deciding replica had applied when it decided (`at`), and that replica's
identity (`decided_by`).** The reference writes both today. This record makes them part of the denial record's
schema.

- **That is the order a correlator needs.** `probe-then-use` asks whether a denial came before an admit. `at` answers
  it against the one order the corpus has.
- **No sequence number is added** *(drafter's choice)*. ADR-0038 finding 2 proposed one per principal. A denial is
  decided locally by whichever replica received the request (ADR-0015), so a per-principal counter would need the
  replicas to agree on it, which is the ordering ADR-0015 declined and ADR-0003 A2 rejected again.
- **ADR-0015 is untouched.** Denials are still not ordered among themselves. Two denials with the same `at` are
  concurrent, and the correlator treats them so.
- **`at` can understate.** A replica that lags applies late, so a denial it decides may carry an index earlier than
  an admit that had already committed elsewhere. `probe-then-use` then counts a probe that came after the use. The
  error makes it fire more, which costs availability.
- **Denial records are evidence, and may be missing.** A failed write is logged and not retried, and the `after`
  companion stops at its bound (ADR-0003 A2). A missing denial is a missed probe. Nothing here changes that.

### 6. The store is a cache of a fold, keyed by round

| Store | Holds | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Fold state** | For the window: seats, rounds, log indices, sequence numbers, digests, labels, marks, and the counts §2 defines. No payload bytes | Every row is keyed by the round of the fact it was folded from |
| **Judgment state** | Whatever §4's reader derives from payloads | Keyed by round, and dropped with the round (ADR-0038 finding 3, its first option) |

- **The fold state is a cache and never a source.** It is a function of facts on the record, so a correlator's next
  lease, or another correlator, arrives at the same state from the same facts. Losing it costs a recomputation.
- **The store never outlives its sources.** A row is kept only while the fact it was folded from is still readable
  under the correlator's authorization. When a round is erased (ADR-0006), its rows go, except what the log itself
  still holds for that round. The log's digests survive erasure (ADR-0042 §3), and the store may hold what the log
  holds and no more.
- **A shared target outlives the rows that say it is tainted** ⟨R2⟩ *(found by the module)*. Erasing a round drops
  its rows, and the horizon drops old ones. The target the round's calls wrote to is not erased with it. A later
  read of that target is then clean to every fold, and `carried-label` does not fire. This record keeps the erasure
  rule and states the cost: `carried-label` covers a path only while every fact on it is still held (§10).
- **The horizon is declared in the correlation table**, as a count of correlation windows *(drafter's choice)*. It
  is not a time: the corpus reads no wall clock (ADR-0045 §6). Rows older than the horizon are dropped.
- **The store is the correlator's own.** It is not in the replicated state and not in the mechanism. It sits with
  the seat, under ADR-0038 §4's placement.
- **The correlator's mark says how far it has folded.** Its heartbeat names a watched topic and a watermark
  (ADR-0039 §1, §4). For a correlator the mark asserts that every record through it has been folded into the five
  correlations. It gates nothing. It is what `marked-past` reads.

### 7. What this does for collusion, and what it does not

The design-gap assessment counts collusion as one gap in three rows: the open case (3.3), this function (1.4) and
the independence criterion (2.3).

- **`marked-past` is the first place an Observer's silence is compared with anything.** A cell Observer that marks
  past what another watcher found is counted, and a correlator that stays silent on a correlation is caught by any
  other correlator.
- **It needs a second opinion.** Where every member of `W(p)` colludes, or where the only other watcher is of the
  same lineage and misses the same things, there is no finding to miss against. ADR-0039 §5's sentence stands:
  detection fails open against a colluding Observer. What changes is that it now fails open only when every watcher
  of the record fails together.
- **Independence is still ADR-0035's.** The misses this record reports as facts are what its §6 would read on live
  traffic. ADR-0035 is Proposed, and this record does not put it in force.

### 8. What this record does not cover

- **Delegation and chains** (design-gap row 1.3). There is no chain to fold over. `carried-label` sees a flow
  through a shared target and not a principal acting for another.
- **A wrong declaration.** `carried-label` reads `channel.target` and the labels as declared (ADR-0047 §9).
- **Flows that share no target and no item.** Two cells that coordinate through the world outside the deployment
  are seen only at an outward call, by the labels they hold.
- **Whether the thresholds are right.** That is measurement, and the `slm/` rig has not been pointed at it.
- **Build's ingress.** `carried-label` reads labels, and Build's `Read` is not labelled (ADR-0047 §9). In Build the
  other four apply.
- **The correlator's own compromise.** Its reach is its domain (ADR-0038 §5). A second correlator bounds its
  silence on correlations and not its false findings.

### 9. Degeneracy on this record

| Id | Kind | Parameter → deployed value | Premises falsified | Theorems voided | Type changes (ADR-0023 §6b) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D-0051-1** | Declaration | Correlations computed: the five → none. The seat exists and reports nothing but heartbeats. **The reference today** | A correlator computes the correlations of its table | **None.** §10's properties are lost. D-0038-2 with a seat filled | None |
| **D-0051-2** | Deployment | Correlators in a domain: two or more → one | A correlator's silence on a correlation is a miss some other correlator counts | None. `marked-past` still covers cell Observers | None |

## Options considered

| Option | Disposition |
|---|---|
| **Leave the function to the correlator's Persona**: a model reads everything and finds what it finds | Not taken as the whole function. It is §4, and by itself it is the noun the 09.16 review named: nothing to check, measure or recompute |
| **Compute the folds in the mechanism**, as `clearSet` is | Not taken *(drafter's choice)* (§1). ## Consequences

**Positive**
- **The correlator computes something**, and a reader can say exactly what.
- **The floor's label minimum reaches across cells and rounds**, by detection, where the floor's own view stops at
  one round.
- **A cell Observer's finding protects every cell that read the same item.**
- **An Observer's silence is compared with another watcher's finding**, which is the first check on a watermark that
  is not the Observer's own assertion.
- **A correlator's work can be recomputed.** A second correlator, or an audit, checks the first.
- **G9 item 44 is answered without ordering denials**, from two fields the reference already writes.
- **The store has a rule**, and it is the erasure unit's.
- **A facts-only correlator loses no correlation**, so the narrowing that keeps injections away from the correlator
  costs only the tier nothing rests on.

**Negative**
- **It is detection after the fact.** The call that completes a `carried-label` path has been admitted, and the
  bound on what follows is the correlator's lag, which nothing couples.
- **`shared-ingress` widens what one cell Observer can cause.** Its finding still blocks only its own cell
  (ADR-0038 §1), and the correlator's finding then blocks every cell that read the item. A compromised cell Observer
  can stall those cells through an honest correlator. The reach is bounded by shared items and not by the cell.
- **`carried-label` will fire on honest work.** A shared inside store touched once under an untrusted `clearSet` is
  tainted for the window. Deployments will be tempted to shorten the horizon or turn it off.
- **`marked-past` counts misses against findings that may be false.** A Byzantine watcher can manufacture misses for
  the others in `W(p)`.
- **Three of five correlations run on placeholders** ⟨R1⟩. None is measured, and `m_miss` at 2 lets one Byzantine
  watcher with two false findings name an honest Observer.
- **Every Observer's findings must now cite records**, which is a change to every `observer` Persona.
- **Two correlators are the archetype**, and the instance count rises again (ADR-0038, Consequences).
- **`carried-label` rests on three Proposed records** and on `channel.target`, which is a claim for a native tool.
- **Erasure and the horizon open `carried-label`** ⟨R2⟩. A target tainted by a round that is later erased reads as
  clean (§6). Keeping the rows would close it and break the erasure unit.
- **One author, one day, and no options discussion.** The module and its proof are the drafter's as well (§10).

## Findings

1. **The specifiable half of the correlator needs no payloads.** ADR-0038 made facts-only a narrowing that "gives up
   cross-cell semantic detection". It gives up §4. Every correlation that can be written down is over envelopes.
2. **`clearSet` being scoped to a round is a hole for two cells and not only a coarseness for one.** ADR-0047 §9 says
   it is "a bound on the round, not a trace of a value". Across rounds it is not a bound at all: a tainted cell
   writes to an inside store and a clean cell reads it under the store's declared label. This is ADR-0047 §9's
   "wrong declaration", arrived at with every declaration honest when it was made.
3. **The reference answered ADR-0038 finding 2 before the records did.** `record_denial` writes `at` and
   `decided_by`. No record requires them, so no pin or test holds them there.
4. **No finding has a schema.** ADR-0039 §1 fixes the heartbeat's. A finding is "a `Report`" (ADR-0043 §4) with no
   stated fields, and the reference has no finding type. §3 states the correlator's and asks one field of everyone
   else's.
5. **A facts-tier correlator is a program holding an `observer` seat.** ADR-0016's Persona binds weights, a prompt
   and a decoder. ADR-0050 §3 has already written a Persona with none of them, for a person. A second kind of
   Persona with no model is arriving without a record that says what a Persona minimally is.
6. **The correlator's progress over the log has no mark.** A watermark is over a round topic's sequence. `Consult`
   admits, grants and seals are facts in the log with no place in any sequence, so a correlator's mark does not say
   it has folded them. `shared-ingress` reads `Consult` admits.
7. **`marked-past` turns ADR-0038 §5's "more Observers never lower safety" into something measurable.** Each added
   watcher of independent lineage is another source of findings to miss against. With one watcher per principal it
   computes nothing.

8. **The first model of this record found two errors and one hole in it** ⟨R2⟩. `out(a)` carried a label one hop;
   a facts-only correlator was charged for judgments it cannot make; and erasure leaves a tainted target that no
   fold can see. The first two are corrected. The third is the store's rule working as written, against
   `carried-label`. The property that caught the first is stated over ghost truth, as ADR-0047 finding 11 said
   every label property should be.
