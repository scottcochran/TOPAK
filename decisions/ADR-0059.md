# ADR-0059 — A deployment may be a tree of planes: each plane is seated by its parent's promotion under a charter it can only narrow, is a havoc issuer bounded by that charter, and is fenced by every plane above it

**Status:** Proposed · 2026.11.10
**Status note:** Confirmation required under **C1**: every grant below the root travels the path this record opens.
**C3** does not apply while this is an overlay ⟨Q11⟩ and would apply on admission to the archetype; §8 lists the
accepted text it would reverse. C2 and C4 do not apply on the drafter's reading. **Modelled, checked and proved in TLA+
⟨R1⟩ (§9); nothing is built.**
**Pending:** none.
**Deployment position:** **An overlay, not the archetype** ⟨Q11⟩. Under ADR-0058 (Proposed) a deployment adds the
nodes this record describes and reports them upstream (§8). A deployment with one plane is the corpus as it stands
and takes nothing from this record.
**Depends on:** ADR-0024 (promotion; a seating is voted), ADR-0025 and ADR-0026 (the anchor as a havoc issuer
bounded by the rules floor), ADR-0030 (bundles cannot add authority), ADR-0031 (the lease, a closed schema),
ADR-0033 §4 (checked again where applied), ADR-0058 (overlays; Proposed)
**Relates to:** ADR-0023 §4 (the admission test), ADR-0045 (the trusted base; Byzantine replicas), ADR-0050 (the
operator), ADR-0054 (its sentence on delegation; Proposed), ADR-0060 to ADR-0068 (drafted with this record)
**Closes:** nothing.
**Carries a debt against:** nothing while this is an overlay. §8 lists what the archetype's records would owe.

## Context

**Every deployment the corpus describes is one plane**: one replica set, one log, one epoch, one set of rules in
force. The custodian asked what follows if TOPAK is the top tier of a command chain, with control planes below it
that only narrow ⟨1⟩.

**A command chain hands authority down, and the corpus excluded that.** "No principal grants another, so there is
no chain of delegation to attenuate" (ToO §2.11). The 11.05 lattice analysis left a federated authority as an axis
it did not place. This record places it: the grantor is a mechanism and not a model, the delegation only
attenuates, and it is budgeted. The sentence is still reversed in letter.

---

## Decision

### 1. A plane is seated by a promotion at its parent, under a charter ⟨1⟩

A **plane** is one replica set with one log, one epoch and one set of rules in force. A plane's parent seats it by
voting on a **charter**, as it votes on any seating (ADR-0024 A3). The charter has a closed schema, as the lease
has (ADR-0031), and a capability that is not a field is not carried.

| Field | What it bounds |
|---|---|
| `plane`, `replicas` | The child's identity: its replica keys and its quorum rule |
| `kind` | `commanded` or `autonomous` (ADR-0060) |
| `parent`, `fences` | Where it hangs, and the epoch and log index of every plane above it at the seating (§6) |
| `floor` | The floor's digest, which equals the parent's (§2) |
| `bundles` | The parent's list of bundle revisions, in order, plus any this seating adds |
| `alphabet`, `labels` | The entries and labels a lease under this charter may carry |
| `ttlMax`, `countMax` | The longest lease `ttl` the plane may grant and the largest count, per effect class |
| `budget` | The most leases the plane and every plane below it may ever issue |
| `expiry` | The charter's own life. Never later than the parent's |
| `release` | Whether a release seat may be seated at this plane (ADR-0066) |

- **Every field is the parent's or narrower.** The comparison is field by field, and it is made three times: before
  the vote, when the seating is applied, and by every verifier of the chain (ADR-0033 §4).
- **A child's `budget` is debited from its parent's** when it is seated, so the root's tally bounds what is allocated
  across the whole tree, and what is issued in any subtree with no captured plane ⟨R1⟩. A captured plane can sign
  more leases than its budget, each to a different vehicle, and no single verifier sees more than its own. What
  survives capture is the honest parent's account and each honest governor's bound (ADR-0063 §3).
- **A chain** is the charters from the root down to a granting plane, with a lease at the end.
- ADR-0060, ADR-0061, ADR-0063 and ADR-0064 add fields that only an autonomous plane carries.

### 2. Policy narrows by composition, and there is one floor ⟨Q8⟩

At any admit or permit, at any plane, the decision is the floor's permit and no refusal from any bundle on the
chain. A bundle a lower plane adds is appended, can refuse, and is never asked whether to grant (ToO §3.1). So
"narrower than the parent" holds of the composed decision for every bundle, at every plane, at every age. This is
ADR-0030's argument applied once per link, and nothing compares two policies.

**One floor serves the whole tree** ⟨Q8⟩. Every charter carries the root's floor digest. Planes differ by bundles
only.

### 3. What kind of act a narrowing is ⟨8⟩ ⟨Q1⟩

"Adding takes a quorum, and denying is local" (ToO §7.10). A narrowing is a promotion when it brings a new issuer or
a new artifact into force. Otherwise it is an admit, ordered by the plane's consensus and signed by one person or by
the mechanism ⟨Q1⟩.

| Narrowing | Example | What it is | Who acts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A new issuer** | Seating a plane under a charter | A promotion. The charter is narrower than its parent's, and it still creates something that can grant | The parent's grant seats, by tally |
| **A new artifact** | A bundle with a mission's rules | A promotion. "Everything crossing from Build to Run is promoted" (ToO §7.1) | The grant seats, by tally |
| **A round inside a promoted seating** | A commander's next intent (ADR-0060) | An ordered admit, signed by one operator. It adds nothing the tally did not vote | One operator, with a live lease and a live peer (ADR-0050) |
| **A step of a voted schedule** | Dropping a silent replica (ADR-0061) | An ordered admit by the mechanism. The schedule was the promotion's | A quorum of the membership being replaced |
| **A value already in force, moved down** | A shorter `ttl`, a smaller budget, an entry removed, a revocation, a fence | An ordered admit. "Runtime subtraction is not promotion" (ToO §7.1) | One operator, or the mechanism |

"Quorum" in the last three rows means the replicas' agreement on the order, and no vote.

### 4. A plane is a havoc issuer, bounded by its charter

ADR-0025 treats the anchor as a havoc issuer bounded by the rules floor. **A plane gets the same treatment, bounded
by its charter.** Whatever a plane signs, a verifier accepts only what every charter on the chain allows. A captured
plane therefore chooses among leases its parent's quorum already voted acceptable: it decides which, never whether.
**The chain check bounds what each lease may carry, and not how many a captured plane signs** ⟨R1⟩ (§1).

### 5. The chain is verified where there is no link, so an exposed plane runs Byzantine agreement ⟨Q7⟩

Every charter and every lease carries a **commit certificate**: signatures from a quorum of the issuing plane's
replicas over the entry's digest and log index. A verifier checks from the anchor's key down, and that key is
resident, as genesis rules are (ADR-0027).

**A crash-recovery protocol cannot supply that certificate**: one lying replica can obtain a majority's signatures
for two entries at one index, in two ballots. **A plane whose replicas can be captured declares it, and runs
Byzantine agreement** ⟨Q7⟩. An autonomous plane is always such a plane (ADR-0061). A plane that declares its
replicas unexposed keeps the corpus's consensus, and its certificates are trusted on that declaration.

### 6. Any plane on the chain fences everything below it ⟨9⟩

A charter and a lease carry the epoch and log index of each plane on the chain, not only the granting plane's. An
admit or a permit compares each against the latest its verifier has heard. **A plane that moves its epoch fences
everything below it.** That act is a **plane fence**: a subtraction, ordered, needing no quorum (ToO §4.1). A fence
travels as far as the link does. What bounds a plane the fence cannot reach is ADR-0063.

### 7. The trusted base

Each plane's replicas are a row of the kind T-0045-1, per plane. Where §5 applies, Byzantine agreement replaces
that row and a forged certificate is its failure. ADR-0068 carries the rows.

### 8. Overlay position, and what is reported upstream ⟨Q11⟩

ADR-0023 §4: a variation that holds a capability the archetype lacks is not a rung, and the archetype is
incomplete. **This record is therefore drafted as an overlay's**, and these are reported upstream under ADR-0058
§6 as findings about the archetype:

- **a mechanism that grants below itself**, bounded by a charter;
- **commit certificates** a third party can check with no plane in reach;
- **a fence that names every plane on a chain.**

**If the archetype takes them, its records owe:** ADR-0054's sentence on delegation and ToO §2.11, restated;
ADR-0024 A3, a charter among the seatings a tally votes; ADR-0031, the lease's fence as one per plane on the chain;
ADR-0045, its decision not to require Byzantine agreement, reversed for exposed planes, which is C3. A one-plane
deployment would then be a declared degeneracy.

## Options considered

| Option | Disposition |
|---|---|
| **One plane, larger** | The corpus as it stands. It cannot hand authority to a group that loses its link |
| **Principals that delegate**, an agent granting an agent | Rejected, as ToO §2.11 rejects it. The grantor here is a mechanism |
| **Every narrowing is a promotion** | Not taken ⟨Q1⟩. A command chain could no longer decide by one commander between votes |
| **A floor per plane** | Not taken ⟨Q8⟩. Each would be a verified object of its own, and narrowing between floors needs a cross-floor check |
| **Trust a plane for its own charter** | Rejected. `M_TRUSTPLANE`. A captured plane would name its own bounds |
| **Crash-fault consensus with certificates** | Rejected for an exposed plane (§5) |
| **Byzantine agreement at every plane** | Not taken ⟨Q7⟩. It is declared where replicas are exposed |
| **Extend the archetype now** | Not taken ⟨Q11⟩. A deployment that never loses its link would pay for what it does not use |

## Consequences

**Positive**
- **Authority reaches a group that will lose its link**, inside bounds the root's tally voted.
- **What a captured plane's lease may carry is bounded** by its charter, and the bound is checked by whoever receives
  the lease. How many it signs is not (§1) ⟨R1⟩.
- **One floor and one fitness question**, however deep the tree.

**Negative**
- **Delegation.** It attenuates, it is a mechanism's and it is budgeted, and it is still delegation.
- **Byzantine agreement** wherever replicas are exposed, which the corpus had declined to require.
- **A deeper trusted base**: a replica row per plane, and a chain every verifier must walk.
- **One author, one day.**
