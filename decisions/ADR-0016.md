# ADR-0016 — The model enters the system as a Persona

**Status:** Accepted (genesis) · 2026.09.12
**Pending:** A1, drafted 2026.10.04, dependent on ADR-0050 (Proposed): a person's Persona (§1). A2, drafted 2026.10.04,
dependent on ADR-0052 (Proposed): the seat binding `emergency` (§1). Both marked inline and not applied.
**Depends on:** ADR-0007 (semantic judgment is never a lift), ADR-0010 (verified object =
mechanism + minimal policy floor), ADR-0002 (`Build(Contain) → Run(Contain)`)
**Companions:** ADR-0017 (archetype provenance), ADR-0018 (typed lift), ADR-0019 (decode
observability), ADR-0020 (replay scope) — each separately supersedable
**Note:** replay scope was drafted here as §8 and **split out to ADR-0020** before ratification;
it is separately supersedable and carries its own revisit triggers.

---

## Context

Every component inside `Contain` is pinned and hash-identified: the enforcement mechanism, the
policy floor, the operational bundle. The one component the architecture exists to contain —
the model — is identified by a provider endpoint and a version string that can be re-pointed
without producing any event the specification can observe.

This is the widest unpinned surface in the system, and it is structurally embarrassing for a
pattern whose thesis is *verify the cage, not the occupant*. The cage is pinned. The occupant
is a name.

Three specific exposures:

1. **Silent substitution.** Provider-side weight or serving changes produce no epoch bump, no
   lease invalidation, and no re-attestation. A model change is a state transition the model
   checker does not know occurred.
2. **Cadence gap.** TOPAK's cadences are release (mechanism, floor), incident (bundle), and
   runtime (lease, epoch). Model change happens at provider cadence, which the system neither
   controls nor observes.
3. **Governance.** NIST AI RMF (Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework) Playbook
   MANAGE 3.2 requires pre-trained models used in development to be inventoried, independently
   and continually monitored, remediated, and decommissioned when they exceed risk tolerance.
   TOPAK has mechanisms adjacent to all four and an inventory entry that is a string. MANAGE
   3.1, MAP 4.1, and GOVERN 6.1/6.2 point at the same unmodeled third party from other angles.

The prior framing treated this as a third-party risk question, which puts it outside the
specification and into organizational process. This ADR relocates it: **the model is an
artifact of Build, and artifacts of Build are governed by machinery that already exists.**

---

## Decision

**A model is admitted to Run only as a Persona: a signed, versioned Build artifact that binds
a model to its complete runtime configuration. Personas are admitted per principal seat by
policy, denied by evidence, and carried in the lease.**

### 1. Persona contents

A Persona record binds, at minimum:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Provenance / lineage | Training corpora identifiers, base-model ancestry, fine-tune chain. Degrades across rungs — see ADR-0017. |
| Persona ID | Cryptographically signed by the Build promotion key. The signature is over the whole record, not the model alone. |
| Sequence number | Monotonically increasing per Persona line. **Ordering only** — not the authority-monotonicity of the floor/bundle relation. Deliberately named to avoid that collision. |
| System prompt | Byte-exact, hashed. |
| Decoding parameters | Temperature, top-k, top-p, penalties, stop sequences. |
| Decoding stack version | The sampler implementation, not only its parameters. Identical weights under a different sampler is a different Persona. |
| Determinism class | Reproducible, seeded-reproducible, or non-reproducible. Recorded for inventory and incident context; **not** an admission precondition — see §8. |
| Context window and truncation policy | An unpinned truncation rule silently changes what the Thinker saw, which makes every trace an assertion about an unknown input. |
| Decoder | Identifier and hash of the model-specific emission decoder shipped with this Persona. See ADR-0018. |
| Decode observability | `none` \| `topk_logprobs`. Optional, rung-gated. Non-default values are admissible **only within the narrow scope ADR-0019 ratifies** — see the constraint below. |
| Seat binding | Which LTRO (Listener/Thinker/Reader/Observer) seat this Persona may occupy. *Since ADR-0037 §2 the values are `thinker` and `observer`; the Listener and the Reader are stages and bind no Persona* ⟨B12⟩ |

*Pending ⟨A1⟩, not applied. **A person's Persona.** A principal who is a person and acts under a key has a Persona
record too (ADR-0050 §3): a signing key, a role, a domain, a lineage, a Persona ID and a sequence number. It has no
prompt, decoding parameters, decoder or weights, and the rows above that describe them do not apply to it. Its
lineage is declared and its root is the identity provider that vouches for the person (ADR-0035 §1). `Seat binding`
gains the value `operator`. §6 holds of it: the Persona is attested at grant and renewal, by a sign-on assertion
that names the key and the fence. §7 holds of it: revoking it is an ordinary deny, and a revoked operator can no
longer deny.*

*Pending ⟨A2⟩, not applied. `Seat binding` gains the value `emergency` (ADR-0052 §1), for a person's Persona whose
attestation at the lease boundary is the break-glass roster's quorum.*

### 2. Verification and suitability are different predicates

The two words are kept apart throughout, because fusing them is read — correctly — as a
retreat from the central thesis.

- **Verification** — the artifact is what it claims to be. Signature validity, hash match,
  provenance chain integrity, Persona record completeness. Decidable. **A legitimate
  precondition of admission.**
- **Suitability** — evaluation results, benchmark scores, red-team findings. Statistical,
  non-exhaustive, carrying no safety property. **Evidence, never a precondition.**

TOPAK verifies Personas. It does not verify models, and this ADR makes no claim that it does.

### 3. Evaluation is deny-only

Directly from ADR-0007. Evaluation output is semantic judgment, therefore never a lift:

> A passing evaluation can never admit a Persona. A failing one can deny or quarantine it.

No amendment to ADR-0007 is required; this is the existing rule applied to a new artifact
class. It is also the answer to the obvious objection that evals are weak evidence — they are,
and the asymmetry is precisely what makes their weakness harmless.

### 4. Imported models pass the same gate

A model that did not originate in Build is admitted only by passing the same verification
predicates. Where the artifact is not in hand, provenance degrades rather than the gate
relaxing:

**verified** (artifact possessed, hash-checked) → **attested** (provider signs a statement) →
**asserted** (a version string and nothing else).

The gate does not soften. The rung changes, and the Persona records which. See ADR-0017.

### 5. Policy admits Personas per seat

Acceptable Persona per role is a policy question, decided by the bundle, bounded by the floor.
This is the load-bearing clause: Persona authorization needs no new authorization model, and
inherits refinement-at-promotion, monotone subtraction, and stale-bundle safety unmodified.

Two obligations on the policy:

- **Seat distinctness.** The Observer's Persona must differ from the Thinker's by lineage, not
  merely by configuration. Expressed as a policy predicate over Persona provenance, which makes
  the cross-family Observer requirement enforceable rather than advisory and closes the
  monoculture open item.
- **Floor bound.** The floor names the Persona classes admissible at all; bundles may only
  subtract from that set.

### 6. The lease carries the Persona ID

Persona identity is a lease parameter, attested at grant and at renewal. A provider-side or
operator-side change that invalidates attestation causes renewal to fail, the lease to expire,
and the principal to be evicted by the existing passive path.

This is the mechanism answer to the cadence gap. Provider cadence no longer needs to be
observed on its own schedule — it is sampled at lease renewal, and unattestable means deny.

### 7. Revocation is an ordinary deny

Persona revocation — evaluation regression, provider disclosure, discovered lineage defect — is
a denial. Local, no consensus, no floor exception.

Explicitly: **revocation does not add to ADR-0013's carve-out count.** It is the one
privileged-looking path in this area that is not privileged, and saying so prevents it being
filed with erasure and break-glass by resemblance.

### 8. Replay scope

Split to **ADR-0020**. Replay reconstructs what crossed the membrane, not what happened inside
it. Persona's determinism-class field is recorded metadata under that decision, never an
admission precondition.

### 9. Ratification constraint on decode observability

`decode_observability` is admitted at ratification with `none` as the only unconditional value.
`topk_logprobs` is admissible **only for the uses ADR-0019 ratifies** —
decoding conformance and substitution detection — and may not be cited in any claim about
Observer detection latency `L`. Widening that scope requires amending ADR-0019, not this record.

This is deliberate. A field whose values are defined by a companion record is how a debt accrues
silently; bounding it here means the debt cannot.

## Consequences

### Positive

- The last unpinned component inside `Contain` acquires an identity. The pinning claim becomes
  true without qualification.
- Persona admission is bundle policy, so it inherits the entire ratified ADR-0010 stack at no
  additional proof cost.
- Cross-family Observer independence becomes machine-checkable.
- MANAGE 3.2 moves from an organizational process control to a mechanism: inventory is the
  Persona registry, decommissioning is revocation plus eviction, remediation is a documented
  Persona sequence.
- Model change acquires an event. Everything downstream — traces, replay, attribution — can
  name the Persona that produced it.

### Negative

- **Persona proliferation.** Every parameter change is a new Persona and a new promotion.
  Temperature is now a deploy-cadence change. This is the correct price and will be unpopular;
  the mitigation is that the floor bounds Persona *classes*, so the registry grows without the
  admission logic growing.
- **Build's proof surface widens again.** Persona verification joins ADR-0007's totality gates
  as a required Build gate, in a domain TOPAK declares hostile.
- **Replay's guarantee is downgraded, deliberately** (ADR-0020). Non-reproducible Personas are
  therefore admissible — the Thinker seat stays open to any model class — at the price of a
  record that establishes action and never intent.
- **The measurement burden rises.** With no provider producing a model card, continual
  monitoring under MANAGE 3.2 points entirely inward, and it joins the unmeasured Observer
  detection latency `L` on the MEASURE side.

### Neutral

- A training run, or any in-Build model adaptation, is an artifact-producing effect inside
  `Build(Contain)` — and Build's membrane has no effect stream (09.12 checkpoint §1). This ADR
  does not create that open item but materially increases its weight.
- Trusting trust relocates rather than terminates: the regress moves from the provider to the
  training data, training code, and hardware. The honest narrow claim is that the untrusted
  parties move inside the declared axiom set.

---

## Open

- **Persona granularity.** Per seat is decided here. Per principal *instance* is not — two
  Thinkers on one Persona is currently permitted and unexamined. *Resolved by ADR-0037 §5: two
  `thinker` seats on one Persona are two principals in two cells, and this is permitted; the
  correlation it creates across cells is the correlator's concern (ADR-0038)* ⟨B12⟩.
- **Registry authority.** Who may propose a Persona is the same shape of question as ADR-0014's
  floor authorship, and probably belongs to it rather than here.
