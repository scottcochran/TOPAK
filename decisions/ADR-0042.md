# ADR-0042 — Mechanism obligations for Run: the sequence counts effects, every `Actuate` leaves a record, and the round seals when the lease ends

## Context

**ADR-0039's bound counts the wrong thing for Run.** `live(o)` requires `seq(t) − mark(o, r) < K`, and `seq(t)` is the
Thinker's *emission* sequence number. In the reference only an emission or a heartbeat advances it (`state.rs`,
`apply_admit`). An admitted `RunInvoke` advances the round count and not the sequence, calls the tool, and writes
nothing to the round topic (`mechanism.rs`, the effect path). Four things follow.

- **The unobserved window is `R` effects, not `K`.** With the placeholders that is 64 against 8. An emission goes to
  the principal's own topic. An invocation changes the world. The coupling bounds the first.
- **A principal that never reports defeats the watermark.** Its sequence stays at zero, every mark passes, and its
  Observer only has to keep sending heartbeats. That is the starved case ADR-0039 §5 counts as closed.
- **Coverage across rounds is vacuous for invocations.** The seal is the final sequence. A round of invocations seals
  at zero and the next grant passes unread. ADR-0039 R1 added the grant condition because nothing bounded the backlog
  across rounds. For invocations nothing does.
- **The Observer has nothing to read.** ADR-0038 §3 says the facts include "admits and denials with their principal,
  lease, epoch and per-principal sequence number". The round topic receives emissions and denials.

**The check did not see this, and could not.** `Coupling.tla` has one `Admit` action, which advances `seq` and tests
it against both `K` and `R`. It models a principal whose every admit is an emission. `UnreadBounded` holds of that
model.

## Decision

### 1. The carrier and the validator

Every Run action carries epoch and lease, and is refused under a fenced epoch ahead of the floor (ADR-0022 §2;
ADR-0040 §2). The validator is total over `Consult`, `Actuate` and `Report`: every request is one of the three with
well-typed parameters, or it is refused. A request naming a retired action is refused. Any error is a deny (ADR-0030
§1).

### 2. The sequence counts effect-bearing admits

**`seq(t)` is the number of `Actuate` and `Report` admits applied under `t`'s lease in its current round.** It is
assigned at apply, so every replica assigns the same number. `Consult` does not advance it.

- **Condition 3 of `live(o)` is unchanged in form** and now bounds what it was written to bound: after an admit, at
  most `K` of the round's effects are unread by its Observer.
- **The round bound `R` counts every admit**, `Consult` included. It is a termination bound (ADR-0022 §3) and a read
  is a step.
- **`K < R` is still required** (ADR-0039 §1), and is still checked at startup for each domain.

ADR-0039 §1's "`seq(t)` is `t`'s emission sequence number" is amended to this definition for Run. Build is not
touched: its coupling is at the gate (ADR-0039 §3).

### 3. Every `Actuate` leaves a record on the round topic

**For each admitted `Actuate`, the mechanism writes one record to the principal's round topic.** The principal does
not write it and cannot: it is not an emission, and no variant produces it.

| Field | What it is | Side of ADR-0004's partition |
|---|---|---|
| `seat`, `fence` | The principal's seat and the lease's fence | Control plane |
| `admit` | The log index at which the admit committed | Control plane |
| `seq` | The sequence number of §2 | Control plane |
| `tool` | Name and digest of the allowlist entry | Control plane |
| `breakGlass` | Whether the admit was under X1 | Control plane |
| `inputs` | Digest of the inputs | Control plane |
| `outcome` | `returned`, `failed` or `unknown` | Control plane |
| `result` | Digest of what the tool returned, when it returned | Control plane |
| payload | The inputs and the result themselves | Cognitive payload |

- **The envelope is fact and the bodies are payload.** This answers ADR-0022's follow-up for Run. The digests let an
  Observer or a correlator that reads facts only (ADR-0038 §3's permitted narrowing) see that a call happened, to
  which tool, and whether two calls carried the same inputs.
- **The record is in the round's unit of erasure.** It is read under the topic's ACL (0003.3), retained with the round
  and dropped with it. Its digests in the log survive erasure, as every admit's do.
- **It is written when the call returns** *(drafter's choice)*, with `outcome` and `result`. The admit is already
  ordered before the effect (ADR-0015), so the intent is on the log whatever happens next. A call that has not
  returned when the round seals is written as `unknown` before the seal marker. **A call that returns after the
  seal delivers nothing** ⟨R1⟩: its record already says `unknown`, and the principal receives that outcome and no
  result. The topic is sealed, so no later record can correct it, and §4 says why the result cannot be handed
  over unrecorded.
- **`Report` is unchanged.** An emission is already written to the topic with its sequence number.
- **`Consult` writes no record to the topic.** Its admit is a fact in the log. It has no place in the sequence, so it
  has no place in what the watermark covers.

### 4. A tool's result is ingress

What `Actuate` returns is delivered to the principal, and it was not content-addressed in advance. **The result is
recorded by digest in §3's record, and the bytes the principal receives are the bytes that digest names.** With
`Consult`, this makes Run's ingress a closed list: the task, the objects consulted, and the results of calls admitted.
ADR-0043 §1 states the list as the Listener's.

**A result the record does not name is not delivered** ⟨R1⟩. When the round sealed before the call returned, the
record says `unknown` (§3) and names no result, so the principal receives the outcome and nothing else. The rule
follows from the sentence above: what the principal receives is what the record's digest names, and an
`unknown` record names nothing. Delivering the result anyway would put ingress on the principal that no record
carries, which is the closed list above with a hole in it. The effect may still have happened; that is what
`unknown` says, and the Observer reads it.

Whether a result is poisoned is an Observer finding and not an enforcement decision, as for the Run root (ADR-0040
§3).

### 5. The round seals when the lease ends

**A Run round ends when its lease ends, and the topic seals at the final sequence number.** The ordinary end is
expiry. The others are subtractions.

| What ends the lease | Seals | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Expiry, on a replica's clock and then ordered | Yes | The ordinary end of a Run round |
| Operator end of round | Yes | An e-stop |
| Epoch fence | Yes | Fleet-wide (ADR-0031 §5) |
| Targeted revocation | Yes | ADR-0031 §5 |
| A new rules epoch | Yes | |
| Renewal at a new fence | **No** | The round continues; the count and the sequence carry over (ADR-0031 §4) |

- **The principal cannot end its own round.** There is no terminal variant (ADR-0040 §4) and no release. Build's
  abstain is `Submit(⊥)` and belongs to Build. **The mechanism refuses it from a Run seat.**
- **Coverage across rounds now covers invocations.** ADR-0039 §2's grant condition is unchanged in text: round `r+1`
  is granted only when a cell Observer's mark is at or beyond the seal of round `r`. The seal now counts every effect,
  so the condition means what R1 intended.
- **Denials after the seal** go to the round's `after` companion (ADR-0003 A2), as today.

### 6. Confinement is an obligation, for Run as for Build

*This section is separable. §1 to §5 may be ratified without it.*

For Run principals the mechanism must admit no residual channel: no shell, no network egress from the principal's
guest, no filesystem access outside the Run root, and no path to any effect except `Actuate`. **This record states the
obligation and does not deliver it.** Until a mechanism enforces it, Run principals are `Mediated` at every rung
(ADR-0034 §1), and every Run containment theorem is conditional on its discharge. This is the obligation ADR-0022 §6,
as revised, says is owed by the record that defines Run's alphabet. ADR-0040 §6 assigns it here.

**The effect channels are the tools', not the principal's.** An allowlisted tool runs on the mechanism's side of the
front door, reached by the mechanism and never by the guest (ADR-0033 §2). So the guest's profile can be Build's in
every clause but one. **ADR-0034 P4 cannot hold for a Run tool**: it forbids the socket a Run tool exists to open.
What replaces it is channel fidelity: a tool opens the channel its allowlist entry declares (ADR-0040 §3) and no
other.

**What discharges it** is the Run profile, an amendment to ADR-0034 (G5 item 26). This record does not write the
profile. Channel fidelity has no owner, as hermeticity for native tools has none in Build (D-0034-3).

### 7. Degeneracy on this record

| Id | Kind | Parameter → deployed value | Premises falsified | Theorems voided | Type changes (ADR-0023 §6b) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D-0042-1** | Mechanism (ADR-0023 §6a) | Effects counted and recorded: `Actuate` and `Report` → `Report` only | Every effect-bearing admit advances the sequence and leaves a record an Observer can mark | **None.** **Properties lost:** `UnreadBounded` for invocations. Within a round the unread invocations are bounded by `R` and not `K`; across rounds they are unbounded, growing by up to `R` a round. Detection fails open against a starved Observer for every principal that does not report | No participant changes type |

**D-0042-1 is the reference as it stands on 2026.09.29.** It is declared so that the state of the reference has a
name, and not because a deployment should choose it. It composes with D-0039-1 with no loss beyond the union: with no
coupling at all, which effects are counted makes no difference.

The row writes "none" under *theorems voided* and states its loss in prose, for the reason ADR-0039 §7 gives: ADR-0023
§6's schema has no field for a property that is not a safety theorem (ADR-0037 finding 6).

### 8. Ratification condition

- **ADR-0040 is ratified first, or together with this record.** §6 may stay Proposed.
- **The amendment to ADR-0039 §1 is drafted**, marked inline as pending.
- **A TLC check, as a new module**, since `Coupling.tla` is cited by digest in an accepted record. One Thinker, one
  cell Observer, at least two rounds, and **three separate actions** in place of one `Admit`:
  - **`UnreadEffectsBounded`**: with an honest Observer, the Thinker's unread `Actuate` and `Report` admits, summed
    over all its rounds, never exceed `K`. Mutation **`M_EMITSEQ`**: only `Report` advances the sequence. It must
    fail, with a trace of invocations admitted past `K` while the mark stands still. **`M_EMITSEQ` is the reference
    before this record.**
  - **`ConsultNeverCoupled`**: a state predicate using `ENABLED`. With a live lease and the round bound not reached,
    `Consult` stays enabled whatever the Observer's state. Mutation `M_COUPLEALL`: `Consult` is coupled. It must fail.
  - **`SealCountsEffects`**: at the end of a round the seal equals the number of effect-bearing admits in it. Mutation
    `M_ROUNDS`, carried over from `Coupling.tla`, must still fail.
  - **`RoundBoundCountsAll`**: no round admits more than `R` actions of any kind.
- **The reference**, with an ADR-0033 addendum: the counter of §2; the record of §3 with its `outcome`; the `unknown`
  record written ahead of the seal; an abstain from a Run seat refused. **Three behaviours, each seen:** an `Actuate`
  refused for a backlog over `K` made of invocations alone; a grant for round `r+1` refused while round `r`'s
  invocations are unread; a `Consult` admitted while both are refused.

## Options considered

| Option | Disposition |
|---|---|
| **Leave invocations bounded by the round alone** | Rejected. `R` is sized for termination and state space, not detection (ADR-0039, Options). It is declared as D-0042-1 |
| Count `Consult` in the sequence too | Not taken. The custodian's decision. A read adds nothing an Observer must see before the next effect |
| Have the principal report its own invocations | Rejected. A Byzantine principal would report what it chose. The mechanism admitted the call and is the only party that knows it happened |
| **Two records for each call**, one at admit and one at return | Not taken *(drafter's choice)*. The admit is already in the log, so a record at admit repeats a fact. One record keeps the sequence one-to-one with effects. The cost is finding 2 |
| Write the record before the call | Not taken. It would carry no result, and the Observer would mark past an effect whose outcome it has not seen |
| **A release operation**, so a finished principal can end its round | Not taken. The custodian's decision: the round seals at lease expiry. It only subtracts and would cost nothing in safety; it is recorded here so that a later record can take it up without reopening this one |
| A terminal variant | Rejected by ADR-0040 §4 |
| The Observer triggers the seal | Rejected. Coverage is a mark at or beyond the seal. An Observer that set the seal could set it at its own mark, and coverage would hold of whatever it had chosen to read |
| Keep results out of the topic and in the log by digest only | Not taken at the archetype, which is the maximum (ADR-0023 §3). It is the facts-only narrowing ADR-0038 §3 already permits for the correlator |
| Put Run's confinement obligation in ADR-0040 | Not taken *(drafter's choice)*. It is a mechanism obligation, and in its own section here it can stay Proposed without holding the alphabet back |

## Consequences

**Positive**
- **`K` bounds effects.** The first mechanism-enforced limit touching `L` (ADR-0039, Consequences) now covers the
  actions that can be irreversible.
- **The cell Observer has a record of every invocation**, and ADR-0038 §3's fact set is what the reference writes.
- **Run's ingress is a closed list** (§4).
- **ADR-0022's partition follow-up is answered for Run.**
- **The reference's present behaviour has a name** (D-0042-1) and a mutation that shows why it changes (`M_EMITSEQ`).

**Negative**
- **The Observer's reading speed is on the path of every tool call**, not only its presence. ADR-0039 §6's three costs
  now apply to invocations. A slow Observer throttles effects to `K` at a time.
- **Results can be large**, and the Observer has to read them to move its mark honestly. Nothing here bounds their
  size.
- **A finished principal waits out its TTL**, and its next grant waits on coverage of the round that just sealed (§5).
  Short Run TTLs are the only relief.
- **Payloads on the topic enlarge the erasure surface** (ADR-0003, ADR-0006).
- **§6 is one more confinement obligation with no mechanism behind it.**
- **One author.**

## Findings

1. **`UnreadBounded` was checked of a model the reference does not implement.** `Coupling.tla`'s single `Admit`
   advances `seq`; the reference keeps `actions` and `emit_seq` apart. The property is sound of the model. The
   module's header says its sequence restarts "as the reference's `emit_seq` does", which names the counter and not
   the difference.
2. **A replica that fails between the effect and the record leaves an admit with no record on the topic.** The gap is
   visible, because the sequence is assigned in the log and the topic then skips a number. Nothing yet says who fills
   it or whether the round can seal across it. The watermark is the Observer's assertion, so an Observer can mark past
   the gap; an honest one cannot say what it read there.
3. **The node's abstain handler does not check the domain** (`mechanism.rs`, `act`, as read on 2026.09.29). The MCP
   front door offers `abstain` to Build seats only, so a Run principal is not shown it. §5 makes the refusal the
   mechanism's. No test exercises the case today.
4. **A renewal keeps the sequence, and ADR-0039 §1 says it restarts "with each lease".** In the reference a renewal
   copies the count and the sequence to the new fence (`apply_renew`), so they restart with each round. The text and
   the code agree on rounds and differ on the word.
5. **ADR-0039 finding 3 is sharpened, not closed.** With the seal at lease expiry, a cell Observer must hold a lease
   after its Thinker's has ended in order to report the final mark. ADR-0043 §5 states the obligation. How rounds are
   laid out is still specified nowhere.
