# ADR-0057 — A deployment declares its specification graph against the archetype's: it keeps, replaces, adds or omits nodes, types every edge it re-establishes, publishes its inventory at every grade, and reports upstream what the archetype lacks

**Status:** Proposed · 2026.11.06
**Status note:** Confirmation required under **C2**, because §2 makes a deployment's declaration claim that every edge
its inventory relies on is in its overlay, which is an exhaustiveness claim. **C1** does not apply. **C3** does not
apply: ADR-0023 §6's schema gains a field (§7), by amendment, and nothing accepted is reversed. **C4** does not apply.
**It rests on ADR-0056**, which is Proposed: the two are ratified together or this one waits.
**Pending:** none.
**Deployment position:** Archetype. The record is about deployments and what they declare. One degeneracy (§10).
**Depends on:** ADR-0056 (the graph, its edge types and grades), ADR-0023 §2 to §7 (inventory as premise loss; the
admission test; the declaration and its composition note), ADR-0045 §3, §4 (an undeclared row is false; a deployment
instantiates rows and adds none that is not a degeneracy), ADR-0053 §5 R7 (derived premises)
**Relates to:** ADR-0049 §4 (checked or claimed), ADR-0029 A2 (who reads), the archetype-evolution record (G6 item 7;
unwritten), `ADOPTERS_GUIDE_README.md` §2 and §9
**Closes:** nothing.
**Carries a debt against:** ADR-0023 §6 (the declaration gains a graph, §7) · `ADOPTERS_GUIDE_README.md` §2 and §9.2
(the declaration and its checklist, §7)

## Context

**Adopting TOPAK is already a declaration.** "You do not install TOPAK; you declare a position against its archetype,
build what that position requires, and inherit exactly the theorems whose premises your declaration leaves standing"
(the Adopter's Guide §1). The declaration today names parameters moved (ADR-0023 §6), the rung per seat (§6c) and the
trusted-base rows instantiated (ADR-0045 §4). Annex B is the reference's.

**It says nothing about artifacts.** An adopter who writes their own mechanism, in another language, has no place to
state what ties it to the modules; T-0045-28 stands for it, as it stands for the reference. An adopter who replaces
the transport, keeps the floor as written and adds their own Rego, or states a protocol in P or Quint rather than
TLA+, can declare the parameters those choices move but not the relations those choices create or cut. And a supplier,
of a tool, a Persona, a policy library or a mechanism, has no form in which to publish what its artifact discharges.

**The corpus has one instance of graded declaration already.** ADR-0049 §4 marks each field of an allowlist entry
*checked* or *claimed*, and the entry says which. That is two grades, for one schema. ADR-0056 gives the corpus eight,
for every edge. This record gives deployments and suppliers the same, against the archetype's graph ⟨K4⟩.

## Decision

### 1. The archetype publishes its graph, and a deployment's graph is an overlay on it

The archetype's graph (ADR-0056 §8) is published with each archetype version, by digest. **A deployment's graph is an
overlay**: for each archetype node, one of four operations, and for each edge, a type.

| Operation | What the deployment does | What it declares |
|---|---|---|
| **Keep** | Uses the node as published | The node's digest; the archetype's edges below it stand, as typed |
| **Replace** | Substitutes its own artifact | The artifact's digest, and each edge the archetype node had, re-established and typed (§2) |
| **Add** | Brings a node the archetype has no counterpart for: a tool's schema, a deployment's own policy, a runtime monitor | The node and its edges, typed |
| **Omit** | Does not use the node | Nothing; its edges are absent and their premises false |

The reference is the first overlay (§9): it keeps every record and module, and adds its crates.

### 2. A replaced node re-establishes its edges, each typed

When a deployment replaces a node, **every edge the archetype node had is re-established at a declared type, or
declared absent**. An edge not mentioned is absent, and its premise is false (ADR-0045 §3, rule 1, as ADR-0056 §4 reads
it). The type is read from ADR-0056 §3's table by what the edge leaves to a person.

**Re-establish at the strongest type you can.** A replacement that reruns the archetype's checks against itself, the
floor's gate suite, the trace replay, the graph check, takes the type of that check. One that does not takes *read*,
with its reader named, or is absent.

### 3. The method is the deployment's, and the edge's type follows from what it leaves ⟨K1⟩

A deployment states its nodes in the formalism that fits the property, as the corpus does (ADR-0056 §10): P, Quint,
Alloy, Dafny, Verus, Kani, Coq, Isabelle, or the corpus's own TLA+, TLAPS and Lean. The type of an edge is read by
what it leaves, whatever the tool: a Verus proof that a Rust mechanism refines a module is *proved*, and its prover is
a row.

**A prover or checker the archetype has no row for is declared as a degeneracy** under ADR-0023 §4a, a D-row citing
the nearest archetype row it stands beside (T-0045-29, T-0045-31 or T-0045-37), as ADR-0045 §4 requires of anything a
deployment trusts that Annex A does not. **And it is reported upstream** (§6), because Annex A has no generic row for a
prover a deployment chose (finding 2).

### 4. Inheritance is graded, and the declaration publishes every grade ⟨K2⟩

The deployment's inventory is computed from its overlay, at every grade, as ADR-0056 §5 computes the corpus's. **The
declaration publishes the inventory at each grade, with each theorem's grade and the route that carried it.** A claim
a deployment makes outside its declaration names its grade: "`NoActionUnderFencedEpoch` holds at *replayed*", not
"holds". A claim with no grade is read at *trusted*.

**Show each node's standing beside each grade.** A grade measures an edge, not whether its ends are in force; a
deployment that executes a Proposed floor stage has a strong edge to a node not in force (ADR-0056 finding 6), and its
declaration says both.

**The composition note carries grades.** ADR-0023 §7a's note lists theorems lost to the joint effect of several
parameters. Under this record it also lists theorems whose grade falls by the joint effect of several overlay
operations beyond what each alone causes. Both are computed from the overlay, and an empty delta is still a claim.

### 5. A deployment may grade above the archetype, and the admission test is unchanged

A deployment that discharges what the archetype only trusts, a mechanism proved to refine the modules where the
reference has T-0045-28, **has the archetype's inventory at a higher grade**. That is not a capability the archetype
lacks in ADR-0023 §4's sense, since §4 reads inventories and the inventory is not larger; the archetype stays the
unique maximum of the parameter lattice (§3). **It is a route the archetype lacks, and it is reported upstream** (§6),
so that the archetype can add it as an alternative in the derived premise for every deployment that takes the same
artifact.

### 6. Report upstream what the archetype lacks

The Adopter's Guide already says, of a variation that holds a capability the archetype lacks: "the archetype is
incomplete. Report it." **A deployment also reports**: a route stronger than the archetype's (§5); a method with no
row (§3); a node it had to add that every deployment of its kind will need, a schema above all (ADR-0056 §9); and an
edge it could not re-establish because the archetype node was underspecified. Each report is a finding about the
archetype, as ADR-0023 §4's rejections are.

### 7. Suppliers publish subgraphs, and a deployment imports them

A **supplier** of a component, a tool, a Persona, a policy library, a transport or a mechanism implementation,
publishes a **subgraph**: its nodes by digest, the archetype nodes it claims to discharge, the edges with their types,
and the evidence for each by digest. **A deployment that uses the component imports the subgraph into its overlay.**
Each imported edge keeps its type if the deployment reruns its check, and enters as *read*, with the supplier as its
reader, if it does not. ADR-0049 §4 is the case already in force: a *checked* field is an edge of its check's type, and
a *claimed* field is *read*, by the party that claimed it.

**Rerun what you can.** A supplier's proof that a deployment re-checks is the deployment's proof; one it does not is
the supplier's word.

### 8. The declaration gains its graph, and the gate reads it

ADR-0023 §6's declaration gains one field, **graph**: the overlay by digest and the graded inventory computed from it
*(dependent entry: an ADR-0023 amendment)*. The Build promotion gate that reads §6's schema (§7b) reads the overlay on
the same check: a declaration whose overlay does not parse against the archetype version it names, or whose inventory
is not the one computed from it, fails as a missing composition note fails.

The Adopter's Guide gains:

| # | Item (§9.2) | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| D-g1 | The overlay | Every archetype node is kept, replaced or omitted, and every added node is listed |
| D-g2 | Each replaced node's edges | Re-established with a type, or declared absent |
| D-g3 | The inventory at every grade | Computed from the overlay, each theorem with its grade, route and the standing of its nodes |
| D-g4 | Imported subgraphs | Each with its supplier, and each edge marked rerun or read |
| D-g5 | Upstream reports | Filed for every case §6 names, or stated as none |

### 9. The reference is the first overlay

The reference's overlay, as the corpus stands on 2026.11.06, **read and not computed** *(the checker of ADR-0056 §8
does not exist yet)*:

| From → to | Type | Fresh |
|---|---|---|
| `topak-floor`'s linked stage → `floor/proposed/workflow-floor.cedar` | executed | yes; the stage is Proposed (G11 item 102) |
| `floor/proposed/` stages → `floor/genesis-floor.cedar` | generated | yes |
| `admit.rs` with the floor wrapper → `FloorComposition.tla` `Decide` | proved of a reading | as of `mechanism/lean` |
| `State::apply`, seat and lease arms → `Seats.tla` | proved of a reading, and replayed | **stale**: `mechanism/lean` (G11 item 103) |
| `State::apply` → `Mechanism.tla` | replayed | **stale** for workflows (G11 item 56) |
| Operator path → `Operators.tla` | tested | yes |
| Every other mechanism theorem's route to the code | trusted (T-0045-28) | — |

Read at grades: every floor theorem the stage carries is inherited at *executed*; the two `Seats` theorems at *proved
of a reading* until `mechanism/lean` is brought into step, and at *replayed* or *trusted* while it is not; the
operator theorems at *tested*; every other mechanism theorem at *trusted*. At *trusted* the inventory is Annex B's.

**Two hypothetical adopters**, to show the operations. One replaces the Kafka transport with another log: the
transport carries no ordering authority (ADR-0005), its edges are *tested* against the reference's e2e suite rerun, and
no theorem's grade moves. One writes the mechanism in another language and proves it refines `Seats`, `Lease_A1` and
`FloorComposition` with Verus: those theorems rise to *proved*, its prover is a D-row beside T-0045-31 (§3), and both
the route and the missing row are reported upstream (§5, §6).

### 10. Degeneracy on this record

| Id | Kind | Parameter → deployed value | Premises falsified | Theorems voided | Type changes (ADR-0023 §6b) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D-0057-1** | Declaration | The overlay: published → not published | Every `edge(…)` premise the deployment's own nodes would carry | None at grade *trusted*; every grade above it unknown | None |

A deployment that publishes no overlay has the inventory it has today and cannot say at what grade. Because every edge
from its own nodes is absent, it cannot claim a kept archetype edge either, if it replaced the node above it.

### 11. The trusted base

**No row is added by this record.** A method a deployment brings enters by §3, as a D-row. A supplier's word enters as
*read*, which is a premise and not a row.

### 12. Ratification condition

- **ADR-0056 is ratified**, or is ratified in the same batch.
- **The reference's overlay is computed** by ADR-0056's checker at a named commit, and its inventory at *trusted*
  equals Annex B's.
- **One replacement overlay is computed**, a stand-in replacing one node, and its grade changes and voids are the ones
  §2 predicts.
- **The dependent entries are drafted**: the ADR-0023 amendment (§8) and the Adopter's Guide's §2 and §9.2 entries.

## Options considered

| Option | Disposition |
|---|---|
| **Fold this into ADR-0056** | Not taken ⟨K4⟩. The method is the archetype's and is not a deployment's to weaken; the overlay is what deployments and suppliers use, and it is formalism-neutral |
| **Prescribe the corpus's methods to adopters** | Rejected. An edge is typed by what it leaves, not by its tool (§3) |
| **Let a deployment declare grades without an overlay** | Rejected. A grade is computed from edges; asserted, it is the "declared by the operator and verified by nobody" ADR-0036 R1 names |
| **Read a supplier's claims at their claimed type** | Rejected. A claim not rerun is *read*, by the supplier (§7) |
| **Treat a stronger route as a capability under ADR-0023 §4** | Rejected. It would make the archetype incomplete for want of evidence, not of a capability (§5) |
| **A generic prover row in Annex A now** | Not taken *(drafter's choice)*. Adding a row is a relaxation that needs its own record (ADR-0045 §4); the reports of §6 are its evidence |
| **Ungraded inheritance for adopters, graded for the corpus** | Rejected ⟨K2⟩. The same computation, or the two inventories cannot be compared |

## Consequences

**Positive**
- **An adopter can say what ties their artifacts to the theorems**, and at what strength, in a form the gate reads.
- **Suppliers can publish what they discharge**, and deployments can see what they rerun and what they take on word.
- **The ecosystem feeds the archetype**: stronger routes, missing rows and missing schemas arrive as findings.
- **A claim carries its grade**, so "TOPAK-conformant" stops meaning one thing for a proof and another for a trust
  root.

**Negative**
- **A declaration is larger**, and a deployment needs ADR-0056's checker to produce it.
- **Graded claims invite grade-shopping**: quoting the theorems that grade well. §4 asks the whole inventory to be
  published, and nothing stops a quotation from leaving it out.
- **Everything rests on ADR-0056's order of types** (its finding 4).

---

## Findings

1. **ADR-0049 already grades declarations**, for one schema and in two grades. This record is that distinction,
   generalized.
2. **Annex A has no row for a prover a deployment chose.** Every method beyond TLC, TLAPS and Lean enters as a
   degeneracy, which reads as a weakening even where it strengthens. The archetype-evolution record would be the home
   of a generic row.
3. **A deployment can exceed the archetype's grade and not its inventory.** ADR-0023 §4 reads inventories; grades
   are a second order on deployments, and the archetype is not its maximum.
4. **The reference's strongest edge ends at a Proposed node** (§9). Grade and standing are separate, and a
   declaration that shows one without the other misleads.
5. **A supplier's evidence is one hand's**, as the corpus's is (G11 §4). The reader on a *read* edge is how a
   deployment sees it.
