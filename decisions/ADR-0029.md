# ADR-0029 — The design process is genesis; the custodian may ratify unilaterally; the anchor bootstraps on a drafted TLA+ specification

**Status:** Accepted (genesis) · 2026.09.15 ⟨B25⟩
**Status note:** Confirmation required: **for A2 only (C3)**. The record otherwise defines the phase and carries no marker.
**Pending:** none. A1 ratified 2026.09.23; A2 ratified 2026.09.25.
**Ratification phase:** Genesis. This record defines the phase.
**Scope:** The TOPAK corpus itself: the pattern's own governance, applying Build's recursion to the
design process.
**Depends on:** ADR-0024, ADR-0025, ADR-0026, ADR-0027

---

## Context

Every ADR so far was drafted, reviewed, and ratified by one person. Calling that a custody
degeneracy is wrong: before the anchor exists there is no archetype custody to degenerate from. The
accurate name is **genesis**. Everything ratified in genesis is trusted by declaration and becomes
part of the genesis TCB.

Revision 1 kept genesis minimal by ratifying only the bootstrap set. That protects the TCB baseline
and ADR-0013's review. It also stalls a corpus that is still changing daily, and it would pressure
early recruitment of at least five people before the design merits their time.

Revision 2 takes the middle path:

- **Genesis continues** and ratification is unilateral.
- **Author-only ratification of the highest-risk records is tracked as debt**, repaid by independent
  confirmation after bootstrap.

## Decision

### 1. The current design process is genesis

Genesis custody is declared: **Scott, sole custodian, (1, 0).** This is genesis custody, not a
degeneracy.

### 2. Unilateral ratification is available

During genesis, the custodian may ratify any record that has decision text, at their discretion and
at any time. Debts are recorded on the record's face. Ratification is available, not required; a
record may remain Proposed indefinitely.

A record **without** decision text cannot be ratified. This covers skeletons, open questions, and
unwritten supersessions.

### 3. Status classes during genesis

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Accepted (genesis)** | Ratified by the custodian and trusted by declaration. Post-bootstrap, it changes only through promotion. |
| **Accepted (genesis) · confirmation required** | As above, plus it must pass a **confirmation promotion** after bootstrap (§6). Until confirmed, any publication citing its guarantees says "author-ratified." |
| **Proposed / Draft** | Unratified |

### 4. Confirmation-required criteria

On ratification, a record is marked confirmation-required if it meets **any** of these:

| Criterion | Examples |
|---|---|
| **C1** Carries a proof obligation over privileged paths (erasure, break-glass, exceptions) | 0013, 0028, and the accepted 0006 and 0009 |
| **C2** Carries an exhaustiveness or confinement claim | 0015, 0022 §6 |
| **C3** Supersedes, or reverses in effect, an accepted record | 0008 supersession; 0017 ordering-clause supersession |
| **C4** Resolves a debt owed by an accepted record | 0013 (debts of 0006 and 0009) |

**Minimum set:**
- 0013, 0028, 0015, 0022 §6 (whenever ratified);
- the two supersession records (whenever written);
- 0006 and 0009, marked retroactively under C1.

**Exception: the bootstrap set cannot be confirmation-required.** The confirmation promotion runs
under 0024, 0025, 0026, and 0027. Confirming the rules that confirmation runs under would be
circular. Those records are covered by the genesis attestation (§5) and ADR-0025's intent axiom.

### 5. Bootstrap trigger and ceremony

The trigger and the ceremony are unchanged from revision 1.

**Trigger.** The anchor *may* bootstrap once a drafted TLA+ bootstrap specification exists. It must
contain five modules and five invariants, each invariant with a mutation that must fail:

| Module | Invariant |
|---|---|
| `Genesis` | `GenesisSatisfiesFloor` |
| `Anchor` | `FloorPreserved` |
| `RulesFloor` | ~~`NoPromotionWithoutAllGrantSeats`~~ `PromotionMeetsIssuedQuorum` ⟨A1⟩ |
| `Promotion` | `StaleVotesNeverCount` |
| `BreakGlass` | `BreakGlassNeverChangesAuthority` |

TLC checking at small constants is recommended; it is not required.

**A1, ratified 2026.09.23.** The `RulesFloor` invariant was named for a mechanism that admitted only unanimity.
ADR-0024 revision 5, ratified 2026.09.23, reads `quorum` from the issued rules as either `"all"` or
a grant-vote threshold *k*, bounded by ADR-0026 R1 and R9. `NoPromotionWithoutAllGrantSeats` is
therefore true only of the archetype instance, and a bootstrap module carrying that name would
either check a weaker mechanism than the one deployed or contradict its own title under any
promotion degeneracy. The replacement states the property the floor actually enforces: **a promotion
meets the quorum the issued rules require, and those rules conform to the floor.** Unanimity remains
the archetype value (R1), and R9 keeps a relaxed threshold at two or more.

**Ceremony.**
1. Seat the key-holders under a declared custody record.
2. Assemble the **genesis bundle**. It contains:
   - the corpus manifest at bootstrap, including the confirmation-required set;
   - the TLA+ specification digest, marked checked or drafted-only;
   - the genesis rules.
3. Every key-holder signs the bundle digest. This is **issuance epoch 0**.
4. The genesis bundle is the only artifact never promoted.
5. Genesis rules issue **no erasure roster**.

### 6. First post-bootstrap work: confirmation

Every confirmation-required record goes through a **confirmation promotion**, in dependency order,
under ADR-0024 with author recusal:

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | Status becomes Accepted; the marker is removed |
| **Rejected** | A superseding record is required. Promotion runs normally for it. |

Genesis-ratified records without the marker are **not** re-reviewed. The confirmation set is the
whole of the deferred-review debt.

### 7. No forcing function

Genesis may continue indefinitely. Bootstrap happens when the custodian declares it and the trigger
(§5) is met. In place of a forcing function:

- **Every checkpoint during genesis carries a genesis debt ledger:**
  - the confirmation-required count;
  - the records in that set;
  - whether the bootstrap specification exists.

  The debt is visible, not bounded.

### A2 (ratified 2026.09.25): the entire corpus is reviewed at bootstrap

Revision 2 drew one conclusion too many from the circularity argument. The argument shows the bootstrap set
cannot pass a **confirmation promotion** — the promotion runs under 0024–0027, and confirming those records
under themselves would be circular. It does not show the bootstrap set cannot be **reviewed**. Revision 2
conflated the two, and §6 extended the gap to every unmarked record, so the most load-bearing records in the
corpus were the only ones guaranteed never to see a second reader. The 09.16 critical review named this CR-5;
the 09.25 review graded it the pattern's central weakness (CR-21). This amendment reverses the declaration.

**A2.1 — §5's trigger gains a second condition.** The anchor may bootstrap once (a) the drafted bootstrap
specification exists, as §5 states, **and** (b) an **independent review of the entire corpus** is complete:
every record — the bootstrap set included — the floors and schema, the specification set and its results, the
reference trees at their cited manifests, and the derived documents, all at one named manifest digest. The
review is performed by at least one reviewer who is not the author. Complete means every finding is
dispositioned: applied as a change, declined with recorded reasons, or carried as named debt into the genesis
debt ledger. A change that results re-enters the ordinary drafting path and moves the manifest digest, so
review and corpus converge by iteration, the way the cleanup passes did.

**A2.2 — the genesis bundle carries the review attestation.** §5's bundle gains a fourth content: the review
attestation — reviewer identities, the manifest digest reviewed, the scope, and the findings disposition.
Key-holders sign it with the rest of the bundle at epoch 0. A bootstrap without the attestation is not a
bootstrap under these rules, the same way a solo bootstrap is not a bootstrap.

**A2.3 — §4's exception is narrowed to what the argument proves.** The sentence "the bootstrap set cannot be
confirmation-required" stands: the marker mechanism remains unavailable to 0024–0027. The sentence that
follows it is struck: those records are no longer covered by the genesis attestation and ADR-0025's intent
axiom alone — they are covered by the bootstrap review (A2.1), the genesis attestation, and the intent axiom.
The review happens before epoch 0 and runs under no rule the bootstrap set defines; it is an environment
event, like seating the key-holders. No circularity is incurred.

**A2.4 — §6's no-re-review clause is struck.** "Genesis-ratified records without the marker are not
re-reviewed" is replaced: every record is reviewed at bootstrap under A2.1. The confirmation set remains
exactly the marked records, and their confirmation promotions still run post-bootstrap under ADR-0024 with
author recusal, as §6 says — the bootstrap review is evidence before those promotions, not a substitute for
them. The closing sentence, "the confirmation set is the whole of the deferred-review debt," is struck as
false under this amendment: the deferred-review debt is the whole corpus, and it is repaid at the bootstrap
gate.

**A2.5 — the debt ledger names the reviewer.** §7's ledger gains a line: whether a bootstrap reviewer is
engaged, and the manifest digest of the last completed review pass, if any.

**Marking.** A2 reverses in effect two clauses of an accepted record — this one — and so carries **C3** on
ratification, scoped to the amendment. Its confirmation promotion runs post-bootstrap like any other's; there
is no circularity, because A2 is not part of the bootstrap set.

**Costs, stated.** Bootstrap acquires a liveness dependency on recruiting at least one reviewer; under §7 that
blocks nothing, since genesis may continue indefinitely. The first quorum's confirmation load shrinks: it
inherits dispositioned findings rather than an unreviewed corpus. And the recursion claim strengthens at the
point the 09.25 review graded weakest — the records that define review are themselves reviewed, by the only
mechanism available to them that is not circular: a reader who is not the author, before the rules take
effect.

**On ratification this amendment owes narrative edits:** rev 7 §9 (the "no independent review, ever" passages
and the bootstrap-trigger paragraph), §12 item 3, and §7.1's author-ratified note on ADR-0024; and the
G3-successor's debt ledger gains the A2.5 line. G3 itself is frozen and is not touched.

## Options considered

| Option | Disposition |
|---|---|
| Ratify only the bootstrap set (revision 1) | Superseded. Protects the baseline, but stalls a corpus still changing daily. |
| Unilateral ratification with no confirmation class | Rejected. 0013's non-composition and the forensic floor would be author-only forever. |
| Forcing function ("bootstrap due once the spec passes mutations") | Declined for now. Replaced by the debt ledger (§7). Revisit if the confirmation set exceeds what a first quorum can review. |
| Declare the solo corpus a degeneracy | Rejected. There is no archetype custody before the anchor exists. |

## Consequences

**Positive**
- **Design pace is preserved.** No quorum overhead during genesis.
- **The deferred independent review is a scheduled obligation,** not a lost one.
- **Publication stays honest.** Confirmation-required guarantees are cited as author-ratified until
  confirmed.
- **The recursion claim still holds.** TOPAK governs its own design with its own mechanism, starting
  at bootstrap.

**Negative**
- **The genesis TCB baseline grows** with every unmarked ratification. The non-increasing-TCB
  property is measured from a larger baseline, which makes it easier to satisfy and therefore
  weaker.
- **Reversals of genesis content before bootstrap are author-only.** Two accepted records were
  reversed in effect within 48 hours during this phase.
- **The first quorum inherits the whole confirmation set at once.** Without a forcing function, that
  set can grow without bound.
- **Headcount is unchanged:** at least 5 humans for an archetype bootstrap. A solo bootstrap is not
  a bootstrap.
