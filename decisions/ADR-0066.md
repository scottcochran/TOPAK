# ADR-0066 — An irreversible effect is a person's: an entry declares its effect class, and a `release` seat held by a person at a commanded plane is the only kind that reaches an irreversible entry

**Status:** Proposed · 2026.11.10
**Status note:** Confirmation required under **C1**: a release act is a privileged path, as break-glass is. **C3**
does not apply while this is an overlay ⟨Q11⟩ and would apply on admission: ADR-0032 §4 says an effect with no
timing budget "must not be reachable from the alphabet at all", and §1 makes it reachable by a person. It is
provisional while ADR-0052 is, whose activation it reuses. **Nothing here is modelled, checked or built.**
**Pending:** none.
**Deployment position:** **An overlay, not the archetype** ⟨Q11⟩. No degeneracy of its own.
**Depends on:** ADR-0059 (commanded planes; the charter's `release` field), ADR-0060 (the intent boundary),
ADR-0062 (the governor; the entry's `effect` part), ADR-0064 (ticks), ADR-0052 (the emergency seating, whose
activation this reuses; provisional), ADR-0013 (anchor-issued rosters), ADR-0058 (overlays; Proposed)
**Relates to:** ADR-0032 §4 (`T_irrev`, and what to do when no budget fits), ADR-0037 (seat kinds), ADR-0049 (the
allowlist entry), ADR-0007 (no semantic judgment is an enabling condition), ADR-0067 (`FD5`, `FD6`, `FD9`)
**Closes:** nothing.
**Carries a debt against:** nothing while this is an overlay. §6 lists what the archetype's records would owe.

---

## Context

The custodian put it as: irreversible effects are human, and asked whether that is HITL ⟨4⟩. **It is**, in the
glossary's sense of a posture defined by its enabling condition: an irreversible effect is enabled only by a signed
act of a person, made for that one effect.

**What the corpus says today.** For an effect irreversible on emission, "the effect must not be reachable from the
alphabet at all" (ADR-0032 §4; A10). The budget `W + L + D < T_irrev` is HOTL's: it buys time to detect and stop
after emission. Under HITL the judgment comes before the emission, and no budget is needed. The precedent for a seat
whose actor is a person is the `emergency` seat (ADR-0052; ToO §3.3).

---

## Decision

### 1. An entry declares its effect class, and an irreversible entry is reachable by a person only ⟨4⟩

The `effect` part of an allowlist entry (ADR-0062 §3) declares the class, `reversible` or `irreversible`, with its
`T_irrev`. It is promoted with the bundle and voted like any entry.

**An irreversible entry is not reachable from any agent seat's alphabet, is not `local`, and is not reachable below
the intent boundary.** It is reachable from one seat kind, which a person holds.

**An effect class is a declaration**, as a label is (A15). An irreversible effect declared reversible is seen by
nothing here.

### 2. `release` is a new seat kind, activated as an emergency seating is ⟨Q6⟩

- **A person holds it**, with a key, and nobody else in the round. No model's Persona binds to the kind.
- **It is seated at a commanded plane and never at an autonomous one**, and only where the charter carries
  `release` (ADR-0059 §1).
- **A roster's quorum activates it** for one named person and one round, as an emergency seating is activated
  ⟨Q6⟩. The roster is anchor-issued (ADR-0013), so no plane below the root can mint a person, and a governor checks
  the key against the roster resident on the vehicle.
- **Its alphabet is `Actuate` on irreversible entries in its envelope, and `Report`.** An emergency envelope is the
  domain's whole allowlist. A release envelope is the opposite, which is why the two kinds are kept apart.

### 3. The person is the actor ⟨Q5⟩

The person composes and signs the act. **A stack may `Report` what it perceives. That report is ingress to the
person, labelled `untrusted`, and it enables nothing.** A design in which an agent proposes and a person approves
would put the agent's output on the path to the effect.

### 4. Each act is one admit, delivered with a certificate, and fresh by the plane's ticks ⟨Q16⟩

- **The act names** the entry, its inputs, the vehicle, the fences, and **a tick of the vehicle's plane**
  (ADR-0064).
- **It is ordered at the commanded plane** and delivered with that plane's commit certificate (ADR-0059 §5). The
  autonomous plane relays it. It can refuse one and cannot originate one.
- **The governor's interlock checks** the person's key, the commanded plane's certificate, that the entry is in the
  seat's envelope, that the act is unused, and that it is **fresh: no more than a declared number of ticks past the
  tick it names** ⟨Q16⟩. An act an adversary withholds arrives aged and is refused. This is the third case of the
  NATO reading's F1.
- **The plane must be in contact with the intent boundary.** With no link there is no irreversible effect.

### 5. What the mechanism records, and what it cannot judge

It records who acted, on what, through which vehicle and when. It does not record why: "the reason is unknown to
the mechanism, by construction" (ADR-0052). Whether the act was right is the person's judgment, and the mechanism
checks none of it. It also "cannot tell a person from a program holding the person's key" (ToO §3.3). A5 applies in
full: a trained, authorized person is present, and this record adds that they can see what the act requires them
to see.

### 6. Overlay position, and what is reported upstream ⟨Q11⟩

Reported under ADR-0058 §6: **an effect class on an entry**, and **an irreversible effect expressible by a person**.

**If the archetype takes them, its records owe:** ADR-0032 §4, relaxed from "not reachable at all" to "reachable by
a person and by nobody else", which is C3; ADR-0037, the seat kind `release`; ADR-0049, the entry's `effect` part;
the floor's `B10-only-thinkers-author`, an exception for a `release` seat's `Actuate`, as it has for an `emergency`
seat.

### 7. Ratification condition

- **ADR-0059, ADR-0060, ADR-0062 and ADR-0064 are ratified first**, or with this record, and ADR-0052 is no longer
  provisional.
- **A module**, `Release`, states at least `IrreversibleIsAPersons`: every irreversible effect has a person-signed
  predecessor ordered at a commanded plane, used once and no older than its declared ticks. Mutations
  `M_AGENTRELEASE`, `M_LITERELEASE`, `M_REPLAY` and `M_LATEFIRST`. And an invariant over `ENABLED`, in the shape of
  `Emergency_live.tla`: while a release lease is live and the plane is in contact, a release call can be admitted
  whatever any agent or Observer is doing. **Not met.**
- **The floor** carries `FD5`, `FD6` and `FD9`, and the relaxation of `B10` routes as one (ADR-0067).
- **The reference** shows a release act admitted; one refused under an agent's seat; one refused at an autonomous
  plane; a second use refused; and a late first delivery refused. **Not met.**
- **A reader who is not the drafter** (ADR-0029 A2).

## Options considered

| Option | Disposition |
|---|---|
| **Irreversible effects inexpressible**, ADR-0032 §4 as written | Not taken ⟨4⟩. The deployment this set describes has them |
| **An agent's seat with a budget** `W + L + D < T_irrev` | Rejected. For an effect irreversible on emission no budget fits, which is ADR-0032's own conclusion |
| **The person countersigns an agent's proposal** | Not taken ⟨Q5⟩. The agent's output would be on the path to the effect, and approval can become a click |
| **Reuse the `emergency` kind** | Not taken ⟨Q6⟩. Its envelope would have to admit irreversible entries, which widens it everywhere |
| **A release seat at an autonomous plane** | Rejected. Every effect is admitted at the intent boundary (ADR-0060 §1), and a plane with no people has nobody to seat |
| **Freshness by a challenge from the governor** | Not taken ⟨Q16⟩. Stronger, at one more round trip before every irreversible effect |
| **A release act that works with no link** | Rejected. It would be a standing grant of an irreversible effect |

## Consequences

**Positive**
- **No agent output is on the path to an irreversible effect.**
- **One act, one effect, one person, on the record.**
- **A withheld act cannot be used late.**

**Negative**
- **With no link there is no irreversible effect**, and an adversary who jams the link gets that for nothing. The
  boundary register criticizes "fail-closed uniformity presented as costless" (ToO §10). Here it is presented as a
  cost, and accepted.
- **ADR-0032 §4 is relaxed.** An irreversible effect is expressible.
- **The mechanism does not make an act lawful or right**, and it records a wrong one without preventing it.
- **HITL is a key and not a judgment** unless a trained, authorized person holds it and can see what the act
  requires.
- **One author, one day.**
