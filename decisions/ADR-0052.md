# ADR-0052 — Break-glass is an emergency seating: a roster's quorum seats a person for one round, and the floor's exception for it is retired

**Status:** Proposed · 2026.10.04
**Status note:** Confirmation required under **C1** (it replaces a privileged path), **C3** (ADR-0009's accepted text
makes break-glass a floor exception, and ADR-0041 §1 and §3 state X1; both are reversed) and **C4** (CR-29; G9 items 8
and 49). C2 does not apply: no confinement claim is made, and an emergency seat is `Mediated`. **A seat kind is
added**, `emergency`, and **a floor exception is removed**.
**Pending:** none.

---

## Context

Break-glass today is floor exception X1: under a quorum on the break-glass roster, a Run seat may `Actuate` a tool
outside its allowlist and inside an issued envelope (ADR-0041 §3). The discussion of 2026.10.04 found four things
about it.

- **Its one unique function is narrow.** Stopping something is done by `Revoke`, `EndRound` and the fence, with no
  quorum. Adding authority is done by promotion. What break-glass alone does is add authority faster than a
  promotion, on a roster that does not need the promotion's unanimity or its gates.
- **It elevates the agent.** ADR-0009 began from a person overriding automation, "at 223 feet with hands on the
  controls". X1 gives an agent's seat more tools on people's approval. Practice elsewhere elevates a named person.
- **It had no bound in time.** An approval is scoped to a seat and an epoch (`mechanism/` I9). R8 would have bounded
  an issued TTL and was withdrawn the same day, because a TTL needs trusted time.
- **It is the second carve-out against the floor.** ADR-0009 says so itself. With ADR-0050 the general mechanism can
  express what X1 expresses: a quorum seats a principal with a wider allowlist.

---

## Decision

### 1. An emergency seat is held by a person, and its kind is `emergency`

- **A fifth seat kind.** A seat of kind `emergency` is declared in the issued rules, in the Run domain. Its alphabet
  is `Consult`, `Actuate` and `Report`. It does not vote, draft or submit.
- **Only a person holds one.** The person has a Persona record with a signing key, as an operator has (ADR-0050 §3),
  and holds no other seat in the round (ADR-0037 §7). The mechanism cannot tell a person from a program holding the
  person's key; this is a rule about what is seated, as it is for operators.
- **The person acts, and no agent does.** Every action under the lease is signed with the person's key. No model's
  Persona binds to the kind.
- **It is `Mediated`.** A person is not confined. The envelope of §2 bounds what the seat may do through the front
  door, and nothing here bounds what a person does elsewhere.
- **Its key does not depend on single sign-on** *(drafter's choice)*. The roster's quorum is the attestation of the
  Persona at the lease boundary (ADR-0016 §6): the approvals name the key. An emergency path that needs the identity
  provider fails when the provider does.

### 2. The envelope is prepared by promotion, and activated by the roster's quorum

| Step | What | By whom | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Prepare** | An **emergency seating**: the seat, the persons who may hold it, its envelope (by default the domain's, below), and the labels a task for it may carry (ADR-0050 §2, §5) | A promotion | In advance |
| **Activate** | One lease on that seating, for one named person and one round, with the round's task | A quorum on the break-glass roster the anchors issued (ADR-0013 §1) | At the emergency |

- **The envelope is generic by default** ⟨R1⟩ (the custodian's decision). An emergency seating that declares its
  envelope as *the domain's* is given, at activation, **every Run allowlist entry in force in the domain**: the
  entries of every bundle revision that a seating in force in the domain names, with no lease's narrowing applied,
  together with the entries of the emergency seating's own bundle. The mechanism computes it from the log at the
  activation's index, and it is fixed for the round. It cannot go stale: a tool promoted yesterday is in it today,
  and nobody has to keep an emergency envelope in step.
- **Emergency-only tools are promoted ahead of time**, in the emergency seating's own bundle: a tool to isolate or to
  roll back, which no ordinary seat's allowlist holds.
- **A deployment may prepare a narrower one.** A seating that names its envelope entry by entry is given exactly
  that. Several seatings may exist; the roster's quorum activates one.
- **The person is told what is in it.** The tool list of the round (ADR-0048 §2) is the envelope, delivered at the
  start of the round and written to the topic as its opening record.
- **No entry is general-purpose.** An envelope is made of tools each held to the channel its entry declares
  (ADR-0049 §5). A shell, or a client that reaches any address, is not an entry the corpus admits anywhere, and an
  emergency does not admit one.
- **Activation is the roster's act alone.** It needs no operator's request, no promotion's unanimity and no gate. That
  is the speed break-glass exists for.
- **The envelope is an ordinary allowlist.** Its tools are entries in ADR-0049's form, with labels, destinations and
  channels declared. The emergency seat's calls are admitted by the ordinary allowlist permit.
- **So the floor needs no exception for it.** `X1-break-glass` is retired. The floor's exceptions are one, erasure
  (ADR-0013 §3; ADR-0026 R6).
- **The label forbids apply** (ADR-0047 F1 to F3; ADR-0041 A1's R5 to R7). A person holding the seat who has read
  untrusted content in the clear takes a destination from a handle like anyone else.

### 3. One round, and nothing after it

**An activation is scoped to the round** (the custodian's decision). The lease it grants is round-coterminous, as
every lease is (ADR-0031 §2).

- **It ends with its round**: at the round bound `R`, at the lease's `ttl`, which is at most `T_persona` on the local
  monotonic clock (ADR-0031 A2), or when the person ends it.
- **It ends sooner** at an epoch increment or a targeted revocation, as every lease does (ADR-0031 §5). Any operator
  may revoke it (ADR-0050 §4).
- **It has no successor and no renewal** *(drafter's choice on renewal)*. An operator's `Regrant` or `Renew` naming an
  emergency seat is refused. Another round is another quorum.
- **This is the time box.** It uses the clock the corpus already accepts for a lease and adds none. R8 stays
  withdrawn: no TTL is issued and none is read from the rules.

### 4. An emergency seat is not coupled to an Observer, and is read by everyone who can stop it

- **`R3-observer-live` does not reach it.** R3 is a forbid on a `thinker` seat (ADR-0039 §2; ADR-0041 §1), and an
  emergency seat is not one. The custodian's decision that break-glass does not wait on a dead Observer is kept, and
  now needs no exemption written into R3.
- **Nothing acts unobserved on an agent's judgment.** What goes uncoupled is a person, seated by a quorum, for one
  round.
- **Its topic is read by the domain's operators**, who are in its `W(p)`, and by a cell Observer if one is seated and
  live. A finding is a deny any of them can make (ADR-0050 §6).
- **The correlator reads it** as it reads every topic in its domain.

### 5. What is recorded, and what is not

- **The activation is a forensic-floor record** (ADR-0028 F5), as a break-glass invocation is today: the roster
  members who approved, the person seated, the seating, the round and the task's digest and label. Erasure does not
  remove it (ADR-0013 NC-2).
- **Every call leaves its record** on the round topic (ADR-0042 §3), with the seat's kind.
- **The reason is unknown to the mechanism, by construction** (the custodian's decision). Why the quorum acted is a
  claim about the world. A field for it would be prose that nothing checks, and a mechanism that recorded it would
  seem to know something it does not (ADR-0007). What is recorded is who approved, whom, for what envelope and for
  which round. The person may `Report` a rationale; that is content on the topic, and no admit turns on it.

### 6. The path is exercised

**The emergency path is exercised at least once in each domain in each epoch, and periodically after that** (the
custodian's decision).

- **A drill is a real activation**: the real roster, a real quorum, a real lease for one round. Its seating is a
  **drill seating**, promoted like any other, whose envelope holds one tool with no outward channel *(drafter's
  choice)*. A drill therefore proves the roster, the keys and the path, and can do nothing.
- **A drill is on the log like any activation**, marked by its seating.
- **The period is the deployment's to declare**, counted in rounds of the domain and not in time *(drafter's choice)*,
  so that it can be read from the log with no clock.
- **It is an obligation with evidence, and it gates nothing** *(drafter's choice)*. A real activation is not refused
  because no drill has been run: an emergency early in an epoch is still an emergency. What the log shows is whether
  the obligation was met, epoch by epoch, and a correlator fold or an audit may count it.
- **A new epoch asks for a new drill.** An epoch increment fences every lease, and it is the moment the path is most
  likely to have gone stale.

### 7. What this record does not cover

- **Stopping.** `Revoke`, `EndRound` and the fence are ADR-0050's and need no emergency seat.
- **Erasure**, which stays a floor exception with its own roster.
- **A person acting outside the mechanism.**
- **Build.** There is no emergency seat in Build; a promotion is Build's way to add authority.
- **Whether the reason was good.** That is the review after the round, by people.

## Consequences

**Positive**
- **The floor has one exception, not two.** The tension ADR-0009 recorded with the floor's minimality is relieved.
- **A person acts in an emergency, and is named.**
- **The grant is bounded** by a round, with no new clock and no rules-floor entry.
- **The path does not wait on an Observer** and needs no exemption to say so.
- **The path is known to work**, because it is exercised.
- **One mechanism.** An emergency is a seating, a lease and an allowlist, checked by what checks every other.

**Negative**
- **The person holds the widest authority in the domain for the round** ⟨R1⟩, under the default envelope. What
  bounds it is the quorum that seated them, the one round, any operator's revocation, and the label forbids.
- **An emergency that needs a capability no tool in the domain has takes a promotion**, or a person acting outside
  the mechanism. X1 had the same limit: its envelope was issued in advance too. The first draft stated this as "an
  emergency has to have been anticipated", which overstated it; with a generic envelope nothing about the emergency
  itself need be foreseen ⟨R1⟩.
- **A person must be available who holds the key**, and the roster's quorum with them.
- **An agent cannot be given more in an emergency.** If the work needs the agent, the person directs it by other
  means or does it themselves. That is the point, and it is a cost.
- **The label forbids bind the person** (§2). In an emergency that will be felt.
- **A drill is a real quorum, every epoch in every domain**, and again on the declared period.
- **Five accepted records move**, two of them by reversal, and two frozen modules owe successors.
- **One author, one day.** The module and its proof are the drafter's as well (§9).

---

## Findings

1. **Break-glass did less than its name suggests.** Of the four things asked of it, three are done elsewhere: the
   stop by revocation, the addition by promotion, the record by the forensic floor. What was left was speed.
2. **X1 was the general mechanism's special case.** Once a quorum can seat a principal on a prepared allowlist
   (ADR-0050), an exception that lets a seat call outside its allowlist says the same thing in the floor's own
   language, at the cost of a carve-out.
3. **The round was the time box all along.** A lease already ends with its round on a clock the corpus accepts.
   R8 looked for a second clock because the approval was scoped to the epoch and not to a lease.
4. **Making the kind do the work removed an exemption.** R3 is written over `thinker` seats. A seat that is not one
   is not coupled, and nothing has to be excepted.
5. **"No reason recorded" is a property, not an omission.** The corpus never lets a judgment be a lift. Writing that
   down keeps a later reader from adding the field.
6. **The limit of an emergency path is what has been promoted, and it always was.** X1 reached tools "inside the
   issued break-glass envelope". A generic envelope moves the limit as far as it can go without a new tool: to
   everything the domain already has.
