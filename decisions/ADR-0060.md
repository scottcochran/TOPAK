# ADR-0060 — Every effect is admitted at the intent boundary: a reversible one as an intent bounded by the promoted seating, and below it an autonomous plane with no path that changes authority

**Status:** Proposed · 2026.11.10
**Status note:** Confirmation required under **C1**, because an intent is a grant one operator makes, and under
**C2**, because §3 claims that an autonomous plane's log holds no act that changes authority, which is an
exhaustiveness claim. **C3** does not apply while this is an overlay ⟨Q11⟩ and would apply on admission: §2 changes
what bounds a successor grant under ADR-0050, which is accepted and provisional. **Modelled, checked and proved in TLA+ ⟨R1⟩
(§7); nothing is built.**
**Pending:** none.
**Deployment position:** **An overlay, not the archetype** ⟨Q11⟩. The autonomous plane is, in the corpus's terms, a
declared degeneracy with additions (§4).
**Depends on:** ADR-0059 (planes, charters, the kinds of narrowing), ADR-0050 (the operator and the successor grant;
provisional), ADR-0054 and ADR-0055 (workflows, the mesh and its budget; both Proposed), ADR-0015 (effects and
authority changes are enumerated), ADR-0058 (overlays; Proposed)
**Relates to:** ADR-0023 (degeneracy), ADR-0037 (seat kinds), ADR-0061 to ADR-0066
**Closes:** nothing.
**Carries a debt against:** nothing while this is an overlay. §6 lists what the archetype's records would owe.

---

## Context

The custodian put two things as settled and one as a question. Each effect is admitted at the last commander's
intent boundary, before the last autonomous plane ⟨10⟩. And is that last plane a "TOPAK lite" ⟨11⟩?

**A command chain decides by one commander, and the corpus decides by quorum.** ADR-0050 already says where one
person acts: "the quorum acts once per seating, and round by round one operator acts" (ToO §7.10). This record puts
the commander there, inside what a quorum voted and not above it.

**The corpus refuses ladders.** "Degeneracy, not a ladder" (ToO §8.1). So "lite" needs a definition that is not a
smaller copy of the pattern.

---

## Decision

### 1. Every effect is admitted at the intent boundary ⟨10⟩

A **commanded plane** has people seated at it. The **intent boundary** is the last commanded plane above a group of
vehicles. The authorizing admit of every effect is ordered there.

| Effect | What is admitted at the intent boundary | By whom | What happens below |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reversible | An **intent**: a task, an envelope, a budget of activations and an expiry | The commander, an operator | The autonomous plane grants each lease inside the intent (ADR-0062) |
| Irreversible | The effect itself, one at a time | A person at a release seat (ADR-0066) | The plane relays it. It can refuse one and cannot originate one |

**This keeps ADR-0015's sentence one level up.** "Every effect therefore has a consensus-ordered, signed,
authorizing predecessor" (ToO §4.1), and here the predecessor is the intent, ordered at a commanded plane. **What is
admitted for a reversible effect is an envelope, and not the act.** The commander voted for, or signed, everything
the envelope allows.

**The predecessor is ordered before the effect and need not be live at it** ⟨R1⟩. An intent that is ended, revoked or
fenced before its `expiry` leaves a lease under it live until the news arrives, so an effect can follow the intent's
end. ADR-0063 bounds how many.

### 2. An intent is a charter for one round, bounded by the promoted seating ⟨Q4⟩

An intent names the round's task, an envelope, an activation budget, an `expiry` and the tick the `expiry` counts
from (ADR-0064). The charter gains `round` and `task` for it.

- **The first intent comes from a quorum.** The mesh is promoted at the intent boundary with its seatings and its
  budget (ADR-0055), and that tally grants the first charter.
- **Later intents come from one person.** This is ADR-0050's successor grant. The request is an add, so it needs a
  live lease and a live peer.
- **Every field of an intent is the promoted seating's or narrower** ⟨Q4⟩. ADR-0050 bounds a successor by the last
  lease. Under that rule a commander who narrowed an intent for one phase could not restore the mission's bounds
  without a quorum, and would learn not to narrow.
- **An intent ends** at its `expiry`, when its budget is spent, when the commander ends or revokes it, or when any
  plane above is fenced (ADR-0059 §6).

### 3. The autonomous plane is defined by one absence ⟨11⟩ ⟨Q2⟩ ⟨Q3⟩

**An autonomous plane has no path that changes authority.** ADR-0015 enumerates authority changes as exactly
promotion and anchor issuance, and it has neither. No person holds a seat at it. "Lite" is a nickname for that.

**This is a claim about an honest plane's log** ⟨R1⟩. A captured plane is a havoc issuer (ADR-0059 §4): it can sign a
lease under an intent that has ended, and its log is not evidence. What bounds it is the chain check and ADR-0063.

| Absent | Present |
|---|---|
| Build: no authoring and no promotion | The admit path, for a grant and for an `ordered` entry (ADR-0062) |
| Grant seats, operators, release seats: no person | The lease, the floor, every bundle on the chain, the fences |
| Anchor issuance, new bundles, seating of planes | The workflow interpreter: a mesh, its activations and its budget |
| Any grant but one | **One grant:** a node's lease, made by the mechanism under a live intent, "with every field the seating's or narrower" (ToO §2.11) |
| | Subtraction: the end of a round, a revocation, an Observer's finding, expiry |
| | A step of its charter's shrink schedule (ADR-0061) |

**Its replicas run on the vehicles' governors** ⟨Q3⟩. A relay node with no stack may host one, and is declared.

By role: T is the same modules, and nothing of T runs on a vehicle. O is the same floor, linked in, with bundles in
Wasm only (ADR-0067). P is Byzantine agreement among the replicas (ADR-0061). A is stacks only. K is the governors'
radios as transport, with the archive above (ADR-0063, ADR-0065).

### 4. It is a declared degeneracy and an addition, not a second pattern ⟨Q2⟩

- **A degenerate plane.** It omits Build, the people and Kafka. A deployment declares that under ADR-0058.
- **An addition.** Charters, commit certificates and operation with no person present are capabilities the
  archetype lacks. ADR-0023 §4 says what that means: the archetype is incomplete, and the report is a finding
  about it (§6).

### 5. Missions are workflows, and a person may route at a commanded plane ⟨Q10⟩

- **A DAG is a plan with branches**, promoted at a commanded plane (ADR-0054). **A person may hold an orchestrator
  seat there** ⟨Q10⟩ and chooses among edges the mechanism has already enabled. A person is `Mediated`, as an
  operator is: attributed, and not contained.
- **A mesh is promoted at the intent boundary and runs at the autonomous plane** (ADR-0055). A node is one round of
  one cell, and the activation budget is the intent's.
- **"Missions are promoted bundles"** is read as: one tally votes a mission's bundle revision, its mesh, and the
  charters and seatings it needs, together.

**Postures.** An irreversible effect is HITL, and needs the link (ADR-0066). A reversible effect is HOTL while the
plane is in contact with the intent boundary, and **unattended** when it is not. A5 says unattended operation is
something "the pattern does not model". **Nothing a cut-off plane does may rest on a person's response**; what
bounds it is ADR-0063.

### 6. Overlay position, and what is reported upstream ⟨Q11⟩

Reported under ADR-0058 §6: **operation with no person seated**, and **a grant by one operator bounded by a seating
and not by the last lease**.

**If the archetype takes them, its records owe:** ADR-0050, the successor bound for intents (§2), which is C3;
ADR-0054, a person in an orchestrator seat; ADR-0037, the seat kinds; ADR-0055, where a mesh runs; A5 and ToO §13,
restated for a plane with nobody in reach.

### 7. Ratification condition

- **ADR-0059 is ratified first**, or with this record. ADR-0050 is no longer provisional, and ADR-0054 and ADR-0055

- **A module** states at least `EveryEffectHasAnIntent`, `IntentNarrows` and `AutonomousAddsNothing`, with
  `M_NOINTENT`, `M_WIDEINTENT` and `M_LITEPROMOTES`. **Met for TLC and TLAPS** ⟨R1⟩: `spec/Intent.tla`
  (`0c90ea5004c4f127`), results `spec/Intent-results-2026.11.10.txt` (`16fe802076da93ee`), 35 rows from
  `spec/Intent-rows.txt`, all as expected; proof `spec/Intent_proof.tla` (`0b81c0025abb588c`), results
  `spec/Intent-proof-results-2026.11.10.txt` (`d903be71c6ca403f`) and
  `spec/Intent-proof-cert-results-2026.11.10.txt` (`6bd2addd6d14054b`): 677 obligations proved, 5 of them LS4 temporal

## Options considered

| Option | Disposition |
|---|---|
| **A person on every plane** | The corpus as it stands. A group that loses its link then has nobody to act |
| **Each act admitted at the intent boundary** | Not taken. It is HITL for every act, and stops with the link |
| **An intent bounded by the last intent**, as ADR-0050 has a successor | Not taken ⟨Q4⟩ |
| **Every intent by quorum** | Not taken (ADR-0059 ⟨Q1⟩) |
| **"Lite" as a second, named profile** | Rejected ⟨Q2⟩. It is the ladder ADR-0023 refuses |
| **Replicas on relay nodes only** | Not taken ⟨Q3⟩. The plane would be as exposed as its relays, and vehicles would be its clients |
| **Agents only in orchestrator seats** | Not taken ⟨Q10⟩ |

## Consequences

**Positive**
- **Tempo.** Between votes, one commander issues the next intent.
- **Narrowing is not a one-way door**, so commanders can narrow.
- **A plane with no person cannot add authority**, by having no path for it.

**Negative**
- **Nobody at the intent boundary sees the individual act.** The envelope is what was authorized.
- **Unattended operation**, which the corpus had left unmodelled.
- **It rests on three records that are not settled**: ADR-0050, ADR-0054 and ADR-0055.
- **One author, one day.**
