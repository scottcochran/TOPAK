ADR-0045 A3), with the rule that keeps a proof from them and three errata. A scan and the custodian's reading of the
67 steps it lists say that no result in the twenty-four proof modules rests on either case. No check detects the
first case. The defects are not reported upstream, by the custodian's decision.

**Two records were drafted and are Proposed**: **ADR-0054**, a workflow as a promoted DAG, and **ADR-0055**, a

floor's `workflow` stage, which is not in force**, at the custodian's direction. As at G10's build, the reference

## 5. Conventions carried at G11

G2 to G10's conventions stand. Added since G10:

- **A second method proves again, and certifies nothing the first did not.** A Lean derivation of a TLAPS theorem is
  recorded beside it, as evidence. The premise table keeps the proved row on T-0045-31 and names the Lean route as a
  derived premise (ADR-0053 §5, R7).
- **A Lean result counts with its replay.** `leanchecker --fresh` replays every constant through the kernel, because
  `#print axioms` alone can be deceived (ADR-0053 finding 1).
- **Every Lean results file names the digests of what it transcribes**, and `tools/lean-sync.sh` holds the packages
  to the tree and the pin as `spec/proof-sync.sh` holds the proofs.
- **An encoding is checked against TLC's state graph**, and a model of code against recorded traces. Neither is only
  proved, and neither is only read.
- **A frozen module's liveness is stated in an extension, `_live`**, not in a successor. Under the base's mutations
  its next-state relation is the base's, and `SpecL => Spec` is proved, so its safety carries over.
- **A proof is written to the prover's known defects**: an untranslated term behind a hidden definition, no definition
  expanded at an argument that names one of its bound variables, and the scan run on every new proof module
  (`spec/proof-certification.md` §10; ADR-0046 A2).
- **A remainder with no bound is checked under a stated cap**, by the custodian's decision, and the cap is a row
  (ADR-0054 R2, `CapLog`; ADR-0055 R1, `CapOnePast`).
- **What the custodian settled in a design discussion is marked where it is used** (⟨K1⟩ to ⟨K9⟩ in ADR-0054, ⟨M1⟩
  to ⟨M5⟩ in ADR-0055), and every other choice is the drafter's.

## 8. What G11 does not claim

- **Nothing here is independently reviewed.** Two batches were ratified on two days. The records, the amendments, the
  Lean packages, the modules, the proofs, the floor stage and the reference are one hand's. The custodian read one
  transcription and 67 steps.
- **The invariants are proved, under named hypotheses, by the prover T-0045-31, and the mutations and witnesses are
  checked by TLC. Neither is a claim about the running reference** (T-0045-28). **Fifteen modules' invariants are
  also proved in Lean of a re-encoding**, on T-0045-37, where ADR-0053 counted thirteen at its ratification. Two
  functions of the reference and the floor's totality are proved in Lean, of a transcription and under Cedar's model.
- **The pinned prover can prove false statements.** Two ways are known. A scan and a reading say that no result a
  record cites rests on either, and no check detects the first. A certified proof is not a verified one.
- **A Lean proof of the code is not a proof of the code.** It is of a transcription or a model, and the package is
  stale at this cut.
- **No liveness is proved.** Leads-to properties are checked by TLC at small constants, for coupling. Two `ENABLED`
  invariants are proved, for the emergency call.
- **The workflow records are Proposed, and the reference follows them**, on a floor that is not in force.
- **The operator design does not stand on evidence**, and **break-glass has no settled form**, as at G10.
- **No floor is ratified.** A floor stated in Lean is not a floor found fit.
- **A label is a declaration and not a finding**, as at G10.
- **No real principal is `Contained`**, as at G10. An operator and an emergency seat are `Mediated` by design.
- **The reference is not the mechanism** (ADR-0033 §1).
- **The values are placeholders**, and **detection still fails open against a colluding Observer**.
- **Bundles are signed under a throwaway key**; gate evidence is still an assertion.
- **The narrative is a revision behind** (item 100).
- **No deployment conforms, and no deployment declaration has been written.**
- **The corpus is not closed.**
