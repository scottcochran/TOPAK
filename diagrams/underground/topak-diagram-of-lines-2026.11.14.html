Drawn 2026.11.14 · state of record: Genesis Checkpoint G11

TOPAK, as a diagram of lines

The pattern redrawn the way a 1930s pocket railway map draws a city: straight lines, 45-degree bends, evenly spaced stations and one river. An action boards at a Thinker's seat and rides the red line. It reaches the world only if it gets across the log.

The river is the consensus log
North of it a request is decided. South of it an effect is performed. Nothing reaches the south bank without being ordered first, and a denial never crosses.
A line is one path of authority or data
A solid line is an accepted record. A line with a pale core is provisional. A broken line is Proposed, drawn as under construction.
A footnote mark is the reference's state
† not built, ‡ simulated, § partial, as the Architecture Description reports the reference implementation. The map itself draws the archetype.

The network

Ten lines, 47 stations. Pick a line to follow it on its own. Hover a station for what happens there.

On a narrow screen the map scrolls sideways. Grid letters and numbers on the frame match the Sq. column of the index.

Line diagrams

The strips that hang above a carriage's windows: one line each, every stop in order. Two of the four are lines the network map does not carry, because their records are Proposed.

Admit line

In force · the reference builds it

Decide, then order, then effect. An action that is refused leaves the line before the log, and nothing reaches the south bank without crossing it.

  1. 1
    Front Door

    One action of the alphabet arrives, with its epoch and its lease.

  2. 2
    Epoch Fence

    Local expiry, then the fence. A fenced epoch is refused by the mechanism, not by policy.

  3. 3
    Lease

    The lease is live, and the action is in the alphabet it names.

  4. 4
    Lift

    A syntactic lift to one frozen input. What the model meant is not consulted.

  5. 5
    Policy Floor

    Cedar answers Allow, the forbids that fired, or no permit matched. An error is a deny.

  6. 6
    Bundle

    Evaluated at the lease's bundle revision. Anything other than allow is a refusal. Refused here or earlier: denied, recorded unordered, digests only.

  7. 7
    Apply

    Across the log as an Admit entry carrying the input digest and both decisions. Fence, lease and alphabet are checked again; if one moved, the admit is void and has no effect.

  8. 8
    Effect

    Performed only now. A crash between commit and effect leaves no effect.

  9. 9
    Round Topic

    The emission, with log index, fence and sequence. The Observer reads to the new sequence number.

Build-Run pipeline

ADR-0069 · Proposed · under construction

One ordered path from intake to rollout, keyed by digest. No stage starts on a name, the stages are not reordered, and an artifact that stops at any stage has no egress. Stage 4's carrier for this repository is built and runs. Nothing else on this line is modelled, checked or built.

  1. 0
    Intake

    Takes objects with their sources. Produces a corpus-root revision, promoted. The only way into a corpus root.

  2. 1
    Round

    Takes a corpus-root revision, a seating and a tool list. Produces leases at a fresh fence.

  3. 2
    Author

    Read, Draft, Invoke, Emit. Produces a workspace and round topics.

  4. 3
    Submit and Seal

    Takes the workspace. Produces the artifact digest and the sealed topic-set digest.

  5. 4
    Gates

    Takes the artifact digest. Produces gate evidence and its digest, counted once an issued key signs it.

  6. 5
    Tally

    Takes artifact, evidence and epoch. Produces the promotion entry in the log. The tally is the log's.

  7. 6
    Sign

    Takes the promotion entry. Produces a signature over the artifact digest.

  8. 7
    Publish

    Takes the artifact and its signature. Produces a content-addressed copy. Publishing locates.

  9. 8
    Roll Out

    Takes a promoted, signed revision. Produces leases naming it. Rollout ends rounds.

Read from ADR-0069 §1 to §8.

Command-chain extension

ADR-0059 to ADR-0068 · Proposed · an overlay, not the archetype

What follows if TOPAK is the top tier of a command chain. Planes are seated down a tree under charters that only narrow. Nothing here is in force or built, and a deployment with one plane takes nothing from it.

  1. 1
    Root Plane

    The anchor's key and the root's tally, which seats each child plane by voting its charter. One floor, in Cedar. (ADR-0059)

  2. 2
    Commanded Planes

    Any number, and where people are seated. Every field of a charter is the parent's or narrower. (ADR-0059 §3)

  3. 3
    Intent Boundary

    The last commanded plane above a group of vehicles. Every effect's authorizing admit is ordered here. A release seat, held by a person, is the only kind that reaches an irreversible entry. (ADR-0060, ADR-0066)

  4. 4
    Autonomous Plane

    Seven replicas under Byzantine agreement, on the vehicles' governors. It grants leases and changes no authority, and its clock is its ticks. (ADR-0060, ADR-0061, ADR-0064)

  5. 5
    Governor

    The trusted part of a vehicle. It permits each entry under the lease against an exact count, with no further round. A vehicle that stops hearing its plane loses its lease at once. (ADR-0062)

  6. 6
    Stack

    Planning, perception, any model: Byzantine. It chooses which permitted entry, never whether. Nothing a peer originates enables anything. (ADR-0062, ADR-0065)

Read from ADR-0059 to ADR-0066; diagram A5.

Survey line

T · counts as of Genesis Checkpoint G11

How the corpus says a claim was established. The four words are kept apart, and none of them is said of the running reference.

  1. 1
    Specified

    Written in TLA+. Thirty-one modules at G11.

  2. 2
    Checked

    TLC ran mutations and witnesses at small constants. Also said of the floor's gates and of trace validation, 82 traces.

  3. 3
    Proved

    The TLA+ Proof System discharged the obligations: twenty-four proof modules, 5,416 obligations.

  4. 4
    Certified

    Every obligation of a proof module is accounted for by a route an independent checker accepted. Twenty-one of the twenty-four.

  5. 5
    Proved in Lean

    The Lean kernel discharged it, of the encoding, transcription or statement it names. Never shortened to proved.
