TOPAK, as a diagram of lines

The pattern redrawn the way a 1930s pocket railway map draws a city: straight lines, 45-degree bends, evenly spaced stations and one river. An action boards at a Thinker's seat and rides the red line. It reaches the world only if it gets across the log.

The river is the consensus log
North of it a request is decided. South of it an effect is performed. Nothing reaches the south bank without being ordered first, and a denial never crosses.
A line is one path of authority or data
A solid line is an accepted record. A line with a pale core is provisional. A broken line is Proposed, drawn as under construction.
A footnote mark is the reference's state
† not built, ‡ simulated, § partial, as the Architecture Description reports the reference implementation. The map itself draws the archetype.
